One in ten black bears is actually born with a white coat which is the result of a special gene that surfaces in a few. 实际上，每十只黑熊中就有一只出生时拥有白色皮毛，这是由于一种特殊基因在少数个体中显现的结果。⭐ be born with... = 天生具有……（B2）｜white coat = 白色皮毛（C1）｜be the result of... = 是……的结果（B2）｜surface = 显现；出现（C1，常见词特殊义）

Because of this, it has always been highly regarded by them -so much that they do not speak of seeing it to anyone else. 正因为如此，它一直受到当地人的高度尊重——甚至到了他们不会向任何外人提及自己见过它的程度。⭐ be highly regarded = 受到高度尊重/重视（C1）｜so much that... = 到了……的程度，以至于……（C1）｜speak of... = 谈及；提到（B2）

The white bear's habitat is quite interesting. 这种白熊的栖息地非常有特点。⭐ habitat = 栖息地（B2）
The bear's strong relationship with the old- growth rainforest is a complex one. 这种熊与原始森林之间的紧密关系十分复杂。⭐ old-growth rainforest = 原始雨林（C1）｜a complex relationship with... = 与……的复杂关系（C1）
The white bear relies on the huge centuries-old trees in the forest in many ways. 白熊在很多方面依赖森林中那些巨大的百年老树。⭐ rely on = 依赖（B2）｜centuries-old = 数百年的（C1）

Keeping these banks intact is important because these streams are home to salmon, which are the bear's main food source. 保持这些河岸完整非常重要，因为这些溪流是鲑鱼的栖息地，而鲑鱼是这种熊的主要食物来源。⭐ keep sth intact = 保持某物完整（C1）｜be home to... = 是……的栖息地（B2）｜food source = 食物来源（B2）
In return, the bear's feeding habits nurture the forest. 作为回报，这种熊的进食习惯滋养了森林。⭐ in return = 作为回报（B2）｜feeding habits = 进食习惯（C1）｜nurture = 养育；滋养（C1）
As the bears eat the salmon, they discard the skin and bones in great amounts on the forest floor, which provide vital nutrients. 当熊吃掉鲑鱼后，它们会将大量鱼皮和鱼骨丢弃在森林地面上，而这些东西提供了重要的营养物质。⭐ discard = 丢弃（C1）｜in great amounts = 大量地（B2）｜forest floor = 森林地表（C1）｜vital nutrients = 重要营养物质（C1）
These produce lush vegetation that sustains thousands of other types of life forms, from birds to insects and more. 这些物质会促进茂盛植被的生长，而这些植被又维持着数千种其他生命形式，从鸟类到昆虫等等。⭐ lush vegetation = 茂盛的植被（C1）｜sustain = 维持；支撑（C1）｜life forms = 生命形式（C1）

The greatest threat to the bear's existence is the loss of its habitat. 对这种熊生存最大的威胁，是其栖息地的丧失。⭐ threat to... = 对……的威胁（B2）｜loss of habitat = 栖息地丧失（C1）

And so, what's the situation going forward? 那么，未来情况会如何发展呢？⭐ going forward = 在未来；接下来（C1）
Community organizations, environmental groups and the British Columbia government are now working together on the problem. 社区组织、环保团体以及不列颠哥伦比亚政府目前正在共同解决这一问题。⭐ work together on sth = 共同处理某事（B2）｜environmental group = 环保组织（B2）
