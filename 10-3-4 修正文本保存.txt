Today, I want to talk about self-regulatory focus theory and how the actions of leaders can affect the way followers approach different situations. 今天，我想讨论一下自我调节焦点理论，以及领导者的行为如何影响追随者处理不同情境的方式。⭐ self-regulatory focus theory = 自我调节焦点理论（C1）｜the actions of leaders = 领导者的行为（B2）｜approach a situation = 应对/处理某种情况（C1）
Self-regulatory focus theory is a theory developed by Tori Higgins. 自我调节焦点理论是由 Tori Higgins 提出的一种理论。⭐ be developed by... = 由……提出/发展（B2）
He says that a person's focus at any given time is to either approach pleasure or avoid pain. 他说，一个人在任何特定时刻的关注点，要么是追求快乐，要么是避免痛苦。⭐ at any given time = 在任何特定时刻（C1）｜either A or B = 要么A要么B（B2）｜approach pleasure = 追求快乐（C1）｜avoid pain = 避免痛苦（B2）
These are two basic motivations that each and every one of us has, and they cause us to have different kinds of goals. 这是我们每个人都拥有的两种基本动机，它们会导致我们形成不同类型的目标。⭐ each and every one of us = 我们每一个人（C1）｜basic motivation = 基本动机（C1）｜cause sb to do sth = 导致某人做某事（B2）
Promotion goals in different life situations emphasise achievement. 在不同生活情境中，促进型目标强调成就。⭐ promotion goal = 促进型目标（C1）｜emphasise achievement = 强调成就（C1）
Prevention goals are oriented towards the avoidance of punishment. 预防型目标则倾向于避免惩罚。⭐ be oriented towards... = 以……为导向（C1）｜avoidance of punishment = 避免惩罚（C1）
In a specific situation, our thoughts might focus more on promotion goals or more on prevention goals. 在具体情境中，我们的想法可能更多集中于促进型目标，或者更多集中于预防型目标。⭐ focus on... = 集中于……（B2）｜specific situation = 特定情境（B2）
The theory suggests that two factors affect which goals we are focusing on.&nbsp; 该理论认为，有两个因素会影响我们关注哪一种目标。首先是一个长期因素。 ⭐ suggest that... = 表明；认为（B2）｜factor = 因素（B2）｜chronic factor = 长期因素（C1）
First, there is a chronic factor. This factor is connected to a person's personality and says that each person has a basic tendency to either focus more on promotion goals or focus more on prevention goals as part of his or her personality. 这一因素与个人性格有关，它认为，每个人都有一种基本倾向，会作为自身性格的一部分，更关注促进型目标或预防型目标。 ⭐ be connected to... = 与……相关（B2）｜basic tendency = 基本倾向（C1）｜as part of... = 作为……的一部分（B2）
Second, there is a situational factor which means that the context we are in can make us more likely to focus on one set of goals or the other. 第二个因素是情境因素，也就是说，我们所处的环境可能使我们更倾向于关注其中一种目标。⭐ situational factor = 情境因素（C1）｜the context we are in = 我们所处的环境（C1）｜make sb more likely to do sth = 使某人更可能做某事（B2）｜one set of...or the other = 两者中的一种（C1）
For example, we are more likely to be thinking about pleasure and to have promotion goals when we are spending time with a friend. 例如，当我们和朋友相处时，我们更可能想到快乐，并形成促进型目标。⭐ be more likely to do sth = 更可能做某事（B2）｜spend time with... = 与……共度时间（B2）
In contrast, if we are working on an important project for our boss, we are more likely to try to avoid making mistakes and therefore have more prevention goals in our mind. 相比之下，如果我们正在为老板负责一个重要项目，我们更可能努力避免犯错，因此脑中会有更多预防型目标。⭐ in contrast = 相比之下（B2）｜work on a project = 负责/处理一个项目（B2）｜avoid making mistakes = 避免犯错误（B2）｜therefore = 因此（B2）
Research has shown that the goals we are focusing on at a given time affect the way we think. 研究表明，我们在某一时刻关注的目标会影响我们的思考方式。⭐ research has shown that... = 研究表明（B2）｜at a given time = 在某一时刻（C1）｜the way we think = 思考方式（B2）
For example, when focusing on promotion goals, people consider their ideal self, their aspirations and gains. 例如，当关注促进型目标时，人们会考虑理想中的自己、自己的愿望以及获得的收益。⭐ ideal self = 理想自我（C1）｜aspiration = 愿望；抱负（C1）｜gain = 收益；获得的东西（B2）
They don't think about what they can lose, so they think in a happier mode. 他们不会考虑自己可能失去什么，因此会以更积极快乐的模式思考。⭐ think about what... = 思考……（B2）｜in a ... mode = 以……模式（C1）
They feel more inspired to change. 他们会更有动力去改变。⭐ feel inspired to do sth = 感到受到鼓舞去做某事（C1）
When people are focusing on prevention goals, they think about their "ought" self. 当人们关注预防型目标时，他们会想到自己的"应该自我"。⭐ ought self = 应然自我（C1）
What are they supposed to be? 他们应该成为什么样的人？⭐ be supposed to... = 应该……（B2）
What are people expecting from them? 别人对他们有什么期待？⭐ expect sth from sb = 对某人期待某事（B2）
They consider their obligations to others. 他们会考虑自己对他人的责任。⭐ obligation = 责任；义务（C1）
As a result, they experience more anxiety and try to avoid situations where they could lose. 因此，他们会体验到更多焦虑，并试图避免可能导致损失的情况。⭐ as a result = 因此（B2）｜experience anxiety = 经历焦虑（C1）｜avoid situations where... = 避免……的情况（C1）
Now that I have talked about the two focuses and how they affect people, I want to look at the idea that the way leaders behave, or their style of leading, can affect the focus that followers adopt in a specific situation. 现在我已经讨论了两种焦点以及它们如何影响人们，我想进一步看看这样一个观点：领导者的行为方式或领导风格，会影响追随者在特定情境下采取哪种焦点。⭐ now that... = 既然……；现在……之后（C1）｜look at the idea that... = 探讨……这一观点（C1）｜leadership style = 领导风格（B2）｜adopt a focus = 采取某种焦点（C1）
In talking about leadership, we often mention transformational leaders and transactional leaders. 谈到领导力时，我们经常提到变革型领导者和交易型领导者。⭐ transformational leader = 变革型领导者（C1）｜transactional leader = 交易型领导者（C1）
Transformational leaders, when interacting with their followers, focus on their development. 变革型领导者在与追随者互动时，会关注他们的发展。⭐ interact with... = 与……互动（B2）｜focus on development = 关注发展（B2）
In their words and actions transformational leaders highlight change. 在他们的语言和行动中，变革型领导者强调改变。⭐ highlight = 强调；突出（C1）
Their speech is passionate and conveys a definitive vision. 他们的讲话充满激情，并传递明确的愿景。⭐ passionate = 充满激情的（B2）｜convey a vision = 传递愿景（C1）｜definitive = 明确的；决定性的（C1）
All of these things can encourage followers to think about what could be. 所有这些因素都会鼓励追随者思考未来可能实现的事情。⭐ encourage sb to do sth = 鼓励某人做某事（B2）｜what could be = 未来可能实现的状态（C1）
In other words, they inspire a promotion focus in their followers. 换句话说，他们激发追随者采取促进型焦点。⭐ in other words = 换句话说（B2）｜inspire = 激发（B2）
In contrast, transactional leaders focus on developing clear structures that tell their followers exactly what is expected of them. 相比之下，交易型领导者关注建立明确的结构，告诉追随者具体应该做什么。⭐ develop structures = 建立结构（C1）｜exactly what is expected of them = 对他们具体的要求（C1）
While they do explain the rewards people will get for following orders, they emphasise more how a follower will be punished or that a follower won't get rewarded if his or her behaviour doesn't change. 虽然他们确实会解释服从指令可以获得的奖励，但他们更强调如果行为不改变，追随者会受到什么惩罚，或者无法获得奖励。⭐ follow orders = 服从命令（B2）｜emphasise more = 更强调（B2）｜get rewarded = 获得奖励（B2）
In short, they emphasise the consequences of making a mistake. 简而言之，他们强调犯错带来的后果。⭐ in short = 简而言之（B2）｜consequence = 后果（B2）
This emphasis will clearly lead followers to focus on avoiding punishment and problems. 这种强调会明显导致追随者关注避免惩罚和问题。⭐ lead sb to do sth = 导致某人做某事（B2）｜focus on avoiding... = 关注避免……（B2）
This is clearly a prevention focus. 这显然是一种预防型焦点。⭐ clearly = 明显地（B2）
In conclusion, it is important to understand that one focus is not necessarily better than the other one. 总而言之，重要的是理解一种焦点并不一定比另一种更好。⭐ in conclusion = 总而言之（B2）｜not necessarily = 不一定（B2）
For a designer who works in a field where a lot of innovation is needed, a promotion focus is probably better. 对于从事需要大量创新领域的设计师来说，促进型焦点可能更好。⭐ a field where... = 一个……的领域（B2）｜innovation is needed = 需要创新（B2）
In contrast, a prevention focus which causes people to work more cautiously and produce higher quality work might be very appropriate for a job like a surgeon, for example. 相比之下，预防型焦点会使人们更加谨慎工作并产生更高质量成果，因此可能非常适合外科医生这样的职业。⭐ work cautiously = 谨慎工作（C1）｜produce high-quality work = 产出高质量工作（B2）｜be appropriate for... = 适合……（B2）
The main point of the research, though, is that the actions of leaders can greatly influence whether people approach a situation with more of a promotion focus or more of a prevention focus. 不过，这项研究的核心观点是，领导者的行为能够极大影响人们在面对某种情况时，是采取更多促进型焦点还是更多预防型焦点。⭐ the main point of... = ……的核心观点（B2）｜greatly influence = 极大影响（B2）｜approach a situation with... = 以……方式面对情况（C1）
