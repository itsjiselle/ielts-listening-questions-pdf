Welcome to Manham Port, where a thousand years of history are brought to life. 欢迎来到Manham港，在这里，千年历史将生动地呈现在大家眼前。⭐️【bring something to life = 使……生动再现】
All the family can enjoy a day out at Manham: visit our copper mine, see models of the machinery it used, have your photo taken in nineteenth-century costume, experience at first hand how people lived at different stages throughout history, and especially how children studied, worked and played. 全家人都能在Manham度过愉快的一天：参观铜矿，观看矿山曾使用的机械模型，穿上十九世纪的服装拍照，亲身体验不同历史时期人们的生活，尤其是孩子们如何学习、工作和玩耍。⭐️【at first hand = 亲身地；直接地】
The port of Manham is located in beautiful and peaceful countryside, on a bend in the great River Avon, and developed here because it's the highest navigable point of the Avon - boats can go no higher up this river&nbsp; Manham港坐落在风景优美、宁静的乡间，位于Avon河的一处转弯处。港口之所以在这里发展起来，是因为这里是Avon河最靠上游的通航点——船只无法再往上游行驶， ⭐ ️【navigable = 可通航的】
and proved a handy place to load and unload cargo to and from the sea, which is over 23 miles away.&nbsp; 也方便装卸往返海上的货物，而入海口距离这里有23英里多。 ⭐ 【handy = 方便的；便利的】⭐️【cargo = 货物，尤指船运或空运货物】
A small port was already established here when, about 900 years ago, tin was discovered nearby, though it wasn't until the Industrial Revolution, when a tremendous need for metals of all kinds developed, that Manham expanded to become one of the busiest ports in the country. 大约九百年前，人们在附近发现锡矿时，这里已经有了一座小港口。不过，直到工业革命时期，各类金属的需求大幅增加，Manham才扩展成为全国最繁忙的港口之一。⭐️【tin = 锡】
And because it was already so busy, prospectors began to look for other minerals, and by the end of the nineteenth century, lead, copper, manganese and arsenic were added to the cargos leaving Manham. 由于这里的贸易已经十分繁忙，勘探者开始寻找其他矿产。到十九世纪末，从Manham运出的货物又增加了铅、铜、锰和砷。⭐️【prospector = 矿产勘探者】⭐️【lead = 铅】⭐️【manganese = 锰】⭐️【arsenic = 砷】
In the early days, the ores had been smelted - or processed - in the same area they were mined. 早期，矿石就在开采地附近进行冶炼，也就是加工处理。⭐️【ore = 矿石】⭐️【smelt = 冶炼】
But, as demand grew, the smelting process required huge factory furnaces or fires to melt the metal from the rock and there was not enough coal in the local area, so the rocks containing minerals had to be shipped Iong distances. 但随着需求增长，冶炼过程需要使用大型工业熔炉或炉火，将金属从矿石中熔炼出来，而当地又没有足够的煤炭，因此含有矿物的岩石不得不通过船只运往远方。⭐️【furnace = 熔炉；工业炉】
Sadly, in the twentieth century, the great port of Manham declined, and thousands of workers were forced to emigrate out of the area. 遗憾的是，二十世纪时，曾经繁荣的Manham港衰落了，数千名工人被迫迁离这一地区。
The building at the port fell into disrepair, and the place became almost forgotten. 港口的建筑年久失修，这个地方也几乎被人遗忘。⭐️【fall into disrepair = 失修；逐渐破败】
But then, the Manham Trust was formed to conserve the historical resources of the area. 后来，Manham信托组织成立，致力于保护这一地区的历史资源。
It organised scores of local volunteers to remove undergrowth to find the original outlines of the installations. 该组织召集了数十名当地志愿者，清除杂草灌木，找出原有设施的轮廓。⭐️【scores of = 数十个；大量的】⭐️【undergrowth = 林下灌木杂草】⭐️【installation = 大型设施；设备】
It then brought in paid professionals to match installations with maps of the original port complex and to set about reconstructing it. 随后，组织聘请专业人员，将这些设施与原港区的地图进行比对，并着手重建港区。⭐️【bring in = 请来；聘请】⭐️【set about doing = 着手做某事】
Today you can see the results of this ambitious programme of restoration. 如今，大家可以看到这项宏大修复计划取得的成果。⭐️【restoration = 修复；复原】
The intention, and we believe this will be realised before the end of the year, is to return Manham Port to the condition it reached at its peak as 'the greatest copper port in the country'. 我们的目标是让Manham港恢复到鼎盛时期的面貌，当时它被誉为“全国最大的铜贸易港”。我们相信，这个目标将在今年年底前实现。⭐️【at its peak = 在鼎盛时期】
But what can you do and see on your visit today? 那么，大家今天参观时可以体验和观看些什么呢？
Here are just a few highlights. We suggest you start with the visit to the copper mine. 下面介绍几个不容错过的项目。我们建议大家先参观铜矿。
Travel on converted mining trains and journey into the depths of the mountain along seams once worked by hundreds of miners. 大家可以乘坐改装后的矿用列车，沿着曾有数百名矿工开采过的矿层，深入山体内部。⭐️【converted = 改装的；改造的】⭐️【seam = 矿层】⭐️【work = 开采，尤指矿山或矿层】
Watch out especially for the great pumping machines which rid the mine of water. 请特别留意那些用于排出矿井积水的大型抽水设备。⭐️【rid something of = 清除某处的……】
But please be warned that, like all mines, ours is very dark and closed in and we do say that children under five and also dogs should NOT be taken into the mine. 但请注意，和所有矿井一样，我们的矿井内部十分昏暗、空间封闭，因此我们明确要求，不要带五岁以下的儿童或狗进入矿井。⭐️【closed in = 封闭狭窄的；令人感到压抑的】
The next recommended visit is to the village school. 接下来推荐大家参观村里的学校。
While looking round the classrooms, take a special look at our display of games, which is one of the largest in the world. 参观教室时，请特别看看我们的游戏展览，这是世界上规模最大的同类展览之一。
And it's recommended that you time your visit to coincide with a guided tour. 此外，建议大家安排好参观时间，赶上一场导览活动。⭐️【time something to coincide with = 安排某事的时间，使其与……同时进行】
This will give you the opportunity to ask lots of questions. 这样，大家就有机会向导游提出各种问题。
Near the school is the beautiful old sailing ketch called The George'. 学校附近停泊着一艘漂亮的古老双桅帆船，名叫“George号”。⭐️【ketch = 双桅帆船，后桅较短】
You are welcome to board the boat and look round the cabins. 欢迎大家登船参观船舱。⭐️【board = 登上船、飞机等】
Look out for the ship's wheel which was missing until only five years ago when it was dredged out of the silt by a local fisherman. 请留意船上的舵轮，它曾经遗失，直到五年前才被一名当地渔民从淤泥中打捞出来。⭐️【ship's wheel = 舵轮】⭐️【dredge out of = 从……中挖出／打捞出】⭐️【silt = 淤泥；泥沙】
We have no idea how it got there but it's been polished and proudly restored to its original place on the boat. 我们不知道它为什么会落在那里，但如今它已经被擦拭打磨干净，重新安放在船上原来的位置，这让我们十分自豪。
Please take care going down the ladders if you wish to visit the lower deck - we don't recommend you allow young children to use them. 如果大家想参观下层甲板，下梯子时请务必小心——我们不建议让年幼的孩子使用这些梯子。⭐️【lower deck = 下层甲板】
So we hope you have a memorable visit to Manham Port and will tell your friends all about us. 希望大家在Manham港度过一段难忘的时光，并把这里介绍给朋友们。
