11-3-2
标题：
Changes in Barford over the last 50 years
题目：
11
In Shona's opinion, why do fewer people use buses in Barford these days?

A.The buses are old and uncomfortable.
B.Fares have gone up too much.
C.There are not so many bus routes.
12
What change in the road network is known to have benefited the town most?

A.the construction of a bypass
B.the development of cycle paths
C.the banning of cars from certain streets
13
What is the problem affecting shopping in the town centre?

A.lack of parking spaces
B.lack of major retailers
C.lack of restaurants and cafés
14
What does Shona say about medical facilities in Barford?

A.There is no hospital.
B.New medical practices are planned.
C.The number of dentists is too low.
15
The largest number of people are employed in

A.manufacturing.
B.services.
C.education.

解析：
【11题干】【审题】关键词people， buses。 考点词画出【fewer】程度词。【定位】buses原词重现，
【11A】定位：old and conformable 原词重现
Even replacing old uncomfortable buses with ... has had little impact on passenger numbers.
【11B】定位：fare原词重现
It's sometimes said that ..., but ..., fares are not much higher than they were 50 years. ago. 
【11C】✅ 定位：most:题干fewer反义，需要定位到选项C
Most people used them frequently, but not now, because the bus companies concentrate on just the routes that attract most passengers. 
concentrate on just ... routes = are not so many 


【12题干】【审题】benefit 态度词， the most 程度词。 【定位】road network原词重现
【12A】定位：bypass原词重现
attempts ... have failed：选项否定原文
【12B】✅ 定位：cycle path原词重现
far more than was expected = benefit ... the most，确定答案
【12C】 定位：close v.关闭 = ban v.禁止
The impact of this is being measured.：选项偷换概念，影响被评估中，不代表已经确定“be known”

【13题干】【审题】关键词shopping， town center。 考点词problem态度词。【定位】shopping，town center原词重现
【13A】定位：car park = parking spaces
open new：否定原文lack 
【13B】✅ 定位：well known chain store = major retailors
few 几乎没有 = lack 缺少，确定答案
【13C】 定位：restaurants and cafes
combine ...with ...:选项lack偷换概念，combine“合并”不意味着lack“缺少”
【特殊备注】验证后发现problem属于特殊疑问词的部分，其实不用作为审题关键词。因为ABC三个选项都是lack of 某东西，都是problem，本题其实主要考察干扰项和同义替换 

【14题干】【审题】关键词medical facilities，无明显考点词。 【定位】medical facilities原词重现 
【14A】✅定位：hospital原词重现
closed ... years ago= no，解释型同义替换，确定答案
【14B】定位： medical practices原词重现
未提及plan，无中生有
【14C】定位：dentists原词重现
more ... than there used to be: 选项low否定原文

【15题干】【审题】关键词people， emplyed。 考点词 largest程度词。 【定位】employment = employ 词性类同义替换
【15A】定位： manufacturing原词重现
hasn't seen the decline：未见衰退，不代表有“最多人”从事，偷换概念
【15B】定位： services原词重现
have grown in importance：越来越重要，不代表有“最多人”从事，偷换概念
【15C】✅定位：schools and colleges = education 上下义词同义替换
main sector=largest number of people近义词同义替换，确定答案
