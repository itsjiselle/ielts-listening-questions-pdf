In public discussion of business, we take certain values for granted. 在有关商业的公开讨论中，我们往往把某些价值观视为理所当然。⭐️【take something for granted = 认为……理所当然】
Today I'm going to talk about four of them: collaboration, hard work, creativity and excellence. 今天我要讨论其中四种：协作、勤奋、创造力和卓越。
Most people would say they're all 'good things'. 大多数人都会说，它们全都是“好东西”。
I'm going to suggest that's an over-simple view. 但我想指出，这种看法过于简单。⭐️【over-simple = 过于简单化的】
The trouble with these values is that they're theoretical concepts, removed from the reality of day-to-day business. 这些价值观的问题在于，它们都是理论概念，与企业日常运营的现实相脱离。⭐️【the trouble with = ……的问题在于】⭐️【be removed from = 与……脱离】⭐️【day-to-day business = 日常业务／日常经营】
Pursue values by all means, but be prepared for what may happen as a result.They can actually cause 【damage】, which is not at all the intention. 当然可以追求这些价值观，但也要做好准备，面对由此可能产生的结果。它们实际上可能造成损害，而这完全不是人们的本意。 ⭐ ️【by all means = 当然可以；尽管去做】⭐️【be prepared for = 为……做好准备】⭐️【as a result = 因此；由此（话语功能：结果）】️【not at all = 完全不】
Business leaders generally try to do the right thing. 企业领导者通常都努力做正确的事。
But all too often the right thing backfires, if those leaders adopt values without understanding and managing the 【side effects】 that arise. 但如果这些领导者采纳某些价值观，却不了解并妥善处理随之产生的副作用，那么所谓正确的做法往往会适得其反。 ⭐ ️【all too often = 过于频繁地；往往】⭐️【backfire = 产生适得其反的结果】⭐️【side effect = 副作用】
The values can easily get in the way of what's actually intended. 这些价值观很容易反而妨碍原定目标的实现。⭐️【get in the way of = 妨碍；阻碍】
OK. So the first value I'm going to discuss is collaboration. 好的。我要讨论的第一种价值观是协作。
Er, let me give you an example. 嗯，我来举个例子。
On a management training course I once attended, we were put into groups and had to construct a bridge across a stream, using building blocks that we were given. 在我曾参加的一次管理培训课程中，我们被分成若干小组，必须使用发给我们的积木，在一条小溪上搭建一座桥。⭐️【be put into groups = 被分成小组】⭐️【building block = 积木；构建模块】
The rule was that everyone in the team had to move at least one building block during the construction. 规则是，搭建过程中每名组员都必须移动至少一块积木。
This was intended to encourage teamwork. 这一规定旨在鼓励团队合作。⭐️【be intended to do = 旨在做……】
But it was really a job best done by one person. 但这项任务其实最适合由一个人独立完成。
The other teams tried to collaborate on building the structure, and descended into confusion, with everyone getting in each other's way. 其他团队试图合作搭建，但很快陷入一片混乱，每个人都在互相妨碍。⭐️【descend into confusion = 陷入混乱】⭐️【get in each other's way = 互相妨碍】
Our team leader solved the challenge brilliantly. 我们的组长极其巧妙地解决了这个挑战。
She simply asked everyone in the team to move a piece a few centimetres, to comply with the rule, and then let the person in the team with an aptitude for puzzles like this build it alone. 她只是让每名组员把一块积木移动几厘米，以符合规定，然后让团队中擅长解决这类谜题的人独自完成搭建。⭐️【comply with = 遵守；符合】⭐️【have an aptitude for = 在……方面有天赋】
We finished before any other team. 我们比其他所有团队都更早完成。
My point is that the task wasn't really suited to teamworking, so why make it one? 我的意思是，这项任务其实并不适合团队协作，那为什么偏要把它变成团队任务呢？⭐️【my point is that = 我的意思／观点是……】⭐️【be suited to = 适合……】
Teamwork can also lead to inconsistency - a common cause of poor sales.In the case of a smartphone that a certain company launched, one director wanted to target the business market, and another demanded it was aimed at consumers. 团队合作也可能造成前后不一致，而这正是销量不佳的一个常见原因。以某家公司推出的一款智能手机为例：一名主管希望它面向企业市场，另一名主管则坚持让它面向普通消费者。 ⭐ ️【lead to inconsistency = 导致不一致】⭐️【poor sales = 销量不佳】️【in the case of = 就……而言；以……为例】⭐️【target the business market = 瞄准企业市场】⭐️【be aimed at consumers = 面向消费者】
The company wanted both directors to be involved, so gave the product a consumer-friendly name, but marketed it to companies. 公司希望两名主管都参与其中，于是给产品取了一个对普通消费者颇具吸引力的名称，却把它推销给企业。⭐️【consumer-friendly = 对消费者友好的／具有消费者吸引力的】⭐️【market something to = 向……推销某物】
The result was that it met the needs of neither group. 结果是，它未能满足任何一类顾客的需求。⭐️【meet the needs of = 满足……的需求】⭐️【neither group = 两个群体都不】
It would have been better to let one director or the other have his way, not both. 当初还不如只让其中一位主管按照自己的想法来，而不是同时迁就两个人。 ⭐ ️【have one's way = 按自己的意愿行事；如愿】
Now industriousness, or hard work. 现在来说说勤勉，也就是努力工作。 ⭐ 【industriousness = 勤勉；勤奋】
It's easy to mock people who say they work hard: after all, a hamster running around in a wheel is working hard - and getting nowhere. 人们很容易嘲笑那些声称自己努力工作的人：毕竟，仓鼠在转轮里不停奔跑也很努力，却始终原地踏步。⭐️【mock = 嘲笑】⭐️【after all = 毕竟（话语功能：补充理由）】⭐️【get nowhere = 毫无进展；没有成果】
Of course hard work is valuable, but only when properly targeted.&nbsp;&nbsp;Otherwise it wastes the 【resources】that companies value most - time and energy.&nbsp; 努力当然有价值，但前提是用对了方向。&nbsp; 否则，它只会浪费企业最重视的资源——时间和精力。 ⭐ ️【be properly targeted = 有明确且恰当的目标】&nbsp; ️【otherwise = 否则（话语功能：条件结果）】
And that's bad for the organisation. 而这对组织不利。
There's a management model that groups people according to four criteria: clever, hard-working, stupid and lazy. 有一种管理模型依据四项标准对人进行分类：聪明、勤奋、愚笨和懒惰。⭐️【group people according to = 根据……将人分类】⭐️【criteria = 标准】
Here 'lazy' means having a rational determination not to carry out unnecessary tasks. 这里的“懒惰”指的是经过理性判断后，坚决不去执行没有必要的任务。⭐️【rational determination = 理性的坚定决定】⭐️【carry out a task = 执行任务】
It doesn't mean trying to avoid work altogether. 它并不是指试图完全逃避工作。⭐️【altogether = 完全；彻底】
Most people display two of these characteristics, and the most valuable people are those who are both clever and lazy: they possess intellectual clarity, and they don't rush into making decisions. 大多数人会表现出其中两种特征，而最有价值的是既聪明又“懒惰”的人：他们思路清晰，也不会仓促作出决定。⭐️【display a characteristic = 表现出某种特征】⭐️【intellectual clarity = 思维上的清晰】⭐️【rush into doing = 仓促做……】
They come up with solutions to save the time and energy spent by the stupid and hard-working group. 他们会想出解决方案，节省那些愚笨却勤奋的人所耗费的时间和精力。⭐️【come up with a solution = 想出解决方案】
Instead of throwing more man-hours at a problem, the clever and lazy group looks for a more effective solution. 聪明而“懒惰”的人不会一味地往问题上投入更多工时，而会寻找更加有效的解决办法。⭐️【instead of = 而不是（话语功能：替代）】⭐️【throw more man-hours at a problem = 为解决问题盲目投入更多工时】⭐️【man-hour = 工时】
Next we come to creativity. 接下来我们谈谈创造力。⭐️【come to = 接下来谈到（话语功能：话题推进）】
This often works well - creating an attention-grabbing TV commercial, for example, might lead to increased sales. 创造力通常能够发挥良好作用——例如，制作一则引人注目的电视广告可能会提高销量。⭐️【attention-grabbing = 引人注目的】⭐️【TV commercial = 电视商业广告】
But it isn't always a good thing. 但它并不总是一件好事。
Some advertising campaigns are remembered for their creativity, without having any effect on sales. This happened a few years ago with the launch of a 【chocolate bar】:&nbsp; 有些广告宣传活动因创意而被人记住，却没有对销量产生任何影响。 几年前，一款巧克力棒上市时就出现了这种情况： ⭐ ️【advertising campaign = 广告宣传活动】⭐️【have an effect on = 对……产生影响】 ️【the launch of = ……的推出／上市】
subsequent research showed that plenty of consumers remembered the adverts, but had no idea what was being advertised. 后续研究表明，许多消费者记得那些广告，却完全不知道广告宣传的是什么产品。 ⭐ ⭐️【subsequent research = 后续研究】⭐️【have no idea = 完全不知道】
The trouble is that the creator derives pleasure from coming up with the idea, and wrongly assumes the audience for the campaign will share that feeling. 问题在于，创作者从构思创意的过程中获得了愉悦感，于是错误地认为广告受众也会有同样的感受。⭐️【derive pleasure from = 从……中获得愉悦】⭐️【assume = 想当然地认为；假定】
A company that brings out thousands of new products may seem more creative than a company that only has a few, but it may be too creative, and make smaller profits. 一家推出数千种新产品的公司，可能看起来比只有少量产品的公司更具创造力，但它也可能创意过度，最终利润反而更少。⭐️【bring out a product = 推出产品】⭐️【make a profit = 获得利润】
Creativity needs to be targeted, to solve a problem that the company has identified. 创造力必须具有明确的针对性，用来解决企业已经发现的问题。
Just coming up with more and more novel products isn't necessarily a good thing. 仅仅不断构思出越来越多的新颖产品，并不一定是一件好事。⭐️【novel = 新颖的；创新的】⭐️【not necessarily = 不一定（话语功能：限制判断）】
And finally, excellence. 最后要讨论的是卓越。
We all know companies that claim they 'strive for excellence', but it takes a long time to achieve excellence. 我们都知道，有些公司声称自己“追求卓越”，但真正达到卓越需要很长时间。⭐️【strive for excellence = 追求卓越】⭐️【achieve excellence = 达到卓越】
In business, being first with a product is more profitable than having the best product. 在商业领域，率先推出产品往往比拥有最出色的产品更有利可图。⭐️【be first with a product = 率先推出某种产品】
A major study of company performance compared pioneers - that is, companies bringing out the first version of a particular product – with followers, the companies that copied and improved on that product. 一项有关企业绩效的大型研究比较了市场先驱与跟随者：前者是率先推出某种产品初始版本的企业，后者则是模仿并改进该产品的企业。⭐️【company performance = 企业绩效】⭐️【pioneer = 市场先驱】⭐️【improve on = 改进；做得比……更好】
The study found that the pioneers commanded an average 【market share】 of 29 percent, while the followers achieved less than half that, only 13 percent - even though their product might have been better. 研究发现，先驱企业平均占据29%的市场份额，而跟随者获得的份额还不到其一半，只有13%——即使跟随者的产品可能更好。 ⭐ ️【command a market share of = 占据……的市场份额】⭐️【while = 而；相比之下（话语功能：对比）】⭐️【even though = 尽管（话语功能：让步）】
Insisting on excellence in everything we do is time-consuming, wastes energy and leads to losing out on opportunities. 坚持把所有事情都做到卓越会耗费大量时间、浪费精力，还会导致我们错失机会。⭐️【insist on = 坚持】⭐️【time-consuming = 耗时的】⭐️【lose out on = 错失；未能获得】
Sometimes, second-rate work is more worthwhile than excellence. 有时，二流水准的工作反而比卓越更值得去做。⭐️【second-rate = 二流的；水平一般的】⭐️【worthwhile = 值得投入时间和精力的】
'Make sure it's excellent' sounds like a good approach to business, but the 'just-get-started' approach is likely to be more successful. “务必做到卓越”听起来是一种很好的经营理念，但“先行动起来再说”的做法可能更容易取得成功。⭐️【approach to business = 经营理念／商业方法】⭐️【just-get-started approach = 先行动起来的做法】
