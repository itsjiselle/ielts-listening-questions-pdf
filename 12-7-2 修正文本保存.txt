In terms of age groups, the over-65s are an important market, and one that's increasing steadily year on year. 从年龄群体来看，65岁以上人群是一个重要的市场，而且规模正在逐年稳步增长。⭐️【year on year = 逐年；与上年相比】

As far as the 43 to 54-year-olds are concerned , bookings there are steady, but I have to say we haven't seen the increase we expected. 至于43至54岁人群，预订量保持稳定，但不得不说，我们并没有看到预期中的增长。
