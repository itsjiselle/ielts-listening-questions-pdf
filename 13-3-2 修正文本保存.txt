So if you're one of those people who hasn't found the perfect physical activity yet - here are some things to think about which might help you make the right decision for you. 所以，如果你还没有找到最适合自己的运动，下面这些需要考虑的因素，或许能帮助你作出适合自己的选择。
The first question to ask yourself is whether you would enjoy training in a gym. 首先要问问自己，你是否会喜欢在健身房锻炼。
Many people are put off by the idea of having to fit a visit to the gym into their busy day - you often have to go very early or late as some gyms can get very crowded. 很多人一想到要在忙碌的一天中挤出时间去健身房，就打了退堂鼓——因为有些健身房会非常拥挤，你往往得很早或很晚才能去。⭐️【be put off by = 因……而失去兴趣；被……劝退】⭐️【fit something into = 挤出时间安排某事】
But with regular training you'll see a big difference in a relatively short space of time. 不过，只要坚持规律训练，你就能在相对较短的时间内看到明显变化。⭐️【in a short space of time = 在短时间内】
Running has become incredibly popular in recent years. 近年来，跑步变得极其流行。
That's probably got a lot to do with the fact that it's a very accessible form of exercise - anyone can run - even if you can only run a few metres to begin with.&nbsp; 这很可能主要是因为跑步是一项门槛很低的运动——任何人都能跑，哪怕一开始只能跑几米。 ⭐ ️【accessible = 容易参与的；门槛低的】
But make sure you get the right shoes - it's worth investing in a high quality pair and they don't come cheap. 不过，一定要选择合适的鞋子——买一双高品质的跑鞋很值得，而这样的鞋通常价格不菲。 ⭐ 【not come cheap = 价格不菲】
Another great thing about running is that you can do it at any time of day or night - the only thing that may stop you is snow and ice. 跑步的另一个好处是，无论白天还是夜晚，你随时都可以跑——唯一可能阻止你的就是冰雪天气。
Swimming is another really good way to build fitness. 游泳也是增强体能的一种好方法。⭐️【build fitness = 增强体能】
What attracts many people is that you can swim in an indoor pool at any time of year. 吸引很多人的一点是，一年四季都可以在室内泳池游泳。
On the other hand, it can be quite boring or solitary - it's hard to chat to people while you're swimming lengths. 另一方面，游泳可能相当单调，或者让人感到孤独——在泳池里来回游时，很难和别人聊天。⭐️【solitary = 独自进行的；缺少他人陪伴的】⭐️【swim lengths = 在泳池中沿池长来回游】⭐️【on the other hand = 另一方面（话语功能：转向不利的一面）】
Cycling has become almost as popular as running in recent years. 近年来，骑行已经变得几乎和跑步一样流行。
That's probably because as well as improving their fitness, many people say being out in the fresh air in a park or in the countryside can be fun, provided the conditions are right, of course - only fanatics go out in the wind and rain! 这可能是因为，除了增强体能，很多人还觉得在公园或乡间呼吸新鲜空气很有乐趣。当然，前提是天气条件合适——只有狂热爱好者才会冒着风雨出门骑行！⭐️【provided = 只要；前提是（话语功能：提出条件）】⭐️【fanatic = 狂热爱好者】
Yoga is a good choice for those of you looking for exercise which focuses on developing both a healthy mind and body. 如果你希望找到一种兼顾身心健康的运动，瑜伽是个不错的选择。
It's a good way of building strength and with the right instructor, there's less chance of hurting yourself than with other more active sports. 瑜伽能有效增强力量，而且在合适的教练指导下，与其他运动量更大的运动相比，受伤的可能性更小。
But don't expect to find it easy - it can be surprisingly challenging, especially for people who aren't very flexible. 但别以为瑜伽很容易——它的难度可能超出你的预期，尤其是对身体柔韧性不太好的人来说。⭐️【flexible = 身体柔韧的】
Getting a personal trainer is a good way to start your fitness programme. 聘请私人教练是开始健身计划的一种好方法。
Obviously there can be significant costs involved. 显然，这可能涉及相当高的费用。
But if you've got someone there to encourage you and help you achieve your goals, you're less likely to give up. 不过，如果有人在身边鼓励你、帮助你实现目标，你就不那么容易放弃。
Make sure you get someone with a recognised qualification, though, or you could do yourself permanent damage. 不过，一定要找具备受认可资质的教练，否则你可能会给自己造成永久性损伤。⭐️【recognised qualification = 受认可的资质】
Whatever you do, don't join a gym unless you're sure you'll make good use of it. 无论如何，除非你确定自己会充分利用健身房，否则不要办会员。⭐️【whatever you do = 无论如何（话语功能：强调提醒）】
So many people waste lots of money by signing up for membership and then hardly ever go. 很多人花钱办了会员，之后却几乎不去，白白浪费了大笔钱。
What happens to their good intentions? 他们最初那些美好的打算，后来都怎么了？
I don't think people suddenly stop caring about improving their fitness, or decide they have more important things to do. 我认为，人们并不是突然不再重视增强体能，也不是认定自己有更重要的事情要做。
I think people lose interest when they don't think they're making enough progress. 我觉得，当人们认为自己的进步不够明显时，就会失去兴趣。
That's when they give up hope and stop believing they'll ever achieve their goals. 这时，他们便会失去希望，不再相信自己能够实现目标。
Also, what people sometimes don't realise when they start is that it takes a lot of determination and hard work to keep training week after week and lots of people don't have that kind of commitment. 此外，人们开始时有时并没有意识到，要一周又一周地坚持训练，需要很大的决心和努力，而很多人并没有那样的投入和毅力。⭐️【week after week = 一周又一周地；连续数周】⭐️【commitment = 持续投入；坚持到底的决心】
One thing you can do to help yourself is to set manageable goals - be realistic and don't push yourself too far. 有一个方法可以帮助你坚持下去，那就是设定力所能及的目标——要切合实际，不要过度勉强自己。⭐️【manageable goals = 力所能及的目标】⭐️【push yourself too far = 过度勉强自己】
Some people advise writing goals down, but I think it's better to have a flexible approach. 有人建议把目标写下来，但我认为采取灵活的方式更好。
Give yourself a really nice treat every time you reach one of your goals. And don't get too upset if you experience setbacks - it's a journey - there are bound to be difficulties along the way. 每实现一个目标，就好好奖励自己。如果遇到挫折，也不要太沮丧——这是一个循序渐进的过程，途中难免会遇到困难。⭐️【give yourself a treat = 犒劳自己；奖励自己】⭐️【setback = 挫折；阻碍进展的事情】⭐️【be bound to = 必然会；难免会】
