Producing enough energy to meet our needs has become a serious problem. 生产足够的能源来满足我们的需求，已经成为一个严峻的问题。⭐️【meet our needs = 满足我们的需求】
Demand is rising rapidly, because of the world's increasing population and expanding industry. 由于世界人口不断增长、工业持续扩张，能源需求正在迅速上升。
Burning fossil fuels, like gas, coal and oil, seriously damages the environment and they'll eventually run out. 燃烧天然气、煤炭和石油等化石燃料会严重破坏环境，而且这些燃料最终会耗尽。⭐️【fossil fuels = 化石燃料】⭐️【run out = 耗尽；用完】
For a number of years now, scientists have been working out how we can derive energy from renewable sources, such as the sun and wind, without causing pollution. 多年来，科学家一直在研究如何在不造成污染的情况下，从太阳和风等可再生能源中获取能量。⭐️【work out = 研究出；弄清楚】⭐️【derive energy from = 从……中获取能量】
Today I'll outline marine renewable energy - also called ocean energy - which harnesses the movement of the oceans. 今天，我将概述海洋可再生能源，也称海洋能，这种能源利用海洋的运动来获取能量。⭐️【outline = 概述】⭐️【harness = 利用、开发自然力量】
Marine renewable energy can be divided into three main categories: wave energy, tidal energy and ocean thermal energy conversion, and I'll say a few words about each one. 海洋可再生能源可以分为三大类：波浪能、潮汐能和海洋温差能转换，我会分别简单介绍一下。⭐️【ocean thermal energy conversion = 海洋温差能转换】
First, wave energy. 首先是波浪能。
Numerous devices have been invented to harvest wave energy, with names such as Wave Dragon, the Penguin and Mighty Whale, and research is going on to try and come up with a really efficient method. 人们已经发明了许多收集波浪能的装置，名称包括“波浪龙”“企鹅”和“巨鲸”等，目前仍在开展研究，试图找到一种真正高效的方法。⭐️【harvest wave energy = 收集、获取波浪能】⭐️【come up with = 想出；提出】
This form of energy has plenty of potential, as the source is constant, and there's no danger of waves coming to a standstill. 这种能源具有很大的潜力，因为其来源持续不断，海浪也不会有完全停止的风险。⭐️【come to a standstill = 完全停止；陷入停滞】
Electricity can be generated using onshore systems, using a reservoir, or offshore systems. 可以利用配有蓄水池的岸上系统，也可以利用海上系统来发电。⭐️【onshore systems = 岸上系统】⭐️【offshore systems = 海上系统】
But the problem with ocean waves is that they're erratic, with the wind making them travel in every direction. 但海浪的问题在于它们变化无常，风会使它们向各个方向运动。⭐️【erratic = 不规则的；变化无常的】
This adds to the difficulty of creating efficient technology: ideally all the waves would travel smoothly and regularly along the same straight line. 这增加了开发高效技术的难度：理想情况下，所有海浪都应沿着同一条直线平稳而有规律地运动。⭐️【add to the difficulty of = 增加……的难度】
Another drawback is that sand and other sediment on the ocean floor might be stopped from flowing normally, which can lead to environmental problems. 另一个缺点是，海底的沙子和其他沉积物可能无法正常流动，从而引发环境问题。⭐️【drawback = 缺点；不利因素】⭐️【sediment = 沉积物】
The second category of marine energy that I'll mention is tidal energy. 我要介绍的第二类海洋能是潮汐能。
One major advantage of using the tide, rather than waves, as a source of energy is that it's predictable: we know the exact times of high and low tides for years to come. 与利用波浪相比，利用潮汐作为能源的一大优势在于它是可以预测的：我们能够知道未来多年涨潮和落潮的确切时间。⭐️【for years to come = 在未来许多年里】
For tidal energy to be effective, the difference between high and low tides needs to be at least five metres, and this occurs naturally in only about forty places on Earth. 要有效利用潮汐能，高潮与低潮之间的水位差至少需要达到五米，而地球上天然具备这一条件的地方只有大约四十处。
But the right conditions can be created by constructing a tidal lagoon, an area of sea water separated from the sea. 不过，可以通过修建潮汐潟湖来创造合适的条件，也就是将一片海水与外海隔开的水域。⭐️【tidal lagoon = 潮汐潟湖】
One current plan is to create a tidal lagoon on the coast of Wales. 目前有一项计划是在威尔士海岸修建一座潮汐潟湖。
This will be an area of water within a bay at Swansea, sheltered by a U-shaped breakwater, or dam, built out from the coast. 这片水域将位于斯旺西的一处海湾内，由一道从海岸向外延伸的U形防波堤，也就是堤坝，围护起来。⭐️【breakwater = 防波堤】⭐️【built out from the coast = 从海岸向外延伸修建的】
The breakwater will contain sixteen hydro turbines, and as the tide rises, water rushes through the breakwater, activating the turbines, which turn a generator to produce electricity. 防波堤内将安装十六台水轮机。涨潮时，海水会迅速穿过防波堤，启动水轮机，再由水轮机带动发电机发电。⭐️【hydro turbines = 水轮机】⭐️【turn a generator = 带动发电机旋转】
Then, for three hours as the tide goes out, the water is held back within the break water, increasing the difference in water level, until it's several metres higher within the lagoon than in the open sea. 随后，在退潮过程中的三个小时里，海水会被拦蓄在防波堤内，使内外水位差不断扩大，直到潟湖内的水位比外海高出数米。⭐️【the tide goes out = 退潮】⭐️【hold back = 阻挡；拦蓄】⭐️【the open sea = 外海】
Then, in order to release the stored water, gates in the breakwater are opened. 然后，为了释放蓄积的海水，防波堤上的闸门会被打开。
It pours powerfully out of the lagoon, driving the turbines in the breakwater in the opposite direction and again generating thousands of megawatts of electricity. 海水会从潟湖中猛烈涌出，推动防波堤内的水轮机反向旋转，再次产生数千兆瓦的电力。⭐️【pour out of = 从……大量涌出】⭐️【in the opposite direction = 朝相反方向】
As there are two high tides a day, this lagoon scheme would generate electricity four times a day, every day, for a total of around 14 hours in every 24 - and enough electricity for over 150,000 homes. 由于每天有两次高潮，这个潟湖项目每天都能发电四次，每二十四小时累计发电约十四小时，产生的电力足以供超过十五万户家庭使用。
This system has quite a lot in its favour: unlike solar and wind energy it doesn't depend on the weather; the turbines are operated without the need for fuel, so it doesn't create any greenhouse gas emissions; and very little maintenance is needed. 这套系统有不少优势：与太阳能和风能不同，它不依赖天气；水轮机无需燃料即可运行，因此不会产生任何温室气体排放；而且所需的维护很少。⭐️【have a lot in its favour = 具备许多有利条件】⭐️【greenhouse gas emissions = 温室气体排放】
It's estimated that electricity generated in this way will be relatively cheap, and that manufacturing the components would create more than 2,000 jobs, a big boost to the local economy. 据估计，以这种方式产生的电力将比较便宜，而且制造相关零部件将创造两千多个就业岗位，大大推动当地经济发展。⭐️【a big boost to the local economy = 对当地经济的有力推动】
On the other hand, there are fears that lagoons might harm both fish and birds, for example by disturbing migration patterns, and causing a build-up of silt, affecting local ecosystems. 另一方面，人们担心潟湖可能会伤害鱼类和鸟类，例如扰乱它们的迁徙规律，以及导致淤泥堆积，从而影响当地生态系统。⭐️【on the other hand = 另一方面，引出相反的考量】⭐️【migration patterns = 迁徙规律】⭐️【a build-up of silt = 淤泥的堆积】
There are other forms of tidal energy, but I'll go on to the third category of marine energy: ocean thermal energy conversion. 潮汐能还有其他形式，不过接下来我要介绍第三类海洋能：海洋温差能转换。⭐️【go on to = 接着转向、讨论】
This depends on there being a big difference in temperature between surface water and the water a couple of kilometres below the surface, and this occurs in tropical coastal areas. 这种方式需要表层海水与海面以下约两公里深处的海水之间存在较大的温差，而热带沿海地区具备这一条件。
The idea is to bring cold water up to the surface using a submerged pipe. 其构想是利用一根水下管道，将冷水输送到海面。⭐️【a submerged pipe = 一根浸没在水下的管道】
