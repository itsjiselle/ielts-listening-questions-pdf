In this series of lectures about the history of weather forecasting, I'll start by examining its early history - that'll be the subject of today's talk. 在这个关于天气预报历史的系列讲座中，我将首先探讨它的早期历史——这就是今天讲座的主题。
OK, so we'll start by going back thousands of years. 好，那么我们先回到几千年前。
Most ancient cultures had weather gods, and weather catastrophes, such as floods, played an important role in many creation myths. 大多数古代文化都有掌管天气的神灵，而洪水等气象灾害在许多创世神话中占据着重要地位。⭐️【weather catastrophes = 气象灾害】⭐️【creation myths = 创世神话】
Generally, weather was attributed to the whims of the gods, as the wide range of weather gods in various cultures shows. 一般来说，人们将天气归因于神灵变幻莫测的意愿，各种文化中形形色色的天气神便体现了这一点。⭐️【be attributed to = 被归因于】⭐️【the whims of = ……一时的念头；……反复无常的意愿】
For instance, there's the Egyptian sun god Ra, and Thor, the Norse god of thunder and lightning. 例如，有埃及的太阳神拉，以及北欧神话中掌管雷电的神托尔。⭐️【Norse = 古代北欧的；北欧神话中的】
Many ancient civilisations developed rites such as dances in order to make the weather gods look kindly on them. 许多古代文明形成了舞蹈等仪式，以祈求天气神的眷顾。⭐️【rites = 宗教仪式；典礼】⭐️【look kindly on = 对……表示善意；眷顾】
But the weather was of daily importance: observing the skies and drawing the correct conclusions from these observations was really important, in fact their survival depended on it. 不过，天气也与日常生活息息相关：观察天空，并根据观察得出正确结论，确实非常重要，实际上他们的生存就依赖于此。⭐️【draw conclusions from = 根据……得出结论】
It isn't known when people first started to observe the skies, but at around 650 BC, the Babylonians produced the first short-range weather forecasts, based on their observations of clouds and other phenomena. 人们最早何时开始观察天空，现已无从得知，但大约在公元前650年，巴比伦人根据对云和其他现象的观察，做出了最早的短期天气预报。⭐️【short-range weather forecasts = 短期天气预报】
The Chinese also recognised weather patterns, and by 300 BC, astronomers had developed a calendar which divided the year into 24 festivals, each associated with a different weather phenomenon. 中国人也认识到了天气的规律，到了公元前300年，天文学家已经制定出一种历法，将一年划分为二十四个节气，每个节气都与不同的天气现象有关。⭐️【weather patterns = 天气规律；天气变化模式】⭐️【be associated with = 与……相关联】
The ancient Greeks were the first to develop a more scientific approach to explaining the weather. 古希腊人最早发展出一种更科学的方式来解释天气。⭐️【an approach to explaining = 一种解释……的方法】
The work of the philosopher and scientist Aristotle, in the fourth century BC, is especially noteworthy, as his ideas held sway for nearly 2,000 years. 公元前四世纪，哲学家兼科学家亚里士多德的研究尤其值得关注，因为他的思想在此后近两千年间一直占据主导地位。⭐️【noteworthy = 值得注意的；显著的】⭐️【hold sway = 占据主导地位；具有支配性影响】
In 340 BC, he wrote a book in which he attempted to account for the formation of rain, clouds, wind and storms. 公元前340年，他写了一本书，试图解释雨、云、风和暴风雨的形成。⭐️【account for = 解释……的原因或成因】
He also described celestial phenomena such as haloes - that is, bright circles of light around the sun, the moon and bright stars - and comets. 他还描述了晕和彗星等天象；所谓“晕”，就是太阳、月亮和明亮恒星周围出现的明亮光圈。⭐️【celestial phenomena = 天象】⭐️【haloes = 晕；光晕】
Many of his observations were surprisingly accurate. 他的许多观察结果准确得令人惊讶。
For example, he believed that heat could cause water to evaporate. 例如，他认为热量能够使水蒸发。⭐️【evaporate = 蒸发】
But he also jumped to quite a few wrong conclusions, such as that winds are breathed out by the Earth. 但他也草率地得出了不少错误结论，例如认为风是地球呼出来的气。⭐️【jump to conclusions = 未充分考虑便下结论；草率下结论】
Errors like this were rectified from the Renaissance onwards. 从文艺复兴时期起，这类错误逐渐得到了纠正。⭐️【rectify = 纠正；改正】⭐️【from … onwards = 从……起，此后一直】
For nearly 2,000 years, Aristotle's work was accepted as the chief authority on weather theory. 在近两千年的时间里，亚里士多德的著作一直被公认为气象理论方面最权威的依据。⭐️【the chief authority on = 关于……最权威的依据或专家】
Alongside this, though, in the Middle Ages weather observations were passed on in the form of proverbs, such as 'Red sky at night, shepherd's delight; red sky in the morning, shepherd's warning'. 不过，与此同时，在中世纪，人们也通过谚语来传承天气观察经验，例如：“晚霞红，牧人喜；朝霞红，牧人警。”⭐️【pass on = 传递；传承】⭐️【in the form of = 以……的形式】
Many of these are based on very good observations and are accurate as contemporary meteorologists have discovered. 当代气象学家发现，其中许多谚语建立在十分可靠的观察之上，而且是准确的。⭐️【contemporary meteorologists = 当代气象学家】
For centuries, any attempt to forecast the weather could only be based on personal observations, but in the fifteenth century scientists began to see the need for instruments. 几个世纪以来，任何预测天气的尝试都只能以个人观察为依据，但到了十五世纪，科学家开始意识到使用仪器的必要性。
Until then, the only ones available were weather vanes - to determine the wind direction and early versions of rain gauges. 在此之前，可用的仪器只有用来确定风向的风向标，以及早期的雨量计。⭐️【weather vanes = 风向标】⭐️【rain gauges = 雨量计】
One of the first, invented in the fifteenth century, was a hygrometer, which measured humidity. 最早出现的仪器之一是十五世纪发明的湿度计，用于测量湿度。⭐️【hygrometer = 湿度计】⭐️【humidity = 湿度】
This was one of many inventions that contributed to the development of weather forecasting. 这是推动天气预报发展的众多发明之一。⭐️【contribute to = 促成；推动】
In 1592, the Italian scientist and inventor Galileo developed the world's first thermometer.&nbsp; 1592年，意大利科学家兼发明家伽利略研制出了世界上第一支温度计。
His student Torricelli later invented the barometer, which allowed people to measure atmospheric pressure. 他的学生托里拆利后来发明了气压计，使人们能够测量大气压。⭐️【barometer = 气压计】⭐️【atmospheric pressure = 大气压】
In 1648, the French philosopher Pascal proved that pressure decreases with altitude.&nbsp; 1648年，法国哲学家帕斯卡证明，气压会随着海拔升高而降低。 ⭐ ️【decrease with altitude = 随海拔升高而降低】
This discovery was verified by English astronomer Halley in 1686; and Halley was also the first person to map trade winds.&nbsp; 1686年，英国天文学家哈雷验证了这一发现；哈雷也是第一个绘制信风分布图的人。 ⭐ ️【verify = 核实；验证】⭐️【map = 绘制……的分布图】⭐️【trade winds = 信风】
This increasing ability to measure factors related to weather helped scientists to understand the atmosphere and its processes better, and they started collecting weather observation data systematically. 随着测量天气相关因素的能力不断增强，科学家得以更深入地了解大气及其变化过程，并开始系统地收集气象观测数据。⭐️【systematically = 系统地；有条理地】
In the eighteenth century, the scientist and politician Benjamin Franklin carried out work on electricity and lightning in particular, but he was also very interested in weather and studied it throughout most of his life. 十八世纪，科学家兼政治家本杰明·富兰克林开展了有关电的研究，尤其是对闪电的研究；不过，他对天气也十分感兴趣，一生中的大部分时间都在研究天气。⭐️【carry out work on = 开展有关……的研究】⭐️【in particular = 尤其；特别是】
It was Franklin who discovered that storms generally travel from west to east. 正是富兰克林发现，暴风雨通常由西向东移动。
In addition to new meteorological instruments, other developments contributed to our understanding of the atmosphere. 除了新型气象仪器，其他方面的发展也增进了我们对大气的认识。⭐️【meteorological instruments = 气象仪器】
People in different locations began to keep records, and in the mid-nineteenth century, the invention of the telegraph made it possible for these records to be collated. 不同地区的人们开始记录天气，而十九世纪中叶电报的发明，使这些记录得以汇集整理。⭐️【keep records = 保存记录；持续记录】⭐️【collate = 汇集并整理】
This led, by the end of the nineteenth century, to the first weather services. 这促使最早的气象服务在十九世纪末出现。
It was not until the early twentieth century that mathematics and physics became part of meteorology, and we'll continue from that point next week. 直到二十世纪初，数学和物理学才被纳入气象学，下周我们将从这里继续讲起。⭐️【It was not until … that … = 直到……才……，强调某事发生得较晚】
