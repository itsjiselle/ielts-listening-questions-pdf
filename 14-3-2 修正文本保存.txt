Volunteers are teenagers and adults who choose to spend some time, unpaid, helping other people in some way. 志愿者是选择抽出时间、无偿以某种方式帮助他人的青少年和成年人。
Most volunteers devote two or three hours to this every week, while a few do much more. 大多数志愿者每周投入两三个小时，而少数人投入的时间要多得多。⭐️【devote time to = 把时间投入……】⭐️【while = 而（话语功能：对比）】
The people they help may have physical or behavioural difficulties, for example. 例如，他们帮助的对象可能存在身体或行为方面的障碍。⭐️【behavioural difficulties = 行为障碍／行为方面的困难】
Volunteers can do all sorts of things, depending on their own abilities and interests. 志愿者可以根据自身能力和兴趣，从事各种各样的工作。
If they're supporting a family that's struggling, for example, they may be able to give them tips on cooking, or recommend how to plan their budget or how to shop sensibly on their income. 例如，如果他们正在帮助一个生活困难的家庭，便可以提供烹饪建议，或者指导他们如何规划预算、根据收入理性购物。⭐️【a family that’s struggling = 生活困难的家庭】⭐️【plan a budget = 规划预算】⭐️【live/shop on an income = 依靠一定收入生活／购物】
They might even do some painting or wallpapering, perhaps alongside any members of the family who are able to do it. 他们甚至可能帮忙刷墙或贴壁纸，也许还会与有能力参与的家庭成员一起完成。⭐️【wallpapering = 贴壁纸】⭐️【alongside = 与……一起】

The benefit from volunteering isn't only for the people being helped. 志愿服务并非只有接受帮助的人才能从中受益。
Volunteers also gain from it: they're using their skills to cope with somebody's mental or physical ill health, and volunteering may be a valuable element of their CV when they're applying for jobs: employers usually look favourably on someone who's given up time to help others. 志愿者同样能够从中获益：他们会运用自己的能力来帮助应对他人的心理或身体疾病；而在求职时，志愿服务也可能成为简历中一项很有价值的经历，因为雇主通常会对愿意牺牲时间帮助他人的人抱有好感。⭐️【gain from = 从……中受益】⭐️【cope with = 应对】⭐️【a valuable element of a CV = 简历中有价值的一项经历】⭐️【look favourably on = 对……抱有好感／给予积极评价】⭐️【give up time to do = 抽出／牺牲时间做……】
