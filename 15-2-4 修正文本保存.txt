I'm going to report on a case study of a programme which has been set up to help rural populations in Mozambique, a largely agricultural country in South-East Africa. 我要汇报一个项目的案例研究，该项目旨在帮助莫桑比克的农村人口。莫桑比克位于非洲东南部，是一个以农业为主的国家。⭐️【a case study = 案例研究】⭐️【rural populations = 农村人口】
The programme worked with three communities in Chicualacuala district, near the Limpopo River. 该项目与林波波河附近、Chicualacuala地区的三个社区开展了合作。
This is a dry and arid region, with unpredictable rainfall. 这是一个十分干旱的地区，降雨情况难以预测。⭐️【arid = 干旱的；缺少雨水的】
Because of this, people in the area were unable to support themselves through agriculture and instead they used the forest as a means of providing themselves with an income, mainly by selling charcoal. 因此，当地人无法依靠农业维持生计，转而利用森林获取收入，主要是出售木炭。⭐️【support themselves = 维持自己的生计】⭐️【as a means of = 作为……的手段】⭐️【charcoal = 木炭】
However, this was not a sustainable way of living in the long term, as they were rapidly using up this resource. 然而，从长远来看，这并不是一种可持续的生活方式，因为他们正在迅速耗尽这一资源。⭐️【in the long term = 从长远来看】⭐️【use up = 用尽；耗尽】
To support agriculture in this dry region, the programme focused primarily on making use of existing water resources from the Limpopo River by setting up systems of irrigation, which would provide a dependable water supply for crops and animals. 为了支持这个干旱地区的农业发展，该项目主要着眼于利用林波波河现有的水资源，通过建立灌溉系统，为农作物和牲畜提供可靠的供水。⭐️【systems of irrigation = 灌溉系统】⭐️【a dependable water supply = 可靠的供水】
The programme worked closely with the district government in order to find the best way of implementing this. 项目方与地区政府密切合作，以找到实施这一方案的最佳方式。⭐️【work closely with = 与……密切合作】⭐️【implement = 实施；执行】
The region already had one farmers' association, and it was decided to set up two more of these. 该地区原本已有一个农民协会，后来决定再成立两个。⭐️【farmers' association = 农民协会】
These associations planned and carried out activities including water management, livestock breeding and agriculture, and it was notable that in general, women formed the majority of the workforce. 这些协会规划并开展了水资源管理、牲畜养殖和农业种植等活动，值得注意的是，总体而言，女性构成了劳动力的主体。⭐️【livestock breeding = 牲畜养殖】⭐️【form the majority of = 构成……的大多数】⭐️【workforce = 劳动力；全体工作人员】
It was decided that in order to keep the crops safe from animals, both wild and domestic, special areas should be fenced off where the crops could be grown. 为了保护农作物免遭野生动物和家养动物破坏，项目决定用围栏隔出专门的区域来种植作物。⭐️【domestic = 家养的；驯养的】⭐️【fence off = 用围栏隔开】
The community was responsible for creating these fences, but the programme provided the necessary wire for making them. 社区负责搭建这些围栏，而项目方提供制作围栏所需的铁丝。
Once the area had been fenced off, it could be cultivated. 土地围起来之后，就可以进行耕种了。⭐️【cultivate = 耕种；开垦】
The land was dug, so that vegetables and cereals appropriate to the climate could be grown, and the programme provided the necessary seeds for this. 人们翻整土地，以便种植适合当地气候的蔬菜和谷物，项目方则提供所需的种子。⭐️【cereals = 谷物】⭐️【appropriate to the climate = 适合当地气候的】
The programme also provided pumps so that water could be brought from the river in pipes to the fields. 项目方还提供了水泵，以便通过管道将河水输送到田地里。
However, the labour was all provided by local people, and they also provided and put up the posts that supported the fences around the fields. 不过，所有劳动都由当地人承担，他们还提供并竖起了支撑农田周围围栏的立柱。⭐️【put up = 竖起；搭建】⭐️【posts = 柱子；桩】
Once the programme had been set up, its development was monitored carefully. 项目建立起来后，其进展受到了仔细监测。⭐️【monitor = 监测；持续跟踪】
The farmers were able to grow enough produce not just for their own needs, but also to sell. 农民种出的农产品不仅足以满足自身需求，还有剩余可以出售。⭐️【produce = 农产品，作不可数名词】
However, getting the produce to places where it could be marketed was sometimes a problem, as the farmers did not have access to transport, and this resulted in large amounts of produce, especially vegetables, being spoiled. 然而，有时将农产品运到能够销售的地方却成了问题，因为农民缺乏可用的运输工具，这导致大量农产品，尤其是蔬菜，腐坏变质。⭐️【market = 推销；销售】⭐️【have access to transport = 有运输工具可用】⭐️【be spoiled = 腐坏；变质】
This problem was discussed with the farmers' associations and it was decided that in order to prevent food from being spoiled, the farmers needed to learn techniques for its preservation. 项目方与农民协会讨论了这个问题，并决定让农民学习食品保存技术，以防止食物变质。⭐️【preservation = 保存；保鲜】
There was also an additional initiative that had not been originally planned, but which became a central feature of the programme. 此外，还有一项最初并未规划的举措，后来却成了该项目的核心内容。⭐️【initiative = 新举措；行动方案】⭐️【a central feature = 核心内容；主要特色】
This was when farmers started to dig holes for tanks in the fenced-off areas and to fill these with water and use them for breeding fish –an important source of protein. 农民开始在围起来的区域内挖坑建水池，再往池中注水，用来养鱼——鱼是蛋白质的重要来源。⭐️【breed fish = 养鱼】
After a time, another suggestion was made by local people which hadn't been part of the programme's original proposal, but which was also adopted later on. 一段时间后，当地人又提出了一项建议，虽然它并不属于项目最初的方案，但后来也被采纳了。⭐️【original proposal = 最初的提案；原始方案】⭐️【adopt a suggestion = 采纳建议】
They decided to try setting up colonies of bees, which would provide honey both for their own consumption and to sell. 他们决定尝试建立蜂群，生产蜂蜜，既供自己食用，也用于出售。⭐️【colonies of bees = 蜂群】⭐️【for their own consumption = 供他们自己食用或使用】
So what lessons can be learned from this programme? 那么，我们能从这个项目中吸取哪些经验呢？
First of all, it tells us that in dry, arid regions, if there is access to a reliable source of water, there is great potential for the development of agriculture. 首先，它告诉我们，在干旱地区，只要能够获得可靠的水源，农业就具有很大的发展潜力。⭐️【great potential for = ……的巨大潜力】
In Chicualacuala, there was a marked improvement in agricultural production, which improved food security and benefited local people by providing them with both food and income. 在Chicualacuala，农业生产显著改善，提高了粮食保障水平，并通过提供食物和收入使当地人受益。⭐️【a marked improvement = 显著改善】⭐️【food security = 粮食安全；稳定获得足够食物的保障】
However, it's important to set realistic timelines for each phase of the programme, especially for its design, as mistakes made at this stage may be hard to correct later on. 然而，为项目的每个阶段制定切实可行的时间安排十分重要，尤其是设计阶段，因为这一阶段出现的错误，日后可能难以纠正。⭐️【set realistic timelines = 制定切实可行的时间安排】⭐️【each phase = 每个阶段】
The programme demonstrates that sustainable development is possible in areas where … 该项目表明，在那些……的地区，可持续发展是可以实现的。⭐️【demonstrate = 表明；证明】
