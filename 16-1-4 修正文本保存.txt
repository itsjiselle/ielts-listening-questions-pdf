Ancient philosophy is not just about talking or lecturing, or even reading long, dense books. 古代哲学不只是交谈、讲授，甚至也不只是阅读篇幅冗长、内容艰深的书籍。⭐️【dense = 内容艰深的；信息密集而难懂的】
In fact, it is something people have used throughout history – to solve their problems and to achieve their greatest triumphs. 事实上，纵观历史，人们一直运用哲学来解决问题，取得人生中最辉煌的成就。
Specifically, I am referring to Stoicism, which, in my opinion, is the most practical of all philosophies and therefore the most appealing. 具体而言，我指的是斯多葛哲学。在我看来，它是所有哲学中最实用的，因此也最具吸引力。⭐️【Stoicism = 斯多葛哲学；斯多葛主义】
Stoicism was founded in Ancient Greece by Zeno of Citium in the early 3rd century BC, but was practised by the likes of Epictetus, Cato, Seneca and Marcus Aurelius. 斯多葛哲学由基提翁的芝诺于公元前三世纪初在古希腊创立，爱比克泰德、加图、塞涅卡和马可·奥勒留等人都曾践行这一哲学。⭐️【the likes of = 像……这样的人】
Amazingly, we still have access to these ideas, despite the fact that the most famous Stoics never wrote anything down for publication. 令人惊叹的是，我们今天仍然能够接触到这些思想，尽管最著名的斯多葛学派人物从未为了出版而写下自己的思想。
Cato definitely didn't. 加图肯定没有这样做过。
Marcus Aurelius never intended his Meditations to be anything but personal. 马可·奥勒留从未打算让《沉思录》成为私人记录以外的任何东西。⭐️【anything but = 除……之外的任何东西（此处与否定搭配，表示“仅仅是……”）】
Seneca's letters were, well, letters and Epictetus' thoughts come to us by way of a note-taking student. 塞涅卡的书信，嗯，本来就只是书信；而爱比克泰德的思想，则是通过一名做笔记的学生流传下来的。⭐️【by way of = 通过；经由】
Stoic principles were based on the idea that its followers could have an unshakable happiness in this life and the key to achieving this was virtue. 斯多葛学派的原则建立在这样一种观念上：追随者能够在此生获得不可动摇的幸福，而实现这一点的关键在于德性。⭐️【unshakable = 不可动摇的】⭐️【virtue = 德性；美德】
The road to virtue, in turn, lay in understanding that destructive emotions, like anger and jealousy, are under our conscious control – they don't have to control us, because we can learn to control them. 而通往德性的道路，在于理解愤怒、嫉妒等破坏性情绪可以受到我们有意识的控制——它们不必支配我们，因为我们可以学会控制它们。⭐️【lie in = 在于】⭐️【conscious control = 有意识的控制】
In the words of Epictetus: “external events I cannot control, but the choices I make with regard to them, I do control”. 用爱比克泰德的话来说：“外界事件我无法控制，但面对这些事件所作的选择，我确实可以控制。”⭐️【with regard to = 关于；对于】
The modern day philosopher and writer Nassim Nicholas Taleb defines a Stoic as someone who has a different perspective on experiences which most of us would see as wholly negative;&nbsp; 当代哲学家兼作家纳西姆·尼古拉斯·塔勒布认为，面对大多数人眼中完全负面的经历，斯多葛主义者能够从不同的角度看待它们； ⭐ ️【initiation = 入门；启蒙；开始接触某种经验】
a Stoic “transforms fear into caution, pain into transformation, mistakes into initiation and desire into undertaking”. 他们“将恐惧转化为谨慎，将痛苦转化为蜕变，将错误转化为启蒙，将欲望转化为行动”。 ⭐ 【undertaking = 着手进行的事情；行动或事业】
Using this definition as a model, we can see that throughout the centuries Stoicism has been practised in more recent history by kings, presidents, artists, writers and entrepreneurs. 以这一定义为参照，我们可以看到，在较近的历史中，几个世纪以来，国王、总统、艺术家、作家和企业家都曾践行斯多葛哲学。
The founding fathers of the United States were inspired by the philosophy. 美国的开国元勋们曾受到这一哲学的启发。⭐️【founding fathers = 开国元勋；创始人】
George Washington was introduced to Stoicism by his neighbours at age seventeen, and later, put on a play based on the life of Cato to inspire his men. 乔治·华盛顿十七岁时，经邻居介绍接触了斯多葛哲学。后来，他还安排上演了一部以加图生平为题材的戏剧，以鼓舞部下。⭐️【put on a play = 上演一部戏剧】
Thomas Jefferson kept a copy of Seneca beside his bed. 托马斯·杰斐逊在床边放着一本塞涅卡的著作。
Writers and artists have also been inspired by the stoics. 作家和艺术家也曾受到斯多葛学派的启发。
Eugène Delacroix, the renowned French Romantic artist (known best for his painting Liberty Leading the People) was an ardent Stoic, referring to it as his “consoling religion”. 著名的法国浪漫主义画家欧仁·德拉克洛瓦，以《自由引导人民》最为人熟知。他是斯多葛哲学的热忱信奉者，将其称为自己“带来慰藉的宗教”。⭐️【ardent = 热忱的；热烈的】⭐️【consoling = 带来安慰的；给予慰藉的】
The economist Adam Smith's theories on capitalism were significantly influenced by the Stoicism that he studied as a schoolboy, under a teacher who had translated Marcus Aurelius' works. 经济学家亚当·斯密关于资本主义的理论，深受他学生时代所学的斯多葛哲学影响。他当时的老师曾翻译过马可·奥勒留的著作。
Today's political leaders are no different, with many finding their inspiration from the ancient texts. 当今的政治领导人也不例外，许多人从这些古代著作中汲取灵感。
Former US president Bill Clinton rereads Marcus Aurelius every single year, and many have compared former President Obama's calm leadership style to that of Cato. 美国前总统比尔·克林顿每年都会重读马可·奥勒留的著作，许多人也将前总统奥巴马沉着的领导风格与加图相提并论。
Wen Jiabao, the former prime minister of China, claims that Meditations is one of two books he travels with and that he has read it more than one hundred times over the course of his life. 中国前总理温家宝表示，《沉思录》是他出行时随身携带的两本书之一，而且他一生中已读过一百多遍。⭐️【over the course of = 在……期间；在……的过程中】
Stoicism had a profound influence on Albert Ellis, who invented Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, which is used to help people manage their problems by changing the way that they think and behave.It's most commonly used to treat depression. 斯多葛哲学对阿尔伯特·埃利斯产生了深远影响。他创立了认知行为疗法，这种疗法通过改变人们的思维和行为方式，帮助他们应对自身问题。这种疗法最常用于治疗抑郁症。 ⭐ ️【profound influence = 深远影响】⭐️【Cognitive Behaviour Therapy = 认知行为疗法】
The idea is that we can take control of our lives by challenging the irrational beliefs that create our faulty thinking, symptoms and behaviours by using logic instead. 其理念是，我们可以运用逻辑，质疑那些导致错误思维、症状和行为的非理性信念，从而掌控自己的生活。⭐️【challenge = 质疑；对……提出异议】⭐️【irrational beliefs = 非理性信念】
Stoicism has also become popular in the world of business. 斯多葛哲学在商界也逐渐流行起来。
Stoic principles can build the resilience and state of mind required to overcome setbacks because Stoics teach turning obstacles into opportunity. 斯多葛哲学的原则能够培养克服挫折所需的韧性和心态，因为它教导人们将障碍转化为机遇。⭐️【resilience = 韧性；受挫后的恢复能力】⭐️【setback = 挫折；阻碍进展的事情】
A lesson every business entrepreneur needs to learn. 这是每位创业者都需要学习的一课。
I would argue that studying Stoicism is as relevant today as it was 2,000 years ago, thanks to its brilliant insights into how to lead a good life. 我认为，学习斯多葛哲学在今天与两千年前同样具有现实意义，因为它对如何过好人生有着精辟的见解。⭐️【relevant = 具有现实意义的；切合当前需要的】
At the very root of the thinking, there is a very simple way of living – control what you can and accept what you can't. 这一思想最根本的核心，是一种非常简单的生活方式：控制你能控制的，接受你无法控制的。⭐️【at the root of = 在……的根本；是……的核心】
This is not as easy as it sounds and will require considerable practice – it can take a lifetime to master. 这并不像听起来那么容易，需要大量练习——可能要用一生才能掌握。
The Stoics also believed the most important foundation for a good and happy life is not money, fame, power or pleasure, but having a disciplined and principled character – something which seems to resonate with many people today. 斯多葛学派还认为，美好而幸福的人生最重要的基础，不是金钱、名声、权力或享乐，而是拥有自律、坚守原则的品格——这一点似乎也引起了当今许多人的共鸣。⭐️【principled = 坚守原则的】⭐️【resonate with = 引起……的共鸣】
