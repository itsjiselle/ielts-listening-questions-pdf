Dancing is something that humans do when they want to have a good time. 人们想要享受欢乐时光时，常常会跳舞。
It's a universal response to music, found in all cultures. 舞蹈是人类对音乐的一种普遍反应，存在于所有文化之中。⭐️【a universal response to = 对……的一种普遍反应】
But what's only been discovered recently is that dancing not only makes us feel good, it's also extremely good for our health. 不过，人们直到最近才发现，跳舞不仅能让我们心情愉悦，还对健康大有益处。
Dancing, like other forms of exercise, releases hormones, such as dopamine, which make us feel relaxed and happy. 和其他运动形式一样，跳舞会促使身体释放多巴胺等激素，让我们感到放松和快乐。⭐️【dopamine = 多巴胺】
And it also reduces feelings of stress or anxiety. 它还能减轻压力感或焦虑感。
Dancing is also a sociable activity, which is another reason it makes us feel good. 跳舞也是一种社交活动，这也是它让我们感觉良好的另一个原因。⭐️【a sociable activity = 一种能与他人交往的活动】
One study compared people's enjoyment of dancing at home in front of a video with dancing in a group in a studio. 一项研究比较了人们在家跟着视频独自跳舞，以及在舞蹈室集体跳舞时的愉悦程度。
The people dancing in a group reported feeling happier, whereas those dancing alone did not. 集体跳舞的人表示自己感到更快乐，而独自跳舞的人则没有这种感受。⭐️【whereas = 而；用于对比两种情况】
In another experiment, university researchers at York and Sheffield took a group of students and sent each of them into a lab where music was played for five minutes. 在另一项实验中，约克和谢菲尔德的大学研究人员招募了一组学生，让他们分别进入实验室，实验室内会播放五分钟音乐。
Each had to choose from three options: to sit and listen quietly to the music, to cycle on an exercise bike while they listened, or to get up and dance. 每个人都必须从三个选项中选择一个：坐着安静地听音乐，边听音乐边骑健身自行车，或者起身跳舞。⭐️【an exercise bike = 健身自行车】
All were given cognitive tasks to perform before and after. 所有人在活动前后都需要完成认知任务。⭐️【cognitive tasks = 认知任务】
The result showed that those who chose to dance showed much more creativity when doing problem-solving tasks. 结果表明，那些选择跳舞的人在完成问题解决任务时，表现出了明显更强的创造力。⭐️【problem-solving tasks = 问题解决任务】
Doctor Lovatt at the University of Hertfordshire believes dance could be a very useful way to help people suffering from mental health problems. 赫特福德大学的Lovatt博士认为，舞蹈可能是帮助心理健康问题患者的一种非常有效的方式。
He thinks dance should be prescribed as therapy to help people overcome issues such as depression. 他认为，应该将舞蹈作为一种治疗方式推荐给患者，帮助他们克服抑郁症等问题。⭐️【be prescribed as therapy = 被作为治疗方式开具或推荐】⭐️【overcome = 克服；战胜】
It's well established that dance is a good way of encouraging adolescent girls to take exercise but what about older people? 舞蹈是鼓励青春期女孩参加锻炼的有效方式，这一点已得到充分证实，但对老年人来说呢？⭐️【It's well established that = ……已得到充分证实】⭐️【adolescent girls = 青春期女孩】
Studies have shown that there are enormous benefits for people in their sixties and beyond. 研究表明，舞蹈对六十多岁及年龄更大的人有极大的益处。⭐️【in their sixties and beyond = 六十多岁及年龄更大】
One of the great things about dance is that there are no barriers to participation. 舞蹈的一大优点是，参与起来没有什么门槛。⭐️【barriers to participation = 参与障碍；参与门槛】
Anyone can have a go, even those whose standard of fitness is quite low. 任何人都可以尝试，即使体能水平较低的人也不例外。⭐️【have a go = 试一试】
Dance can be especially beneficial for older adults who can't run or do more intense workouts, or for those who don't want to. 对于无法跑步或进行更高强度锻炼的老年人，以及不愿进行这些运动的人来说，舞蹈尤其有益。⭐️【intense workouts = 高强度锻炼】
One 2015 study found that even a gentle dance workout helps to promote a healthy heart. 2015年的一项研究发现，即使是温和的舞蹈锻炼，也有助于促进心脏健康。⭐️【a gentle dance workout = 低强度的舞蹈锻炼】
And there's plenty of evidence which suggests that dancing lowers the risk of falls, which could result in a broken hip, for example, by helping people to improve their balance. 此外，大量证据表明，跳舞能帮助人们改善平衡能力，从而降低跌倒的风险，而跌倒可能造成髋部骨折等后果。⭐️【lower the risk of falls = 降低跌倒风险】⭐️【a broken hip = 髋部骨折】
There are some less obvious benefits of dance for older people too. 舞蹈对老年人还有一些不那么明显的益处。
One thing I hadn't realised before researching this topic was that dance isn't just a physical challenge. 在研究这个话题之前，我没有意识到的一点是，舞蹈带来的挑战并不只在身体层面。
It also requires a lot of concentration because you need to remember different steps and routines. 它还需要高度专注，因为你必须记住不同的舞步和成套动作。⭐️【routines = 成套动作；固定编排的动作组合】
For older people, this kind of activity is especially important because it forces their brain to process things more quickly and to retain more information. 对老年人来说，这类活动尤其重要，因为它迫使大脑更快地处理信息，并记住更多内容。⭐️【retain information = 记住信息；保留信息】
Current research also shows that dance promotes a general sense of well-being in older participants, which can last up to a week after a class. 目前的研究还表明，跳舞能提升老年参与者整体的身心舒适感，而且这种感受在一节课结束后可以持续长达一周。⭐️【a general sense of well-being = 整体的身心舒适感；整体幸福感】
Participants report feeling less tired and having greater motivation to be more active and do daily activities such as gardening or walking to the shops or a park. 参与者表示，他们感觉不那么疲惫，也更有动力增加活动，进行园艺劳动、步行去商店或公园等日常活动。⭐️【have greater motivation to = 更有动力去……】
Ballroom or country dancing, both popular with older people, have to be done in groups. 交谊舞和乡村舞都很受老年人欢迎，而且都需要集体参与。⭐️【ballroom dancing = 交谊舞】⭐️【country dancing = 乡村舞】
They require collaboration and often involve touching a dance partner, all of which encourages interaction on the dance floor. 这些舞蹈需要相互配合，而且往往涉及与舞伴的身体接触，这些都有助于促进舞池中的互动。⭐️【collaboration = 合作；协作】
This helps to develop new relationships and can reduce older people's sense of isolation, which is a huge problem in many countries. 这有助于建立新的人际关系，并能减轻老年人的孤独感，而这种孤独感在许多国家都是一个严重问题。⭐️【a sense of isolation = 孤独感；与他人疏离的感觉】
I also looked at the benefits of Zumba. 我还研究了尊巴舞的益处。
Fifteen million people in 180 countries now regularly take a Zumba class, an aerobic workout based on Latin American dance moves. 如今，一百八十个国家的一千五百万人定期参加尊巴课程，这是一种以拉丁美洲舞蹈动作为基础的有氧锻炼。⭐️【an aerobic workout = 有氧锻炼】
John Porcari, a professor of exercise and sport science at the University of Wisconsin, analysed a group of women who were Zumba regulars and found that a class lasting 40 minutes burns about 370 calories. 威斯康星大学运动与体育科学教授John Porcari研究了一组经常参加尊巴课程的女性，发现一节四十分钟的课程大约能消耗三百七十卡路里。⭐️【regulars = 经常参加某项活动的人；常客】
This is similar to moderately intense exercises like step aerobics or kickboxing. 这与踏板操或搏击操等中等强度运动的效果相近。⭐️【moderately intense = 中等强度的】⭐️【step aerobics = 踏板有氧操】⭐️【kickboxing = 踢拳；此处指相关搏击健身运动】
A study in the American Journal of Health Behavior showed that when women with obesity did Zumba three times a week for 16 weeks, they lost an average of 1.2 kilos and lowered their percentage of body fat by 1%. 《美国健康行为杂志》上的一项研究表明，患有肥胖症的女性连续十六周、每周跳三次尊巴后，体重平均减轻了1.2公斤，体脂率下降了一个百分点。⭐️【obesity = 肥胖症】⭐️【percentage of body fat = 体脂率】
More importantly, the women enjoyed the class so much that they made it a habit and continued to attend classes at least once a week – very unusual for an aerobic exercise programme. 更重要的是，这些女性非常喜欢这门课程，因此养成了习惯，并继续保持每周至少上一次课——对于有氧运动项目来说，这种情况十分少见。⭐️【make it a habit = 将其养成习惯】
Dance is never going to compete with high-intensity workouts when it comes to physical fitness gains, but its popularity is likely to keep on rising because it's such a fun way to keep fit. 就体能提升而言，舞蹈始终无法与高强度锻炼相媲美，但它很可能会越来越受欢迎，因为这是一种充满乐趣的健身方式。⭐️【compete with = 与……相媲美；与……竞争】⭐️【when it comes to = 谈到；就……而言】⭐️【physical fitness gains = 体能的提升】
