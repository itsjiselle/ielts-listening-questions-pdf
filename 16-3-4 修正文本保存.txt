So today we're going to look at an important creative activity and that's hand knitting. 今天我们将探讨一项重要的创意活动，那就是手工编织。⭐️【hand knitting = 手工编织；手工针织】
Ancient knitted garments have been found in many different countries, showing that knitting is a global activity with a long history. 许多不同国家都发现过古代的针织衣物，这表明编织是一项历史悠久、遍布世界的活动。⭐️【knitted garments = 针织衣物】
When someone says the word ‘knitting' we might well picture an elderly person – a grandmother perhaps – sitting by the fire knitting garments for themselves or other members of the family. 当有人提起“编织”这个词时，我们很可能会想象一位老人——也许是一位祖母——坐在炉火旁，为自己或其他家庭成员编织衣物。⭐️【might well = 很可能会】⭐️【picture = 想象；在脑海中描绘】
It's a homely image, but one that may lead you to feel that knitting is an activity of the past – and, indeed, during the previous decade, it was one of the skills that was predicted to vanish from everyday life. 这幅画面充满居家生活的温馨，但也可能让你觉得编织是一项属于过去的活动。事实上，在此前的十年间，编织曾被预测为将从日常生活中消失的技能之一。⭐️【homely = 温馨舒适的；有家庭气息的】⭐️【vanish from = 从……中消失】
For although humans have sewn and knitted their own clothing for a very long time, many of these craft-based skills went into decline when industrial machines took over – mainly because they were no longer passed down from one generation to another. 这是因为，尽管人类自己缝制和编织衣物已有很长的历史，但在工业机器接手这些工作后，许多手工技艺逐渐衰落，主要原因是它们不再代代相传。⭐️【go into decline = 开始衰落】⭐️【take over = 接手；取代原有方式】⭐️【pass down from one generation to another = 代代相传】
However, that's all changing and interest in knitting classes in many countries is actually rising, as more and more people are seeking formal instruction in the skill. 然而，这一切正在改变。随着越来越多的人寻求正规的编织指导，许多国家的人们对编织课程的兴趣实际上正在上升。⭐️【formal instruction = 正规指导；系统教学】
With that trend, we're also seeing an increase in the sales figures for knitting equipment. 伴随这一趋势，编织工具的销售额也在增长。⭐️【sales figures = 销售数据；销售额】
So why do people want to be taught to knit at a time when a machine can readily do the job for them? 那么，在机器可以轻松代劳的时代，人们为什么还想学习编织呢？⭐️【readily = 轻易地；毫不困难地】
The answer is that knitting, as a handicraft, has numerous benefits for those doing it. 答案是，编织作为一种手工艺，能给参与者带来许多益处。⭐️【handicraft = 手工艺】
Let's consider what some of these might be. 让我们来看看其中可能有哪些益处。
While many people knitted garments in the past because they couldn't afford to buy clothes, it's still true today that knitting can be helpful if you're experiencing economic hardship. 过去，许多人编织衣物是因为买不起衣服；而在今天，如果你正面临经济困难，编织仍然能够有所帮助。⭐️【experience economic hardship = 经历经济困难】
If you have several children who all need warm winter clothes, knitting may save you a lot of money. 如果你有几个孩子，都需要保暖的冬衣，编织或许能为你省下不少钱。
And the results of knitting your own clothes can be very rewarding, even though the skills you need to get going are really quite basic and the financial outlay is minimal. 而且，亲手织出衣服会让人很有成就感，尽管入门所需的技能其实相当基础，资金投入也很少。⭐️【rewarding = 带来满足感或成就感的】⭐️【get going = 开始；起步】⭐️【financial outlay = 资金支出；经济投入】⭐️【minimal = 极少的】
But the more significant benefits in today's world are to do with well-being. 不过，在当今世界，编织更重要的益处与身心健康有关。⭐️【be to do with = 与……有关】⭐️【well-being = 身心健康；幸福感】
In a world where it's estimated that we spend up to nine hours a day online, doing something with our hands that is craft-based makes us feel good. 据估计，如今我们每天上网的时间可长达九个小时，而在这样的世界里，动手做些手工活会让我们感觉良好。
It releases us from the stress of a technological, fast-paced life. 它让我们从科技化、快节奏生活带来的压力中解脱出来。⭐️【release someone from = 使某人摆脱……】⭐️【fast-paced life = 快节奏的生活】
Now, let's look back a bit to early knitting activities. 现在，让我们回顾一下早期的编织活动。
In fact, no one really knows when knitting first began, but archaeological remains have disclosed plenty of information for us to think about. 事实上，没有人确切知道编织最早始于何时，但考古遗存已经揭示了大量值得我们思考的信息。⭐️【archaeological remains = 考古遗存】⭐️【disclose = 揭示；透露】
One of the interesting things about knitting is that the earliest pieces of clothing that have been found suggest that most of the items produced were round rather than flat. 关于编织，有趣的一点是，已发现的最早衣物表明，当时制作的大多数物品都是环状的，而不是平面的。
Discoveries from the 3rd and 4th centuries in Egypt show that things like socks and gloves, that were needed to keep hands and feet warm, were knitted in one piece using four or five needles. 埃及出土的公元三、四世纪的物品表明，袜子和手套等用于手脚保暖的物品，是用四根或五根织针一体织成的。⭐️【in one piece = 一体成形地；作为一个整体】
That's very different from most knitting patterns today, which only require two. 这与今天大多数只需要两根织针的编织图样大不相同。⭐️【knitting patterns = 编织图样；编织说明】
What's more, the very first needles people used were hand carved out of wood and other natural materials, like bone, whereas today's needles are largely made of steel or plastic and make that characteristic clicking sound when someone's using them. 此外，人们最早使用的织针，是用木头和骨头等天然材料手工雕刻而成的；而如今的织针大多由钢或塑料制成，使用时会发出那种特有的咔嗒声。⭐️【carve out of = 用……雕刻制成】⭐️【whereas = 而；用于对比】⭐️【characteristic = 特有的；具有典型特征的】
Ancient people knitted using yarns made from linen, hemp, cotton and wool, and these were often very rough on the skin. 古人用亚麻、大麻、棉和羊毛制成的纱线进行编织，而这些纱线接触皮肤时往往十分粗糙。⭐️【yarn = 纱线】⭐️【linen = 亚麻；亚麻纤维】⭐️【hemp = 大麻纤维】⭐️【rough on the skin = 接触皮肤时粗糙不适的】
The spinning wheel, which allowed people to make finer yarns and produce much greater quantities of them, led to the dominance of wool in the knitting industry – often favoured for its warmth. 纺车使人们能够制作更细的纱线，并大幅提高产量，从而促使羊毛在编织业中占据主导地位——羊毛常因其保暖性而受到青睐。⭐️【spinning wheel = 纺车】⭐️【finer yarns = 更细的纱线】⭐️【dominance = 主导地位】⭐️【be favoured for = 因……而受到青睐】
Another interesting fact about knitting is that because it was practised in so many parts of the world for so many purposes, regional differences in style developed. 关于编织，另一个有趣的事实是，由于世界各地的人们出于各种目的进行编织，其风格逐渐形成了地区差异。⭐️【regional differences = 地区差异】
This visual identity has allowed researchers to match bits of knitted clothing that have been unearthed over time to the region from which the wearer came or the job that he or she did. 这些可辨识的外观特征，使研究人员能够根据陆续出土的针织衣物残片，判断穿着者来自哪个地区，或从事什么职业。⭐️【visual identity = 可辨识的视觉特征】⭐️【unearth = 挖掘出；使出土】⭐️【match … to … = 将……与……对应起来】
As I've mentioned, knitting offered people from poor communities a way of making extra money while doing other tasks. 正如我之前提到的，编织让贫困社区的人们能够在做其他事情的同时赚取额外收入。
For many centuries, it seems, men, women and children took every opportunity to knit, for example, while watching over sheep, walking to market or riding in boats. 几个世纪以来，男人、女人和孩子似乎都抓住一切机会编织，例如在看守羊群、步行去集市或乘船时。⭐️【take every opportunity to = 抓住一切机会做……】⭐️【watch over = 看管；照看】
