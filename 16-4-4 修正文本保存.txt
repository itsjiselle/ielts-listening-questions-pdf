One of the most famous cases of extinction is that of a bird known as the dodo. 最著名的物种灭绝案例之一，就是一种名为渡渡鸟的鸟类的灭绝。⭐️【extinction = 灭绝】
In fact there's even a saying in English, 'as dead as the dodo', used to refer to something which no longer exists. 事实上，英语中甚至有“像渡渡鸟一样死绝了”这样的说法，用来指某种已经不复存在的事物。⭐️【as dead as the dodo = 彻底消失的；早已不复存在的】
But for many centuries the dodo was alive and well, although it could only be found in one place, the island of Mauritius in the Indian Ocean. 但几个世纪以来，渡渡鸟一直安然生存着，只不过它仅存在于一个地方——印度洋上的毛里求斯岛。⭐️【alive and well = 活得好好的；安然存在的】
It was a very large bird, about one metre tall, and over the centuries it had lost the ability to fly, but it survived happily under the trees that covered the island. 它是一种体型很大的鸟，身高约一米，在几个世纪的演变中失去了飞行能力，但仍在覆盖全岛的树林下安然生活。
Then in the year 1507 the first Portuguese ships stopped at the island. 后来，在1507年，第一批葡萄牙船只在这座岛上停靠。
The sailors were carrying spices back to Europe, and found the island a convenient stopping place where they could stock up with food and water for the rest of the voyage, but they didn't settle on Mauritius. 水手们正将香料运回欧洲，发现这座岛是一个便利的中途停靠点，可以在这里补充食物和饮水，为余下的航程做准备，但他们没有在毛里求斯定居。⭐️【stock up with = 储备；补充所需的物资】⭐️【voyage = 航海旅程；航程】
However, in 1638 the Dutch arrived and set up a colony there. 然而，1638年，荷兰人来到这里，并建立了殖民地。⭐️【set up a colony = 建立殖民地】
These first human inhabitants of the island found the dodo birds a convenient source of meat, although not everyone liked the taste. 岛上的这批最早人类居民发现，渡渡鸟是一种容易获取的肉食来源，尽管并非所有人都喜欢它的味道。⭐️【inhabitants = 居民；居住者】
It's hard to get an accurate description of what the dodo actually looked like. 我们很难准确描述渡渡鸟究竟长什么样。
We do have some written records from sailors, and a few pictures, but we don't know how reliable these are. 我们确实有一些水手留下的文字记录，以及几幅图画，但不知道这些资料有多可靠。
The best-known picture is a Dutch painting in which the bird appears to be extremely fat, but this may not be accurate – an Indian painting done at the same time shows a much thinner bird. 最著名的图像是一幅荷兰绘画，画中的渡渡鸟显得极其肥胖，但这可能并不准确——同一时期的一幅印度绘画中的渡渡鸟就瘦得多。
Although attempts were made to preserve the bodies of some of the birds, no complete specimen survives. 尽管人们曾尝试保存一些渡渡鸟的遗体，但没有完整的标本留存至今。⭐️【specimen = 标本】⭐️【survive = 留存；保存至今】
In the early 17th century four dried parts of a bird were known to exist – of these, three have disappeared, so only one example of soft tissue from the dodo survives, a dodo head. 十七世纪初，已知存在四份渡渡鸟身体部位的干制标本，其中三份已经遗失，因此如今仅存一份渡渡鸟软组织标本，也就是一颗渡渡鸟头部。⭐️【soft tissue = 软组织】
Bones have also been found, but there's only one complete skeleton in existence. 人们也发现过渡渡鸟的骨骼，但现存的完整骨架只有一具。⭐️【in existence = 现存的；存在的】
This single dodo skeleton has recently been the subject of scientific research which suggests that many of the earlier beliefs about dodos may have been incorrect. 最近，这唯一的一具渡渡鸟骨架成为科学研究的对象，研究表明，过去关于渡渡鸟的许多看法可能都是错误的。⭐️【the subject of scientific research = 科学研究的对象】
For example, early accounts of the birds mention how slow and clumsy it was, but scientists now believe the bird's strong knee joints would have made it capable of movement which was not slow, but actually quite fast. 例如，早期记述提到渡渡鸟行动迟缓、笨拙，但科学家如今认为，它强健的膝关节应该使它能够相当快速地移动，而不是缓慢行走。⭐️【accounts = 记述；描述】⭐️【clumsy = 笨拙的】⭐️【knee joints = 膝关节】
In fact, one 17th century sailor wrote that he found the birds hard to catch. 事实上，一位十七世纪的水手曾写道，他发现这些鸟很难捕捉。
It's true that the dodo's small wings wouldn't have allowed it to leave the ground, but the scientists suggest that these were probably employed for balance while going over uneven ground. 渡渡鸟的小翅膀确实无法让它飞离地面，但科学家认为，它们很可能被用来在经过崎岖地面时保持平衡。⭐️【be employed for = 被用于】⭐️【uneven ground = 凹凸不平的地面】
Another group of scientists carried out analysis of the dodo's skull. 另一组科学家分析了渡渡鸟的头骨。⭐️【skull = 头骨】
They found that the reports of the lack of intelligence of the dodo were not borne out by their research, which suggested the bird's brain was not small, but average in size. 他们发现，研究结果并不支持那些认为渡渡鸟智力低下的说法；研究表明，它的大脑并不小，而是处于平均大小。⭐️【bear out = 证实；支持某种说法】⭐️【average in size = 大小处于平均水平】
In fact, in relation to its body size, it was similar to that of the pigeon, which is known to be a highly intelligent bird. 事实上，相对于体型而言，它的大脑大小与鸽子的相近，而鸽子是一种公认非常聪明的鸟。⭐️【in relation to = 相对于】⭐️【that of = 指代前面提到的同类事物，此处指大脑大小】
The researchers also found that the structure of the bird's skull suggested that one sense which was particularly well-developed was that of smell. 研究人员还发现，渡渡鸟的头骨结构表明，它有一种特别发达的感官，那就是嗅觉。⭐️【well-developed = 发达的；发展充分的】
So the dodo may also have been particularly good at locating ripe fruit and other food in the island's thick vegetation. 因此，渡渡鸟可能也特别擅长在岛上茂密的植被中寻找成熟的果实和其他食物。⭐️【locate = 找到……的位置】⭐️【thick vegetation = 茂密的植被】
So it looks as if the dodo was better able to survive and defend itself than was originally believed. 因此，渡渡鸟的生存和自我防卫能力，似乎比人们最初认为的更强。
Yet less than 200 years after Europeans first arrived on the island, they had become extinct. 然而，在欧洲人首次登岛后不到两百年的时间里，渡渡鸟就灭绝了。
So what was the reason for this? 那么，原因究竟是什么呢？
For a long time, it was believed that the dodos were hunted to extinction, but scientists now believe the situation was more complicated than this. 长期以来，人们认为渡渡鸟是被捕猎至灭绝的，但科学家现在认为，实际情况比这更复杂。⭐️【be hunted to extinction = 被捕猎至灭绝】
Another factor may have been the new species brought to the island by the sailors. 另一个因素可能是水手们带到岛上的新物种。
These included dogs, which would have been a threat to the dodos, and also monkeys, which ate the fruit that was the main part of the dodos' diet. 其中包括可能威胁渡渡鸟的狗，以及以水果为食的猴子，而水果也是渡渡鸟的主要食物。⭐️【a threat to = 对……的威胁】
These were brought to the island deliberately, but the ships also brought another type of creature – rats, which came to land from the ships and rapidly overran the island. 这些动物是被人有意带到岛上的，但船只还带来了另一种动物——老鼠，它们从船上来到陆地，迅速遍布全岛，泛滥成灾。⭐️【deliberately = 故意地；有意地】⭐️【overrun = 大量侵占；在……泛滥】
These upset the ecology of the island, not just the dodos but other species too. 老鼠破坏了岛上的生态平衡，不仅影响了渡渡鸟，也影响了其他物种。⭐️【upset = 扰乱；破坏原有平衡】⭐️【ecology = 生态；生态关系】
However, they were a particular danger to the dodos because they consumed their eggs, and since each dodo only laid one at a time, this probably had a devastating effect on populations. 不过，它们对渡渡鸟的威胁尤其严重，因为它们会吃掉渡渡鸟的蛋，而每只渡渡鸟一次只产一枚蛋，因此这很可能对渡渡鸟种群造成了毁灭性的影响。⭐️【lay = 产卵；下蛋】⭐️【have a devastating effect on = 对……造成毁灭性影响】
However, we now think that probably the main cause of the birds' extinction was not the introduction of non-native species, but the introduction of agriculture. 然而，我们现在认为，导致渡渡鸟灭绝的主要原因，可能不是外来物种的引入，而是农业的引入。⭐️【non-native species = 外来物种】⭐️【the introduction of = ……的引入】
This meant that the forest that had once covered all the island, and that had provided a perfect home for the dodo, was cut down so that crops such as sugar could be grown. 这意味着，曾经覆盖全岛、为渡渡鸟提供理想栖息地的森林遭到砍伐，以便种植甘蔗等作物。⭐️【cut down = 砍伐】
So although the dodo had survived for thousands of years, suddenly it was gone. 因此，尽管渡渡鸟已经生存了数千年，却突然就消失了。
