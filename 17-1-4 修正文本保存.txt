Labyrinths have existed for well over 4,000 years. 单径迷宫的历史已有四千多年。⭐️【well over = 远远超过】
Labyrinths and labyrinthine symbols have been found in regions as diverse as modern-day Turkey, Ireland, Greece, and India. 人们在如今的土耳其、爱尔兰、希腊和印度等差异很大的地区，都发现了单径迷宫及迷宫图案。⭐️【regions as diverse as = ……等各不相同的地区】⭐️【labyrinthine = 迷宫般的；错综复杂的】
There are various designs of labyrinth but what they all have in common is a winding spiral path which leads to a central area. 单径迷宫有各种设计，但它们的共同点是都有一条蜿蜒盘旋、通向中心区域的路径。⭐️【have in common = 有共同之处】⭐️【a winding spiral path = 一条蜿蜒盘旋的路径】
There is one starting point at the entrance and the goal is to reach the central area. 入口处有一个起点，目标是到达中心区域。
Finding your way through a labyrinth involves many twists and turns, but it’s not possible to get lost as there is only one single path. 穿行单径迷宫需要经过许多转弯，但由于只有一条路径，所以不可能迷路。⭐️【twists and turns = 曲折；弯弯绕绕】
In modern times, the word labyrinth has taken on a different meaning and is often used as a synonym for a maze. 到了现代，labyrinth这个词有了不同的含义，常被用作maze，也就是多岔迷宫的同义词。⭐️【take on a different meaning = 获得不同的含义】⭐️【a synonym for = ……的同义词】
A maze is quite different as it is a kind of puzzle with an intricate network of paths. 多岔迷宫则很不一样，它是一种由错综复杂的路径网络构成的解谜游戏。⭐️【intricate = 错综复杂的；精细复杂的】
Mazes became fashionable in the 15th and 16th centuries in Europe, and can still be found in the gardens of great houses and palaces. 十五、十六世纪时，多岔迷宫在欧洲流行起来，如今仍能在豪宅和宫殿的花园里见到。⭐️【great houses = 豪宅；大型宅邸】
The paths are usually surrounded by thick, high hedges so that it’s not possible to see over them. 这些路径通常被高大浓密的树篱围住，让人无法越过树篱看见另一边。⭐️【hedges = 树篱】
Entering a maze usually involves getting lost a few times before using logic to work out the pattern and find your way to the centre and then out again. 进入多岔迷宫后，人们通常会先迷路几次，然后运用逻辑弄清路径的布局，找到通往中心的路，再走出来。⭐️【work out the pattern = 弄清布局或规律】
There are lots of dead ends and paths which lead you back to where you started. 里面有许多死胡同，还有一些路径会把你带回起点。⭐️【dead ends = 死胡同；走不通的路】
The word ‘maze’ is believed to come from a Scandinavian word for a state of confusion. 人们认为，maze这个词源自一个表示“困惑状态”的斯堪的纳维亚语词。
This is where the word ‘amazing’ comes from.&nbsp; amazing这个词也源于此。
Labyrinths, on the other hand, have a very different function. 另一方面，单径迷宫的功能则大不相同。⭐️【on the other hand = 另一方面，用于引出对比】
Although people now often refer to things they find complicated as labyrinths, this is not how they were seen in the past. 尽管现在人们经常把自己认为复杂的事物称为“迷宫”，但过去人们并不是这样看待单径迷宫的。
The winding spiral of the labyrinth has been used for centuries as a metaphor for life’s journey. 几个世纪以来，单径迷宫中蜿蜒盘旋的路径一直被用来比喻人生旅程。⭐️【a metaphor for = 对……的比喻】
It served as a spiritual reminder that there is purpose and meaning to our lives and helped to give people a sense of direction. 它在精神层面提醒人们，我们的人生有目标、有意义，也帮助人们找到方向感。⭐️【serve as = 起到……的作用】⭐️【a sense of direction = 方向感；人生目标感】
Labyrinths are thought to encourage a feeling of calm and have been used as a meditation and prayer tool in many cultures over many centuries. 人们认为单径迷宫能带来平静感，许多文化几百年来一直将其用作冥想和祈祷的工具。⭐️【meditation = 冥想；静思】
The earliest examples of the labyrinth spiral pattern have been found carved into stone, from Sardinia to Scandinavia, from Arizona to India to Africa. 从撒丁岛到斯堪的纳维亚，从亚利桑那到印度，再到非洲，人们发现的最早迷宫螺旋图案都雕刻在石头上。⭐️【carved into stone = 雕刻在石头上的】
In Europe, these spiral carvings date from the late Bronze Age. 在欧洲，这些螺旋形雕刻可以追溯到青铜时代晚期。⭐️【date from = 始于；可追溯至】⭐️【the late Bronze Age = 青铜时代晚期】
The Native American Pima tribe wove baskets with a circular labyrinth design that depicted their own cosmology. 美洲原住民皮马部落编织的篮子上带有圆形迷宫图案，展现了他们自己的宇宙观。⭐️【depict = 描绘；表现】⭐️【cosmology = 宇宙观；宇宙论】
In Ancient Greece, the labyrinth spiral was used on coins around four thousand years ago. 大约四千年前，在古希腊，迷宫螺旋图案被用于硬币上。
Labyrinths made of mosaics were commonly found in bathhouses, villas and tombs throughout the Roman Empire. 在整个罗马帝国，浴场、别墅和墓葬中都常见用马赛克拼成的迷宫图案。⭐️【mosaics = 马赛克；镶嵌图案】⭐️【bathhouses = 公共浴场】
In Northern Europe, there were actual physical labyrinths designed for walking on. 在北欧，还存在专门供人们行走的实体单径迷宫。
These were cut into the turf or grass, usually in a circular pattern. 这些迷宫是在草皮或草地上开辟出来的，通常呈圆形图案。⭐️【turf = 草皮；草坪】
The origin of these walking labyrinths remains unclear, but they were probably used for fertility rites which may date back thousands of years. 这些步行迷宫的起源仍不清楚，但它们很可能被用于祈求生育或丰饶的仪式，而这些仪式可能已有数千年历史。⭐️【fertility rites = 祈求生育或丰饶的仪式】
Eleven examples of turf labyrinths survive today, including the large stone at Saffron Walden, England, which used to have a large tree in the middle of it. 如今仍有十一处草地迷宫留存，其中包括英格兰萨弗伦沃尔登的那块大石头，它的中央过去曾有一棵大树。⭐️【survive = 留存至今】
More recently labyrinths have experienced something of a revival. 近些年来，单径迷宫在一定程度上重新流行起来。⭐️【something of a revival = 某种程度的复兴；一定程度的重新流行】
Some believe that walking a labyrinth promotes healing and mindfulness, and there are those who believe in its emotional and physical benefits, which include slower breathing and a restored sense of balance and perspective. 有些人认为，行走单径迷宫能够促进身心康复和正念，也有人相信它能带来情绪和身体上的益处，包括让呼吸放缓，以及恢复内心的平衡和看待事物的理性视角。⭐️【mindfulness = 正念；对当下的专注觉察】⭐️【a sense of perspective = 客观看待事物、分清轻重的能力】
This idea has become so popular that labyrinths have been laid into the floors of spas, wellness centres and even prisons in recent years. 这一观念变得如此流行，以至于近年来，水疗馆、健康中心，甚至监狱都在地面上铺设了迷宫图案。⭐️【wellness centres = 健康中心；身心康养中心】
A pamphlet at Colorado Children’s Hospital informs patients that ‘walking a labyrinth can often calm people in the midst of a crisis’. 科罗拉多儿童医院的一本宣传册告诉患者：“在单径迷宫中行走，往往能让身处危机中的人平静下来。”⭐️【pamphlet = 小册子；宣传册】⭐️【in the midst of a crisis = 身处危机之中】
And apparently, it’s not only patients who benefit. 而且，显然受益的不只是患者。
Many visitors find walking a labyrinth less stressful than sitting in a corridor or waiting room. 许多来访者发现，在单径迷宫中行走，比坐在走廊或候诊室里更能减轻压力。
Some doctors even walk the labyrinth during their breaks. 一些医生甚至会在休息期间走一走单径迷宫。
In some hospitals, patients who can’t walk can have a paper ‘finger labyrinth’ brought to their bed. 在一些医院，无法行走的患者可以让人把纸质的“手指迷宫”送到床边。
The science behind the theory is a little sketchy, but there are dozens of small-scale studies which support claims about the benefits of labyrinths. 这一理论背后的科学依据还不够充分，但有几十项小规模研究支持有关迷宫益处的说法。⭐️【sketchy = 不充分的；不够详尽的】⭐️【small-scale studies = 小规模研究】
For example, one study found that walking a labyrinth provided ‘short-term calming, relaxation, and relief from anxiety’ for Alzheimer’s patients. 例如，一项研究发现，在单径迷宫中行走能够让阿尔茨海默病患者“短暂地平静、放松，并缓解焦虑”。⭐️【relief from anxiety = 焦虑的缓解】
So, what is it about labyrinths that makes their appeal so universal? 那么，究竟是单径迷宫的什么特点，让它具有如此普遍的吸引力呢？⭐️【appeal = 吸引力】
