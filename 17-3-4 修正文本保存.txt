Scientists believe that a majority of the earth’s bird population migrate in some fashion or other. 科学家认为，地球上的大多数鸟类都会以某种方式迁徙。⭐️【in some fashion or other = 以某种方式】
Some travel seasonally for relatively short distances, such as birds that move from their winter habitats in lowlands to mountain tops for the summers. 有些鸟类会进行距离相对较短的季节性迁徙，例如从低地的越冬栖息地迁往山顶度夏。⭐️【winter habitats = 越冬栖息地】⭐️【lowlands = 低地】
Others, like the Arctic Tern, travel more than 25,000 miles seasonally between the northern and southern poles. 另一些鸟类，例如北极燕鸥，则会随季节在南北两极之间迁徙，行程超过两万五千英里。⭐️【Arctic Tern = 北极燕鸥】
Bird migration has been studied over many centuries through a variety of observations. 几个世纪以来，人们一直通过各种观察研究鸟类迁徙。
But until relatively recently, where birds went to in the winter was considered something of a mystery. 但直到相对不久之前，鸟类冬天去了哪里，在某种程度上仍被认为是个谜。⭐️【something of a mystery = 某种程度上是个谜；颇为神秘的事】
The lack of modern science and technology led to many theories that we now recognize as error-filled and even somewhat amusing. 由于缺乏现代科学技术，人们提出了许多理论，如今看来，这些理论错误百出，甚至有些令人发笑。
Take hibernation theory for example – two thousand years ago, it was commonly believed that when birds left an area, they went underwater to hibernate in the seas and oceans. 以冬眠理论为例——两千年前，人们普遍认为，鸟类离开一个地区后，会潜入海洋中的水下冬眠。⭐️【hibernation = 冬眠】⭐️【Take … for example = 以……为例，引出具体例子】
Another theory for the regular appearance and disappearance of birds was that they spent winter hidden in mud till the weather changed and food became abundant again. 对于鸟类有规律地出现和消失，还有一种解释：它们冬天藏在泥里，直到天气变化、食物再次充足时才出来。⭐️【abundant = 大量的；充足的】
The theory that some birds hibernate persisted until experiments were done on caged birds in the 1940s which demonstrated that birds have no hibernation instinct. 一些鸟类会冬眠的理论一直延续到二十世纪四十年代，直到对笼养鸟类进行的实验证明，鸟类并没有冬眠的本能。⭐️【persist = 持续存在】⭐️【hibernation instinct = 冬眠本能】
One of the earliest naturalists and philosophers from ancient Greece was Aristotle who was the first writer to discuss the disappearance and reappearance of some bird species at certain times of year. 亚里士多德是古希腊最早的博物学家和哲学家之一，也是第一位在著作中讨论某些鸟类在一年中特定时间消失、又重新出现的人。⭐️【naturalist = 博物学家；研究动植物的学者】
He developed the theory of transmutation, the seasonal change of one species into another, by observing redstarts and robins. 通过观察红尾鸲和知更鸟，他提出了物种转变理论，即一种物种会随季节转变成另一种物种。⭐️【transmutation = 转变；此处指物种之间的转化】⭐️【redstarts = 红尾鸲】⭐️【robins = 知更鸟】
He observed that in the autumn, small birds called ‘redstarts’ began to lose their feathers, which convinced Aristotle that they changed into robins for the winter, and back into redstarts in the summer. 他观察到，秋天时，一种叫作“红尾鸲”的小鸟开始掉羽毛，这使亚里士多德确信，它们在冬天变成知更鸟，到了夏天又变回红尾鸲。
These assumptions are understandable given that this pair of species are similar in shape, but are a classic example of an incorrect interpretation based on correct observations. 考虑到这两种鸟的外形相似，这些推断是可以理解的，但它们也是基于正确观察得出错误解释的典型例子。⭐️【given that = 考虑到；鉴于】⭐️【a classic example of = ……的典型例子】
The most bizarre theory was put forward by an English amateur scientist, Charles Morton, in the seventeenth century. 最离奇的理论是十七世纪一位名叫Charles Morton的英国业余科学家提出的。⭐️【bizarre = 离奇的；怪诞的】⭐️【put forward = 提出】
He wrote a surprisingly well-regarded paper claiming that birds migrate to the moon and back every year. 他写了一篇论文，声称鸟类每年都会迁往月球，再飞回来，而这篇论文竟然颇受推崇。⭐️【well-regarded = 受到好评的；受推崇的】
He came to this conclusion as the only logical explanation for the total disappearance of some species. 他得出这一结论，认为这是某些物种完全消失的唯一合乎逻辑的解释。⭐️【come to a conclusion = 得出结论】
One of the key moments in the development of migration theory came in 1822 when a white stork was shot in Germany. 迁徙理论发展过程中的一个关键时刻出现在1822年，当时一只白鹳在德国被射杀。⭐️【white stork = 白鹳】
This particular stork made history because of the long spear in its neck which incredibly had not killed it – everyone immediately realised this spear was definitely not European. 这只白鹳因脖子上插着的一根长矛而载入史册，令人难以置信的是，这根长矛竟然没有使它丧命——大家立刻意识到，这根矛绝不是来自欧洲的。⭐️【make history = 创造历史；载入史册】⭐️【spear = 长矛】
It turned out to be a spear from a tribe in Central Africa. 结果发现，这是一根来自非洲中部某个部落的长矛。⭐️【turn out to be = 结果发现是；原来是】
This was a truly defining moment in the history of ornithology because it was the first evidence that storks spend their winters in sub-Saharan Africa. 这在鸟类学史上是一个真正具有决定性意义的时刻，因为它首次提供了证据，证明白鹳会在撒哈拉以南非洲越冬。⭐️【a defining moment = 具有决定性意义的时刻；转折点】⭐️【ornithology = 鸟类学】⭐️【sub-Saharan Africa = 撒哈拉以南非洲】
You can still see the ‘arrow stork’ in the Zoological Collection of the University of Rostock in Germany. 如今，你仍可以在德国罗斯托克大学的动物学藏品中看到这只“箭鹳”。⭐️【Zoological Collection = 动物学藏品】
People gradually became aware that European birds moved south in autumn and north in summer but didn’t know much about it until the practice of catching birds and putting rings on their legs became established. 人们逐渐意识到，欧洲鸟类会在秋天向南迁徙、夏天向北迁徙，但直到捕捉鸟类并在它们腿上佩戴脚环的做法确立起来之前，人们对此了解仍然不多。⭐️【become established = 得到确立；成为普遍采用的做法】
Before this, very little information was available about the actual destinations of particular species and how they travelled there. 在此之前，关于特定鸟类实际迁往何处，以及它们如何到达那里，人们掌握的信息非常有限。
People speculated that larger birds provided a kind of taxi service for smaller birds by carrying them on their backs. 人们猜测，大鸟会把小鸟背在背上，为它们提供一种类似出租车的搭载服务。⭐️【speculate = 推测；猜测】
This idea came about because it seemed impossible that small birds weighing only a few grams could fly over vast oceans. 之所以产生这种想法，是因为体重只有几克的小鸟似乎不可能飞越辽阔的海洋。⭐️【come about = 产生；发生】⭐️【vast oceans = 辽阔的海洋】
This idea was supported by observations of bird behaviour such as the harassment of larger birds by smaller birds. 人们观察到小鸟骚扰大鸟等鸟类行为，认为这些现象支持了这一想法。⭐️【harassment = 骚扰；反复侵扰】
The development of bird ringing, by a Danish schoolteacher, Hans Christian Cornelius Mortensen, made many discoveries possible. 丹麦教师Hans Christian Cornelius Mortensen发展了鸟类环志技术，使许多发现成为可能。⭐️【bird ringing = 鸟类环志，通过佩戴脚环标记鸟类以便追踪研究】
This is still common practice today and relies upon what is known as ‘recovery’ – this is when ringed birds are found dead in the place they have migrated to, and identified. 如今，这仍是一种常见做法，依靠的是所谓的“回收记录”——也就是人们在鸟类迁徙到的地方发现已死亡的戴环鸟，并确认其身份。⭐️【common practice = 常见做法】⭐️【recovery = 此处指环志鸟被重新发现并确认身份的记录】
Huge amounts of data were gathered in the early part of the twentieth century and for the first time in history people understood where birds actually went to in winter. 二十世纪初，人们收集了大量数据，并在历史上首次弄清楚了鸟类冬天究竟去了哪里。
In 1931, an atlas was published showing where the most common species of European birds migrated to.&nbsp; 在1931年，一本图集出版，展示了欧洲最常见鸟类的迁徙目的地。 ⭐ ️【atlas = 地图集；图集】
