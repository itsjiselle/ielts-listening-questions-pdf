Hello everyone. 大家好。
Today we’re going to look at another natural food product and that’s maple syrup. 今天我们将介绍另一种天然食品，那就是枫糖浆。⭐️【maple syrup = 枫糖浆】
What is this exactly? 它究竟是什么呢？
Well, maple syrup looks rather like clear honey, but it’s not made by bees; it’s produced from the plant fluid – or sap – inside the maple tree and that makes maple syrup a very natural product. 枫糖浆看起来很像清澈的蜂蜜，但它并不是蜜蜂酿造的，而是由枫树内部的植物液体——也就是树液——制成，因此是一种非常天然的产品。⭐️【sap = 树液】
Maple syrup is a thick, golden, sweet-tasting liquid that can be bought in bottles or jars and poured onto food such as waffles and ice cream or used in the baking of cakes and pastries. 枫糖浆是一种浓稠、金黄、味甜的液体，以瓶装或罐装形式出售，可以浇在华夫饼、冰淇淋等食物上，也可以用于烘焙蛋糕和酥点。⭐️【pastries = 酥点；油酥糕点】
It contains no preservatives or added ingredients, and it provides a healthy alternative to refined sugar. 它不含防腐剂或其他添加成分，是精制糖的一种健康替代品。⭐️【preservatives = 防腐剂】⭐️【an alternative to = ……的替代品】⭐️【refined sugar = 精制糖】
Let’s just talk a bit about the maple tree itself, which is where maple syrup comes from. 我们先简单谈谈枫树本身，也就是枫糖浆的来源。
So, there are many species of maple tree, and they’ll grow without fertilizer in areas where there’s plenty of moisture in the soil. 枫树有许多种，在土壤水分充足的地区，它们无需施肥就能生长。⭐️【moisture = 水分；湿气】
However, they’ll only do this if another important criterion is fulfilled, which is that they must have full or partial sun exposure during the day and very cool nights – and I’ll talk more about that in a minute. 不过，它们只有在另一个重要条件得到满足时才能这样生长，那就是白天必须得到充分或部分日照，而夜间要非常凉爽——稍后我会进一步解释这一点。⭐️【fulfil a criterion = 满足一项标准或条件】⭐️【sun exposure = 日照；阳光照射】
There are only certain parts of the world that provide all these conditions: one is Canada, and by that, I mean all parts of Canada, and the other is the north-eastern states of North America. 世界上只有某些地区同时具备这些条件：一处是加拿大，我指的是加拿大所有地区，另一处是北美洲东北部的各州。
In these areas, the climate suits the trees perfectly. 在这些地区，气候完全适合枫树生长。
In fact, Canada produces over two-thirds of the world’s maple syrup, which is why the five-pointed maple leaf is a Canadian symbol and has featured on the flag since 1964. 事实上，全球超过三分之二的枫糖浆产自加拿大，这也就是为什么五角枫叶成为加拿大的象征，并从1964年起出现在加拿大国旗上。⭐️【feature on = 出现在……上；成为……的一部分】
So how did maple syrup production begin? 那么，枫糖浆的生产是如何开始的呢？
Well, long before Europeans settled in these parts of the world, the indigenous communities had started producing maple sugar. 早在欧洲人到这些地区定居之前，当地原住民群体就已经开始生产枫糖。⭐️【indigenous communities = 原住民群体】
They bored holes in the trunks of maple trees and used containers made of tree bark to collect the liquid sap as it poured out. 他们在枫树树干上钻孔，用树皮制成的容器收集流出的树液。⭐️【bore holes = 钻孔】⭐️【tree bark = 树皮】
As they were unable to keep the liquid for any length of time – they didn’t have storage facilities in those days – they boiled the liquid by placing pieces of rock that had become scorching hot from the sun into the sap. 由于当时没有储存设施，他们无法将树液保存多久，于是便把被太阳晒得滚烫的石块放入树液中，将其煮沸。⭐️【for any length of time = 持续一段时间，否定句中常指“保存多久”】⭐️【scorching hot = 滚烫的；灼热的】
They did this until it turned into sugar, and they were then able to use this to sweeten their food and drinks. 他们一直这样做，直到树液变成糖，随后便可以用它给食物和饮品增添甜味。⭐️【sweeten = 使变甜；给……加甜味】
Since that time, improvements have been made to the process, but it has changed very little overall. 从那时起，虽然生产工艺有所改进，但总体上变化很小。
So let’s look at the production of maple syrup today. 接下来，我们看看如今枫糖浆的生产过程。
Clearly, the maple forests are a valuable resource in many Canadian and North American communities. 显然，对于加拿大和北美许多社区而言，枫树林是一种宝贵的资源。
The trees have to be well looked after and they cannot be used to make syrup until the trunks reach a diameter of around 25 centimetres. 枫树必须得到妥善照料，而且只有当树干直径达到约二十五厘米时，才能用来生产枫糖浆。⭐️【diameter = 直径】
This can take anything up to 40 years. 这可能需要长达四十年的时间。⭐️【anything up to = 最多可达；可能长达】
As I’ve already mentioned, maple trees need the right conditions to grow and also to produce sap. 正如我已经提到的，枫树的生长和树液的产生都需要适宜的条件。
Why is this? 这是为什么呢？
Well, what happens is that during a cold night, the tree absorbs water from the soil, and that rises through the tree’s vascular system. 具体来说，在寒冷的夜晚，枫树从土壤中吸收水分，水分再通过树木的维管系统向上输送。⭐️【vascular system = 维管系统，植物输送水分和养分的组织系统】
But then in the warmer daytime, the change in temperature causes the water to be pushed back down to the bottom of the tree. 而到了较温暖的白天，温度的变化又使水分被向下推回树木底部。
This continual movement – up and down – leads to the formation of the sap needed for maple syrup production. 这种持续的上下流动，促成了生产枫糖浆所需树液的形成。
When the tree is ready, it can be tapped and this involves drilling a small hole into the trunk and inserting a tube into it that ends in a bucket. 当树木达到采集条件时，就可以采集树液：先在树干上钻一个小孔，再插入一根管子，管子的另一端通向桶中。⭐️【tap = 在树上开孔采集树液】⭐️【insert … into … = 将……插入……】
The trees can often take several taps, though the workers take care not to cause any damage to the healthy growth of the tree itself. 一棵树通常可以承受多个取液孔，不过工作人员会小心操作，避免损害树木本身的健康生长。⭐️【take = 承受；容许】⭐️【taps = 取液孔；取液口】
The sap that comes out of the trees consists of 98 percent water and 2 percent sugar and other nutrients. 从树中流出的树液，百分之九十八是水，其余百分之二是糖和其他营养物质。⭐️【consist of = 由……组成】
It has to be boiled so that much of that water evaporates, and this process has to take place immediately, using what are called evaporators. 树液必须立即用一种叫作蒸发器的设备煮沸，使其中大量水分蒸发。⭐️【evaporate = 蒸发】⭐️【evaporators = 蒸发器】
These are basically extremely large pans – the sap is poured into these, a fire is built and the pans are then heated until the sap boils. 这些设备基本上就是非常大的锅——先将树液倒进去，再生火加热，直到树液沸腾。⭐️【build a fire = 生火】
As it does this, the water evaporates, and the syrup begins to form. 随着树液沸腾，水分逐渐蒸发，糖浆开始形成。
The evaporation process creates large quantities of steam, and the sap becomes thicker and denser, and, at just the right moment, when the sap is thick enough to be called maple syrup, the worker removes it from the heat. 蒸发过程会产生大量水蒸气，树液也会变得越来越浓稠。当树液浓稠到足以称为枫糖浆时，工作人员就会把握好时机，将其移离热源。⭐️【remove … from the heat = 将……移离热源；停止加热】
After this process, something called ‘sugar sand’ has to be filtered out as this builds up during the boiling and gives the syrup a cloudy appearance and a slightly gritty taste. 完成这一步后，还必须过滤掉一种叫作“糖砂”的物质，因为它会在煮沸过程中不断积聚，使糖浆外观浑浊，口感略带砂砾感。⭐️【filter out = 过滤掉】⭐️【build up = 逐渐积聚】⭐️【cloudy = 浑浊的】⭐️【gritty = 有砂砾感的】
Once this has been done, the syrup is ready to be packaged so that it can be used for a whole variety of products. 完成过滤后，糖浆就可以包装起来，用于各种各样的产品。⭐️【a whole variety of = 各种各样的】
It takes 40 litres of sap to produce one litre of maple syrup so you can get an idea of how much is needed! 生产一升枫糖浆需要四十升树液，所以你可以想象所需的树液量有多大！⭐️【get an idea of = 大致了解；形成直观认识】
So that’s the basic process. 以上就是基本的生产流程。
