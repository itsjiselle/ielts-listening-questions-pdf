Good evening, everyone. 大家晚上好。
Let me start by welcoming you all to this talk and thanking you for taking the time to consider joining ACE voluntary organisation. 首先，欢迎大家参加这次讲座，也感谢各位抽出时间，考虑加入ACE志愿服务组织。
ACE offers support to people and services in the local area and we’re now looking for more volunteers to help us do this. ACE为当地居民和服务机构提供支持，目前我们正在招募更多志愿者，协助开展这些工作。
By the way, I hope you’re all comfortable – we have brought in extra seats so that no one has to stand, but it does mean that the people at the back of the room may be a bit squashed. 顺便说一下，希望大家坐得还舒服。我们增加了一些座位，让大家都不必站着，不过这也意味着坐在房间后排的人可能会有点拥挤。 ⭐ ️【squashed = 挤在一起的；空间局促的】 bring in 拿来，带来，不是借来，所以不能说明not enough
We’ll only be here for about half an hour so, hopefully, that’s OK. 讲座只持续大约半个小时，希望大家能够接受。
One of the first questions we’re often asked is how old you need to be to volunteer. 大家经常首先问到的一个问题是：多大年龄才能成为志愿者？
Well, you can be as young as 16 or you can be 60 or over; it all depends on what type of voluntary work you want to do. 最小可以是16岁，60岁或以上也可以，具体取决于你希望从事哪类志愿工作。
Other considerations, such as reliability, are crucial in voluntary work and age isn’t related to these, in our experience. 对志愿工作而言，是否可靠等其他因素至关重要，而根据我们的经验，这些与年龄无关。⭐️【reliability = 可靠性；值得信赖的程度】
Another question we get asked relates to training. 另一个经常被问到的问题与培训有关。
Well, there’s plenty of that and it’s all face-to-face. 我们会提供充足的培训，而且全部采用面对面的形式。
What’s more, training doesn’t end when you start working for us – it takes place before, during and after periods of work. 而且，培训不会在你开始为我们工作后就结束——在各阶段工作开始前、进行中和结束后，都会安排培训。⭐️【what’s more = 而且；此外（话语功能：递进补充）】
Often, it’s run by other experienced volunteers as managers tend to prefer to get on with other things. 培训通常由其他有经验的志愿者负责，因为管理人员往往更愿意去处理其他事务。⭐️【get on with = 继续做；着手处理】
Now, I would ask you to consider a couple of important issues before you decide to apply for voluntary work. 在决定申请志愿工作之前，我希望大家先考虑几个重要问题。
We don’t worry about why you want to be a volunteer – people have many different reasons that range from getting work experience to just doing something they’ve always wanted to do. 我们并不介意你为什么想成为志愿者。大家的理由各不相同，有人想积累工作经验，有人只是想做一件自己一直想做的事。
But it is critical that you have enough hours in the day for whatever role we agree is suitable for you – if being a volunteer becomes stressful then it’s best not to do it at all. 但至关重要的是，无论我们共同确定哪个岗位适合你，你都必须有足够的时间投入其中。如果做志愿者反而让你压力重重，那最好就不要做。
You may think that your income is important, but we don’t ask about that. 你可能认为自己的收入是一个重要因素，但我们不会询问这方面的情况。
It’s up to you to decide if you can work without earning money. 你是否能够从事没有报酬的工作，由你自己决定。
What we value is dedication. 我们看重的是投入与奉献精神。⭐️【dedication = 全心投入；奉献精神】
Some of our most loyal volunteers earn very little themselves but still give their full energy to the work they do with us. 我们一些最忠诚的志愿者，自身收入也很少，但仍然全身心地投入在这里承担的工作。
OK, so let’s take a look at some of the work areas that we need volunteers for and the sort of things that would help you in those. 好的，接下来看看我们在哪些工作领域需要志愿者，以及哪些能力有助于你胜任这些工作。
You may wish simply to help us raise money. 你可能只是想帮我们筹集资金。
If you have the creativity to come up with an imaginative or novel way of fundraising, we’d be delighted, as standing in the local streets or shops with a collection box can be rather boring! 如果你有创意，能想出富有想象力或新颖的筹款方式，我们会非常高兴，因为拿着募捐箱站在当地街头或商店里，可能相当枯燥！⭐️【novel = 新颖的】⭐️【fundraising = 筹款；募款】⭐️【collection box = 募捐箱】
One outdoor activity that we need volunteers for is litter collection and for this it’s useful if you can walk for long periods, sometimes uphill. 我们需要志愿者参与的一项户外活动是捡拾垃圾。如果你能长时间步行，而且有时还能走上坡路，就会很有帮助。
Some of our regular collectors are quite elderly, but very active and keen to protect the environment. 我们一些经常参加垃圾清理的志愿者年纪已经很大，但仍然十分活跃，也很热心于环境保护。
If you enjoy working with children, we have three vacancies for what are called ‘playmates’. 如果你喜欢和孩子相处，我们有三个叫作“玩伴”的志愿者岗位空缺。
These volunteers help children learn about staying healthy through a range of out-of-school activities. 这些志愿者通过各种校外活动，帮助孩子学习如何保持健康。
You don’t need to have children yourself, but it’s good if you know something about nutrition and can give clear instructions. 你自己不必有孩子，但如果你了解一些营养知识，并能清楚地说明活动要求，就会很有帮助。⭐️【nutrition = 营养；营养学】
If that doesn’t appeal to you, maybe you would be interested in helping out at our story club for disabled children, especially if you have done some acting. 如果这对你没有吸引力，也许你会有兴趣到我们面向残障儿童的故事俱乐部帮忙，尤其是如果你有过表演经验。
We put on three performances a year based on books they have read and we’re always looking for support with the theatrical side of this. 我们每年会根据孩子们读过的书举办三场演出，一直都需要有人协助戏剧表演方面的工作。⭐️【put on = 举办；上演】⭐️【theatrical = 戏剧的；舞台表演的】
The last area I’ll mention today is first aid. Volunteers who join this group can end up teaching others in vulnerable groups who may be at risk of injury. 今天我要介绍的最后一个领域是急救。加入这一小组的志愿者，之后可能会向容易受到伤害的弱势群体教授急救知识。⭐️【vulnerable groups = 弱势群体；易受伤害的群体】
Initially, though, your priority will be to take in a lot of information and not forget any important steps or details. 不过，刚开始时，你的首要任务是理解并掌握大量信息，记牢所有重要步骤和细节。⭐️【take in = 理解；吸收，尤指信息】
Right, so does anyone have any questions … 好的，大家有什么问题吗……
