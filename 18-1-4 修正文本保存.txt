For my presentation today I want to tell you about how groups of elephants have been moved and settled in new reserves. 在今天的演讲中，我想向大家介绍成群的大象如何被迁移并安置到新的保护区。⭐️【settle something in a new reserve = 将……安置到新的保护区】
This is known as translocation and has been carried out in Malawi in Africa in recent years. 这一过程被称为“迁地保护”，近年来已在非洲的马拉维实施。⭐️【translocation = 迁移安置；迁地保护】⭐️【carry out = 实施；开展】
The reason this is being done is because of overpopulation of elephants in some areas. 之所以采取这种做法，是因为某些地区的大象数量过多。⭐️【overpopulation = 数量过多；种群过度增长】
Overpopulation is a good problem to have and not one we tend to hear about very often. 种群数量过多算是一种“幸福的烦恼”，而且我们通常很少听说这种问题。⭐️【a good problem to have = 幸福的烦恼；由积极进展带来的问题】
In Malawi’s Majete National Park the elephant population had been wiped out by poachers, who killed the elephants for their ivory. 在马拉维的Majete国家公园，大象种群曾被偷猎者赶尽杀绝；这些人为了象牙而猎杀大象。⭐️【wipe out = 彻底消灭】⭐️【poacher = 偷猎者】⭐️【ivory = 象牙】
But in 2003, the park was restocked and effective law enforcement was introduced. 但在2003年，公园重新引入了大象，并开始实施有效的执法措施。⭐️【restock = 重新引入动物；补充种群】⭐️【law enforcement = 执法】
Since then, not a single elephant has been poached. 从那以后，没有一头大象遭到偷猎。⭐️【not a single = 一个也没有】
In this safe environment, the elephant population boomed. 在这一安全的环境中，大象数量迅速增长。⭐️【boom = 迅速增长】
Breeding went so well that there were more elephants than the park could support. 大象的繁殖情况非常理想，以至于其数量超过了公园所能承载的限度。⭐️【support a population = 承载一个种群】
This led to a number of problems. 这引发了一系列问题。
Firstly, there was more competition for food, which meant that some elephants were suffering from hunger. 首先，大象之间对食物的争夺加剧，这意味着部分大象正在忍饥挨饿。⭐️【competition for food = 对食物的争夺】⭐️【suffer from hunger = 遭受饥饿】
As there was a limit to the amount of food in the national park, some elephants began looking further afield. 由于国家公园内的食物数量有限，一些大象开始到更远的地方寻找食物。⭐️【look further afield = 到更远的地方寻找】
Elephants were routinely knocking down fences around the park, which then had to be repaired at a significant cost. 大象经常撞倒公园四周的围栏，而修复这些围栏又需要花费一大笔钱。⭐️【routinely = 经常地；惯常地】⭐️【knock down = 撞倒；推倒】⭐️【at a significant cost = 付出高昂的成本】
To solve this problem, the decision was made to move dozens of elephants from Majete National Park to Nkhotakota Wildlife Park, where there were no elephants. 为了解决这个问题，人们决定将数十头大象从Majete国家公园迁往当时没有大象的Nkhotakota野生动物公园。⭐️【dozens of = 数十个】⭐️【the decision was made to = 人们决定……】
But, obviously, attempting to move significant numbers of elephants to a new home 300 kilometres away is quite a challenge. 但显然，要把数量如此庞大的大象迁移到300公里以外的新家，是一项相当艰巨的任务。⭐️【significant numbers of = 数量众多的】⭐️【quite a challenge = 相当艰巨的挑战】
So how did this translocation process work in practice? 那么，这一迁移过程在实际操作中是如何进行的呢？⭐️【in practice = 在实际操作中】
Elephants were moved in groups of between eight and twenty, all belonging to one family. 大象以每组8至20头的规模进行迁移，同一组中的大象全部属于同一个家族。
Because relationships are very important to elephants, they all had to be moved at the same time. 由于家族关系对大象非常重要，同一家族的成员必须同时迁移。
A team of vets and park rangers flew over the park in helicopters and targeted a group, which were rounded up and directed to a designated open plain. 一队兽医和公园护林员乘坐直升机飞越公园，锁定一个象群，然后将它们聚集起来并引导至一片指定的开阔平原。⭐️【park ranger = 公园护林员】⭐️【round up = 将人或动物聚集起来】⭐️【designated = 指定的】
The vets then used darts to immobilise the elephants – this was a tricky manoeuvre, as they not only had to select the right dose of tranquiliser for different-sized elephants but they had to dart the elephants as they were running around. 随后，兽医使用麻醉镖使大象暂时无法行动——这是一项操作难度很高的任务，因为他们不仅要根据大象体形的不同选择合适剂量的镇静剂，还必须在大象四处奔跑时准确射中它们。⭐️【immobilise = 使无法移动】⭐️【a tricky manoeuvre = 难度很高的操作】⭐️【dose of tranquiliser = 镇静剂的剂量】
This also had to be done as quickly as possible so as to minimise the stress caused. 这项工作还必须尽快完成，以最大限度地减少给大象造成的压力。⭐️【so as to = 为了（话语功能：表示目的）】⭐️【minimise the stress caused = 尽量减轻造成的压力】
As soon as the elephants began to flop onto the ground, the team moved in to take care of them. 大象一开始瘫倒在地，团队便立即上前照料它们。⭐️【flop onto the ground = 无力地倒在地上】⭐️【move in = 迅速上前采取行动】
To avoid the risk of suffocation, the team had to make sure none of the elephants were lying on their chests because their lungs could be crushed in this position.So all the elephants had to be placed on their 【sides.】 为避免窒息风险，团队必须确保没有大象俯卧，因为这种姿势可能会使它们的肺部受到挤压。因此，所有大象都必须被调整为侧卧姿势。 ⭐ ️【suffocation = 窒息】⭐️【lie on one’s chest = 俯卧】 ️【be placed on one’s side = 被调整为侧卧姿势】
One person stayed with each elephant while they waited for the vets to do checks. 等待兽医进行检查时，每头大象身边都会留有一名工作人员照看。
It was very important to keep an eye on their breathing – if there were fewer than six breaths per minute, the elephant would need urgent medical attention. 密切留意它们的呼吸情况非常重要——如果每分钟呼吸少于六次，大象就需要接受紧急医疗救治。⭐️【keep an eye on = 密切留意】⭐️【urgent medical attention = 紧急医疗救治】
Collars were fitted to the matriarch in each group so their movements could be tracked in their new home. 工作人员会为每个象群的母象首领戴上项圈，以便追踪它们迁入新家后的活动。⭐️【matriarch = 母系群体的女性首领】⭐️【track one’s movements = 追踪……的活动轨迹】
Measurements were taken of each elephant’s tusks – elephants with large tusks would be at greater risk from poachers – and also of their feet. 工作人员还会测量每头大象的象牙和脚部——象牙较大的大象面临的偷猎风险更高。⭐️【tusk = 象牙】⭐️【be at greater risk from = 面临更大的……风险】
The elephants were then taken to a recovery area before being loaded onto trucks and transported to their new home. 随后，大象会被送往恢复区，再被装上卡车运往新家。⭐️【recovery area = 恢复区】⭐️【load something onto a truck = 将某物装上卡车】
The elephants translocated to Nkhotakota settled in very well and the project has generally been accepted to have been a huge success – and not just for the elephants.【Employment】 prospects have improved enormously, contributing to rising living standards for the whole community. 被迁移到Nkhotakota的大象很好地适应了新环境，这一项目也被普遍认为取得了巨大成功——而且受益的不只是大象。当地的就业前景得到了极大改善，从而推动了整个社区生活水平的提高。 ⭐ ️【settle in = 适应新环境】⭐️【be generally accepted to have been = 被普遍认为是……】️【employment prospects = 就业前景】⭐️【contribute to = 促成；推动】⭐️【living standards = 生活水平】
Poaching is no longer an issue, as former poachers are able to find more reliable sources of income.In fact, many of them volunteered to give up their 【weapons】, as they were no longer of any use to them. 偷猎已不再是一个问题，因为曾经的偷猎者现在能够找到更加稳定可靠的收入来源。事实上，他们当中的许多人主动交出了武器，因为这些武器对他们来说已经毫无用处。 ⭐ ️【no longer an issue = 不再是问题】⭐️【source of income = 收入来源】️【volunteer to do = 主动做某事】⭐️【give up one’s weapons = 交出／放弃武器】⭐️【be of use to someone = 对某人有用】
More than two dozen elephants have been born at Nkhotakota since relocation. 自迁移以来，已有二十多头大象在Nkhotakota出生。
With an area of more than 1,800 square kilometres, there’s plenty of space for the elephant population to continue to grow.&nbsp; Nkhotakota的面积超过1,800平方公里，有充足的空间供大象种群继续增长。
Their presence is also helping to rebalance Nkhotakota’s damaged ecosystem and providing a sustainable conservation model, which could be replicated in other parks. 大象的存在也有助于重新平衡Nkhotakota已经遭到破坏的生态系统，并提供一种可持续的自然保护模式；这一模式还可以在其他公园复制推广。⭐️【rebalance an ecosystem = 重新平衡生态系统】⭐️【sustainable conservation model = 可持续的自然保护模式】⭐️【be replicated = 被复制推广】
All this has been a big draw for tourism, which contributes five times more than the illegal wildlife trade to GDP, and this is mainly because of the elephants. 这一切都对游客产生了巨大的吸引力。旅游业对国内生产总值的贡献是非法野生动物交易的五倍，而这主要归功于大象。⭐️【a big draw for tourism = 对游客极具吸引力的事物】⭐️【illegal wildlife trade = 非法野生动物交易】
There’s also been a dramatic rise in interest … 人们对这里的兴趣也出现了大幅上升……⭐️【a dramatic rise in = ……的大幅上升】
