Good morning. 早上好。
Now, we’ve been asked to choose an aspect of European clothing or fashion and to talk about its development over time. 我们被要求选择欧洲服装或时尚的某个方面，介绍它随着时间的发展变化。
I decided to focus on a rather small area of clothing and that’s pockets. 我决定关注服装中一个相当细小的部分，那就是口袋。
I chose pockets for two reasons, really. 我选择口袋，其实有两个原因。
We all have them – in jeans, jackets, coats, for example – and even though we often carry bags or briefcases as well, nothing is quite as convenient as being able to pop your phone or credit card into your pocket. 我们的衣服都有口袋，例如牛仔裤、夹克和外套。尽管我们也经常携带包或公文包，但没有什么比随手把手机或信用卡放进口袋更方便了。⭐️【pop … into … = 随手将……放入……】
Yet, I suspect that, other than that, people don’t really think about pockets too much and they’re rather overlooked as a fashion item. 不过，我想除此之外，人们其实很少关注口袋，作为一种时尚元素，它们颇受忽视。⭐️【other than that = 除此之外】⭐️【overlook = 忽视；未予重视】
It’s certainly very interesting to go back in time and see how pockets developed for men and women. 回顾历史，看看男女服装上的口袋是如何发展的，确实十分有趣。
In the 18th century, fashions were quite different from the way they are now, and pockets were too. 十八世纪的时尚与如今大不相同，口袋也是如此。
If we think about male fashion first … that was the time when suits became popular. 如果先来看男装……那正是套装开始流行的时期。
Trousers were knee-length only and referred to as ‘breeches’, the waistcoats were short and the jackets were long, but all three garments were lined with material and pockets were sewn into this cloth by whichever tailor the customer used. 当时的裤子只到膝盖，被称为“及膝马裤”；马甲较短，外套较长，但这三件衣物都有衬里，顾客聘请的裁缝会将口袋缝进这层布料中。⭐️【breeches = 及膝马裤】⭐️【waistcoats = 马甲；西装背心】⭐️【be lined with = 内衬有……】
The wearer could then carry small objects such as pencils or coins on their person and reach them through a gap in the lining. 这样，穿着者就能随身携带铅笔或硬币等小物件，并通过衬里的开口取用。⭐️【on their person = 随身；在身上】⭐️【lining = 衬里；内衬】
Coat pockets became increasingly decorative on the outside for men who wanted to look stylish, but they were often larger but plainer if the wearer was someone with a profession who needed to carry medical instruments – a doctor or physician, for example. 对于想要穿着时髦的男性来说，外套口袋的外部装饰越来越丰富；但如果穿着者因职业需要携带医疗器械，例如医生，口袋通常就会更大、更朴素。⭐️【decorative = 装饰性的】⭐️【plain = 朴素的；无装饰的】⭐️【medical instruments = 医疗器械】
The development of women’s pockets was a little different. 女装口袋的发展则有所不同。
For one thing, they weren’t nearly as visible or as easy to reach as men’s. 首先，女装口袋远没有男装口袋那么显眼，也没有那么容易取用。⭐️【for one thing = 首先；其中一个原因是】⭐️【not nearly as … as … = 远不如……那么……】
In the 18th and 19th centuries, women carried numerous possessions on their person and some of these could be worth a lot of money. 在十八和十九世纪，女性会随身携带许多物品，其中一些可能相当值钱。
Women were more vulnerable to theft and wealthy women, in particular, worried constantly about pickpockets. 女性更容易遭遇盗窃，尤其是富裕女性，常常担心扒手。⭐️【be vulnerable to = 容易受到……的侵害】⭐️【pickpockets = 扒手】
So – what they did was to have a pair of pockets made that were tied together with string. 因此，她们会让人制作一对用细绳连在一起的口袋。⭐️【have something made = 请人制作某物】
The pockets were made of fabric, which might be recycled cloth if the wearer had little money or something more expensive, such as linen, sometimes featuring very delicate embroidery. 这些口袋由布料制成，如果穿着者经济拮据，可能会使用旧布；也有人使用亚麻等更昂贵的材料，有时上面还带有十分精细的刺绣。⭐️【linen = 亚麻布】⭐️【delicate embroidery = 精细的刺绣】⭐️【feature = 带有；以……为特色】
Women tied the pockets around their waist so that they hung beneath their clothes. 女性将口袋系在腰间，让它们垂挂在衣服下面。
Remember, skirts were long then and there was plenty of room to hide a whole range of small possessions between the layers of petticoats that were commonly worn. 别忘了，当时裙子很长，而且女性通常穿着多层衬裙，层与层之间有足够的空间藏放各种小物件。⭐️【petticoats = 衬裙】⭐️【a whole range of = 各种各样的】
They would have an opening in the folds of their skirts through which they could reach whatever they needed, like their perfume. 她们会在裙子的褶裥中留出开口，通过这个开口取用所需的物品，例如香水。⭐️【folds = 衣物的褶裥；褶皱】
Working women, of course, also needed to carry around items that they might use for whatever job or trade they were involved in, but their pairs of pockets still remained on the inside of their clothing, they just got bigger or longer – sometimes reaching down to their knees! 当然，劳动女性也需要随身携带从事工作或手艺时可能用到的物品，但她们成对的口袋仍放在衣服里面，只是变得更大或更长——有时甚至一直垂到膝盖！⭐️【trade = 行业；手艺】
So the tie-on pockets went well into the 19th century and only changed when fashion altered towards the end of that period. 因此，这种系挂式口袋一直沿用到十九世纪相当晚的时候，直到该世纪末时尚发生变化，它们才随之改变。⭐️【tie-on pockets = 系挂式口袋】⭐️【well into = 一直延续至某个时期较晚的阶段】
That’s when dresses became tighter and less bulky, and the pairs of pockets became very noticeable – they stood out too much and detracted from the woman’s image. 那时，连衣裙变得更加贴身，不再那么宽大，而成对的口袋就显得十分明显——它们过于突出，影响了女性的整体形象。⭐️【bulky = 体积大的；宽大厚重的】⭐️【stand out = 显眼；突出】⭐️【detract from = 有损于；减损】
Women who had been used to carrying around a range of personal possessions – and still wanted to – needed somewhere to carry these items about their person. 女性原本已经习惯随身携带各种个人物品，而且仍然希望如此，因此需要另找地方来随身装这些东西。⭐️【be used to doing = 习惯于做……】⭐️【about their person = 随身；在身上】
That was when small bags, or pouches as they were known, came into fashion and, of course, they inevitably led on to the handbag of more modern times, particularly when fashion removed pockets altogether. 正是在那时，小包——当时被称为“小袋”——开始流行，并自然而然地逐渐发展成了近现代的手提包，尤其是在时尚潮流使口袋完全从服装上消失之后。⭐️【pouches = 小袋；小包】⭐️【come into fashion = 开始流行】⭐️【lead on to = 进一步发展为；进而促成】
