Presenter: This evening we’re delighted to welcome Dan Beagle, who’s just written a book on looking for and finding food in the wild. 主持人：今晚，我们很高兴请到Dan Beagle，他刚写了一本关于在野外寻找食物的书。
He’s going to tell us everything we need to know about picking wild mushrooms. 他将向我们介绍采摘野生蘑菇需要了解的知识。
Dan: Thank you very much. Well, I need to start by talking about safety. Dan：非常感谢。首先，我需要谈谈安全问题。
You really need to know what you’re doing because some mushrooms are extremely poisonous. 你必须清楚自己在做什么，因为有些蘑菇有剧毒。
Having said that, once you know what to look for, it’s really worth doing for the amazing variety of mushrooms available – which you can’t get in the shops. 话虽如此，一旦你知道该寻找什么样的蘑菇，采摘就很值得尝试，因为野生蘑菇的种类丰富得令人惊叹，而且这些品种在商店里买不到。⭐️【having said that = 话虽如此（话语功能：让步转折）】
But of course, you have to be very careful and that’s why I always say you should never consume mushrooms picked by friends or neighbours – always remember that some poisonous mushrooms look very similar to edible ones and it’s easy for people to get confused. 不过，你当然必须非常谨慎，所以我总是说，绝对不要食用朋友或邻居采来的蘑菇。要始终记住，有些毒蘑菇与可食用蘑菇的外形非常相似，很容易混淆。⭐️【edible = 可食用的】
The other thing to avoid is mushrooms growing beside busy roads for obvious reasons. But nothing beats the taste of freshly picked mushrooms – don’t forget that the ones in the shops are often several days old and past their best. 还要避免采摘生长在繁忙道路旁的蘑菇，原因显而易见。不过，没有什么能比得上新鲜采摘的蘑菇的味道——别忘了，商店里的蘑菇往往已经存放了好几天，过了最佳食用期。⭐️【nothing beats = 没有什么比……更好】⭐️【past their best = 已过最佳状态／最佳食用期】
There are certain ideas about wild mushrooms that it’s important to be aware of. 关于野生蘑菇，有些说法需要大家特别留意。
Don’t listen to people who tell you that it’s only OK to eat mushrooms that are pale or dull – this is completely untrue. 如果有人告诉你，只有颜色浅淡或暗淡的蘑菇才能吃，不要相信——这种说法完全不正确。⭐️【dull = 颜色暗淡的；不鲜艳的】
Some edible mushrooms are bright red, for example. 例如，有些可食用蘑菇就是鲜红色的。
Personally, I prefer mushrooms cooked but it won’t do you any harm to eat them uncooked in salads – it’s not necessary to peel them. 就我个人而言，我更喜欢吃做熟的蘑菇，不过，把它们生着放进沙拉里吃也不会有什么害处，而且不必去皮。
Another thing you should remember is that you can’t tell if a mushroom is safe to eat by its smell – some of the most deadly mushrooms have no smell and taste quite nice, apparently. 还要记住，不能通过气味判断蘑菇是否可以安全食用——有些毒性最强、足以致命的蘑菇没有气味，据说味道还相当不错。⭐️【tell … by … = 根据……判断……】⭐️【deadly = 致命的】
Finally, just because deer or squirrels eat a particular mushroom doesn’t mean that you can. 最后，鹿或松鼠能吃某种蘑菇，并不意味着你也能吃。
Of course, mushroom picking is associated with the countryside but if you haven’t got a car, your local park can be a great place to start. 当然，人们通常会把采蘑菇与乡间联系起来，但如果你没有车，当地公园也是一个很好的起点。
There are usually a range of habitats where mushrooms grow, such as playing fields and wooded areas. 公园里通常有多种适合蘑菇生长的环境，比如运动场和林地区域。⭐️【wooded = 树木覆盖的；林木茂密的】
But you need to be there first thing in the morning, as there’s likely be a lot of competition – not just from people but wildlife too. 不过，你需要一大早就到那里，因为很可能会有许多竞争者——不仅有其他人，还有野生动物。⭐️【first thing in the morning = 一大早】
The deer often get the best mushrooms in my local park. 在我家附近的公园里，最好的蘑菇往往都被鹿先吃掉了。
If you’re a complete beginner, I wouldn’t recommend going alone or relying on photos in a book, even the one I’ve written! 如果你完全没有经验，我不建议你独自前往，也不建议你仅凭书中的照片来辨认，哪怕那本书是我写的！
There are some really good phone apps for identifying mushrooms, but you can’t always rely on getting a good signal in the middle of a wood. 有些手机应用在辨认蘑菇方面确实很好用，但在树林深处，你不能指望手机总能收到良好的信号。
If possible, you should go with a group led by an expert – you’ll stay safe and learn a lot that way. 如果可以，你应该参加由专家带领的采摘小组——这样既能保障安全，也能学到很多知识。
Conservation is a really important consideration and you must follow a few basic rules. 生态保护是一个非常重要的考虑因素，你必须遵守几条基本规则。
You should never pick all the mushrooms in one area – collect only enough for your own needs. 绝对不要把某一区域的蘑菇全部采光——只采够自己需要的数量。
Be very careful that you don’t trample on young mushrooms or other plants. 务必小心，不要踩踏幼小的蘑菇或其他植物。⭐️【trample on = 踩踏；践踏】
And make sure you don’t pick any mushrooms that are endangered and protected by law. 还要确保不采摘任何濒危且受法律保护的蘑菇。
There’s been a decline in some varieties of wild mushrooms in this part of the country. 在本国这一地区，一些野生蘑菇品种的数量已经有所减少。
Restaurants are becoming more interested in locally sourced food like wild mushrooms, but the biggest problem is that so many new houses have been built in this area in the last ten years. 餐馆对野生蘑菇这类本地食材越来越感兴趣，但最大的问题是，过去十年间，这一地区新建了大量住宅。 ⭐ ️【locally sourced = 从当地采购的；取自当地的】
And more water is being taken from rivers and reservoirs because of this, and mushroom habitats have been destroyed. 因此，人们从河流和水库中取用了更多的水，蘑菇的生长环境也遭到了破坏。⭐️【reservoir = 水库】
Anyway, a word of advice on storing mushrooms. 接下来，给大家一点储存蘑菇的建议。⭐️【a word of advice = 一点建议】
Collect them in a brown paper bag and as soon as you get home,&nbsp; 采摘时用牛皮纸袋装好，
put them in the fridge. They’ll be fine for a couple of days, but it’s best to cook them as soon as possible, 回家后立即放进冰箱。它们可以保存几天，但最好尽快烹饪.
after washing them really carefully first, of course. 当然，烹饪前要先仔细清洗。 ⭐ 
So everybody knows what a mushroom tastes like, right? 大家都知道蘑菇是什么味道，对吧？
Well, you’ll be surprised by the huge variety of wild mushrooms there are. 不过，野生蘑菇的种类之丰富，一定会让你感到惊讶。
Be adventurous! They’re great in so many dishes – stir fries, risottos, pasta. 大胆尝试吧！它们适合加入各种菜肴——炒菜、意式烩饭、意大利面都很不错。⭐️【adventurous = 敢于尝试新事物的】⭐️【risotto = 意式烩饭】
But just be aware that some people can react badly to certain varieties so it’s a good idea not to eat huge quantities to begin with. 不过要注意，有些人吃某些品种后可能会出现不良反应，因此刚开始时最好不要大量食用。⭐️【react badly to = 对……产生不良反应】
