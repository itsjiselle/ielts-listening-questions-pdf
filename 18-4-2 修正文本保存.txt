Good morning everyone, and welcome to the Museum of Farming Life. 大家早上好，欢迎来到农耕生活博物馆。
I understand it’s your first visit here, so I’d like to give you some background information about the museum and then explain a little about what you can see during your visit. 我了解到这是各位第一次来这里，所以我想先介绍一下博物馆的背景，再简单说明参观期间大家可以看到些什么。
So, where we’re standing at the moment is the entrance to a large building that was constructed in 1880 as the home of a local businessman, Alfred Palmer, of the Palmer biscuit factory. 我们现在站的位置，是一栋大型建筑的入口。这栋建筑建于1880年，原本是当地商人、Palmer饼干厂的Alfred Palmer的住宅。
It was later sold and became a hall of residence for students in 1911, and a museum in 1951. In 2005, a modern extension was built to accommodate the museum’s collections. 后来，这栋建筑被出售，1911年成为学生宿舍，1951年改为博物馆。2005年，又增建了一座现代化建筑，用于容纳博物馆的藏品。⭐️【hall of residence = 学生宿舍楼】⭐️【extension = 扩建部分；增建建筑】⭐️【accommodate = 容纳】
The museum’s owned by the university, and apart from two rooms that are our offices, the university uses the main part of the building. 博物馆归大学所有。除了两间用作我们办公室的房间外，这栋建筑的主要部分由大学使用。
You may see students going into the building for lessons, but it’s not open to museum visitors, I’m afraid. 大家可能会看到学生进入这栋建筑上课，不过很遗憾，这里不向博物馆游客开放。
It’s a shame because the interior architectural features are outstanding, especially the room that used to be the library. 这很可惜，因为建筑内部的设计特色十分出色，尤其是过去用作图书馆的那个房间。⭐️【interior architectural features = 建筑内部的设计特色】
Luckily, we’ve managed to keep entry to the museum free. 幸运的是，我们一直设法保持博物馆免费开放。
This includes access to all the galleries, outdoor areas and the rooms for special exhibitions. 免费参观范围包括所有展厅、户外区域以及特展展室。
We run activities for children and students, such as the museum club, for which there’s no charge. 我们为儿童和学生举办各种活动，比如博物馆俱乐部，这些活动也不收费。
We do have a donation box just over there so feel free to give whatever amount you consider appropriate. 我们在那边设有一个捐款箱，欢迎大家自愿捐款，金额随意。
We do have a cloakroom, if you’d like to leave your coats and bags somewhere. 如果大家想寄存外套和包，我们也设有衣帽寄存处。⭐️【cloakroom = 衣帽寄存处】
Unlike other museums, photography is allowed here, so you might like to keep your cameras with you. 与其他博物馆不同，这里允许拍照，因此大家不妨随身携带相机。
You might be more comfortable not carrying around heavy rucksacks, though keep your coats and jackets on as it’s quite cold in the museum garden today. 不背着沉重的背包四处走，大家可能会更舒服些。不过，请穿好外套和夹克，因为今天博物馆花园里比较冷。⭐️【rucksack = 双肩背包】
I’d like to tell you about the different areas of the museum. 接下来，我想介绍一下博物馆的不同区域。
Just inside, and outside the main gallery, we have an area called Four Seasons. 刚进入馆内、位于主展厅外的位置，有一个叫“四季”的区域。
Here you can watch a four-minute animation of a woodland scene. It was designed especially for the museum by a group of young people on a film studies course, and it’s beautiful. 在这里，大家可以观看一段长达四分钟、展现林地景色的动画。这是一群学习电影研究课程的年轻人专门为博物馆制作的，画面非常优美。
Children absolutely love it, but then, so do adults. 孩子们特别喜欢它，不过，大人们也同样喜欢。⭐️【but then = 不过；话说回来（话语功能：补充说明）】
The main gallery’s called Town and Country. 主展厅叫“城镇与乡村”。
It includes a photographic collection of prizewinning sheep and shepherds. Leaving Town and Country, you enter Farmhouse Kitchen, which is … well, self-explanatory. 这里展出了一组获奖绵羊和牧羊人的照片。离开“城镇与乡村”后，大家会进入“农舍厨房”，这个名字嘛……已经把展览内容说得很清楚了。⭐️【self-explanatory = 不言自明的；无需解释的】
Here we have the oldest collection of equipment for making butter and cheese in the country. 这里收藏着全国年代最久远的一批黄油和奶酪制作器具。
And this morning, a specialist cheesemaker will be giving demonstrations of how it’s produced. 今天上午，一位专业奶酪制作师将现场演示奶酪的制作过程。⭐️【give a demonstration = 作示范；进行演示】
You may even get to try some. 大家甚至可能有机会品尝一些。
After that, you can go in two directions. 之后，大家可以选择往两个方向走。
To the right is a staircase that takes you up to a landing from where you can look down on the galleries. 右边有一段楼梯，沿着楼梯上去会到达一个平台，从那里可以俯瞰各个展厅。⭐️【landing = 楼梯平台】
To the left is a room called A Year on the Farm. 左边是一个叫“农场的一年”的展室。
There’s lots of seating here as sometimes we use the room for school visits, so it’s a good place to stop for a rest. 这里座位很多，因为我们有时会用这个房间接待学校参观团，所以很适合停下来休息。
If you’re feeling competitive, you can take our memory test in which you answer questions about things you’ve seen in the museum. 如果大家想挑战一下，可以参加我们的记忆测试，回答一些与馆内所见展品有关的问题。⭐️【competitive = 好胜的；有竞争意识的】
The next area’s called Wagon Walk. This contains farm carts from nearly every part of the country. 下一个区域叫“农车长廊”，里面陈列着来自全国几乎各个地区的农用车。
It’s surprising how much regional variation there was. 当年各地农用车的差异之大，令人惊讶。⭐️【regional variation = 地区差异】
Beside the carts are display boards with information about each one. 每辆车旁边都有展板，介绍相应的信息。
The carts are old and fragile, so we ask you to keep your children close to you and ensure they don’t climb on the carts. 这些农用车年代久远，容易损坏，因此请大家让孩子待在自己身边，确保他们不要攀爬车辆。⭐️【fragile = 易损坏的；脆弱的】
From Wagon Walk, you can either make your way back to reception or go out into the garden – or even go back to take another look in the galleries. 从“农车长廊”出发，大家可以返回接待处，也可以走到花园里，或者回到展厅再参观一遍。⭐️【make your way = 前往；走向】
In the far corner of the garden is Bees are Magic, but we’re redeveloping this area so you can’t visit that at the moment. 花园远端的角落里是“神奇的蜜蜂”展区，不过我们正在改造这一区域，因此目前无法参观。
You can still buy our honey in the shop, though. 不过，大家仍然可以在商店里买到我们出产的蜂蜜。
Finally, there’s The Pond, which contains all kinds of interesting wildlife. 最后，还有“池塘”区域，那里生活着各种有趣的野生动物。
There are baby ducks that are only a few days old, as well as tiny frogs. 那里有刚出生几天的小鸭子，还有小小的青蛙。
The Pond isn’t deep and there’s a fence around it, so it’s perfectly safe for children. 池塘不深，周围也设有围栏，所以对孩子们来说十分安全。
