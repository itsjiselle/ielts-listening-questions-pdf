The person I’ve chosen to talk about is the French writer Victor Hugo – many people have heard of him because his novel, Les Misérables, which he wrote in 1862, is famous around the world. 我选择介绍的人物是法国作家维克多·雨果。许多人听说过他，是因为他于1862年创作的小说《悲惨世界》享誉全球。
It became a stage musical in the 1980s, and a film version was also released in 2012. 这部小说在二十世纪八十年代被改编成舞台音乐剧，电影版也于2012年上映。⭐️【a stage musical = 舞台音乐剧】⭐️【be released = 上映；发行】
So, some of us, I’m sure, have a pretty general idea of the plot, but we know much less about the author. 因此，我相信，我们有些人对故事情节大致有所了解，但对作者本人的了解却少得多。⭐️【have a general idea of = 对……有大致了解】
Today, I’m going to provide a little more insight into this talented man and I’m going to talk particularly about the home he had on the island of Guernsey in the British Channel Islands. 今天，我将带大家进一步了解这位才华横溢的人物，重点介绍他位于英国海峡群岛中根西岛上的住所。⭐️【provide insight into = 帮助深入了解……】
But first, his early career … as I’ve said, he was a writer, he was at the height of his career in Paris and he was very highly regarded by his colleagues. 不过，先来谈谈他早期的事业……正如我所说，他是一位作家，当时在巴黎正处于事业巅峰，深受同行推崇。⭐️【at the height of his career = 处于事业巅峰】⭐️【be highly regarded by = 深受……推崇】
As far as literature was concerned, he was the leading figure of the Romantic movement. 就文学而言，他是浪漫主义运动的领军人物。⭐️【as far as … was concerned = 就……而言】⭐️【the leading figure = 领军人物】
However, as well as being a literary genius, he also gave many speeches about issues like the level of poverty in his society. 然而，除了是一位文学天才，他还发表过许多演讲，讨论当时社会的贫困程度等问题。⭐️【a literary genius = 文学天才】
He felt very strongly about this and about other areas where change was needed, like education. 他对贫困问题，以及教育等其他需要改革的领域，都有着强烈的关切。⭐️【feel strongly about = 对……抱有强烈的看法或感情】
This kind of outspoken criticism was not well liked by the rulers of France and, eventually, the emperor – Napoleon III – told Victor Hugo to leave Paris and not return; in other words, he sent him into exile. 这种直言不讳的批评令法国统治者不满，最终，皇帝拿破仑三世命令雨果离开巴黎，不得返回；换句话说，他将雨果放逐了。⭐️【outspoken criticism = 直言不讳的批评】⭐️【send someone into exile = 将某人放逐】
So Victor Hugo was forced to reside in other parts of Europe. 因此，雨果被迫居住在欧洲其他地区。⭐️【reside = 居住；定居】
Guernsey was actually his third place of exile and he landed there in 1855. 根西岛实际上是他的第三处流亡地，他于1855年抵达那里。
He produced a lot while on Guernsey – including Les Misérables – and to do this, he had to spend a great deal of time in the home that he had there. 在根西岛期间，他创作了大量作品，包括《悲惨世界》；为此，他需要长时间待在当地的家中。⭐️【produce = 创作；产出作品】
This was a property that he bought using the money he’d made in France from the publication of a collection of his poetry. 他用自己在法国出版一本诗集所赚的钱，买下了这处房产。⭐️【a collection of his poetry = 他的一部诗集】
It was the only property he ever owned, and he was very proud of it. 这是他一生中唯一拥有过的房产，他对此十分自豪。
The property Victor Hugo bought on Guernsey was a large, five-storey house in the capital town of St Peter Port and he lived there for 15 years, returning to France in 1870 when Napoleon’s Empire collapsed. 雨果在根西岛购买的房产，是位于首府圣彼得港的一幢五层大宅。他在那里生活了十五年，直到1870年拿破仑的帝国垮台后才返回法国。⭐️【five-storey = 五层的】⭐️【collapse = 垮台；崩溃】
He decorated and furnished each level, or floor, of the house in unique and wonderful ways, and many people consider the inside of the house to be a ‘work of art’. 他以独特而精妙的方式装饰房屋的每一层，并布置家具，许多人将这座房屋的内部视为一件“艺术品”。⭐️【furnish = 为房屋配备家具】⭐️【a work of art = 一件艺术品】
Today it’s a museum that attracts 200,000 visitors a year. 如今，这里是一座博物馆，每年吸引二十万名游客。
He lived in the house with his family … and portraits of its members still hang in rooms on the ground floor, along with drawings that he did during his travels that he felt were important to him. 他与家人一起住在这座房子里……底层的房间中至今仍挂着家人的肖像，以及他在旅行中画下、并认为对自己具有重要意义的画作。⭐️【portraits = 肖像画】⭐️【the ground floor = 底层；一楼】
In other ground-floor rooms, there are huge tapestries that he would have designed and loved. 底层的其他房间里挂着巨幅挂毯，想必是他亲自设计、十分喜爱的作品。⭐️【tapestries = 挂毯；壁毯】⭐️【would have = 此处表示对过去情况的推测】
The walls are covered in dark wood panelling that Victor Hugo created himself using wooden furniture that he bought in the market. 墙面覆盖着深色木护墙板，是雨果利用从市场买来的木家具亲手制作的。⭐️【wood panelling = 木护墙板；木质镶板】
The items were relatively inexpensive, and he used them to create intricate carvings. 这些家具价格相对低廉，他利用它们制作了精细复杂的雕刻。⭐️【intricate carvings = 精细复杂的雕刻】
They gave an atmosphere on the lower level that was shadowy and rather solemn. 它们使房屋下层呈现出幽暗而颇为庄严的氛围。⭐️【shadowy = 阴暗的；光线幽暗的】⭐️【solemn = 庄严的；肃穆的】
On the next level of the house there are two impressive lounges, where he entertained his guests. 房屋上一层有两间令人印象深刻的会客厅，他在这里招待客人。⭐️【entertain guests = 招待客人】
One lounge has entirely red furnishings, such as sofas and wall coverings, and the other blue. 一间会客厅的陈设，包括沙发和墙面装饰，全部是红色的，另一间则全部是蓝色的。⭐️【furnishings = 家具及室内陈设】⭐️【wall coverings = 墙面装饰材料】
There’s a strong Chinese influence in these areas in things like the wallpaper pattern and the lamps – which he would have made himself by copying original versions. 这些空间中的壁纸图案和灯具等物品都带有浓厚的中国风格——这些灯具想必是他模仿原版亲手制作的。
His library, where he left many of his favourite books, forms the hallway to the third floor and was a comfortable area where he could relax and enjoy his afternoons. 他的藏书区里留有许多他喜爱的书籍，这里构成了通往第三层的过道，也是一个舒适的空间，让他可以放松身心，享受午后时光。⭐️【hallway = 过道；走廊】
And then, at the very top of the house, there’s a room called the Lookout – called that because it looks out over the harbour. 再往上，在房屋的最顶层，有一间名为“瞭望室”的房间，之所以这样命名，是因为从这里可以俯瞰港口。⭐️【lookout = 瞭望处；瞭望室】⭐️【look out over = 俯瞰；眺望】
In contrast to the rather dark lower levels, it’s full of light and was like a glass office where he would write until lunchtime – often at his desk. 与较为昏暗的下层相比，这里光线充足，像一间玻璃办公室，他常在这里伏案写作，一直写到午餐时间。⭐️【in contrast to = 与……形成对比】⭐️【would = 此处表示过去经常发生的行为】
So, Victor Hugo was a man of many talents, but he was also true to his values. 因此，雨果不仅多才多艺，也始终坚守自己的价值观。⭐️【be true to his values = 忠于、坚守自己的价值观】
While living in his house on Guernsey, he entertained many other famous writers, but he also invited a large group of local children from the deprived areas of the island to dinner once a week. 在根西岛居住期间，他招待过许多其他著名作家，但他也每周邀请一大群来自岛上贫困地区的当地孩子共进晚餐。⭐️【deprived areas = 贫困地区；生活条件匮乏的地区】
What’s more, he served them their food, which was an extraordinary gesture for the time period. 不仅如此，他还亲自为孩子们上菜，这在当时是一个非同寻常的举动。⭐️【gesture = 表达某种态度或心意的举动】
In 1927, the house was owned by his relatives, and they decided to donate it to the city of Paris.&nbsp; 在1927年，这座房子归他的亲属所有，他们决定将它捐赠给巴黎市。 ⭐ ️【donate … to … = 将……捐赠给……】
It has since been restored using photographs from the period and, as I mentioned earlier, is now a museum that is open to the public. 此后，人们依据当时的照片对它进行了修复；正如我之前提到的，它如今是一座向公众开放的博物馆。⭐️【restore = 修复；恢复原貌】
