For my presentation today, I’m going to talk about the Céide Fields in the northwest of Ireland, one of the largest Neolithic sites in the world. 今天的展示，我将介绍位于爱尔兰西北部的Céide Fields，它是世界上规模最大的新石器时代遗址之一。⭐️【Neolithic sites = 新石器时代遗址】
I recently visited this site and observed the work that is currently being done by a team of archaeologists there. 我最近参观了这处遗址，并观察了一支考古团队目前在那里开展的工作。
The site was first discovered in the 1930s by a local teacher, Patrick Caulfield. 这处遗址最早由当地教师Patrick Caulfield于二十世纪三十年代发现。
He noticed that when local people were digging in the bog, they were constantly hitting against what seemed to be rows of stones. 他注意到，当地人在泥炭沼泽中挖掘时，总会碰到一些看起来排列成行的石头。⭐️【bog = 泥炭沼泽】⭐️【hit against = 碰到；撞上】
He realised that these must be walls and that they must be thousands of years old for them to predate the bog which subsequently grew over them. 他意识到，这些一定是墙，而且既然它们早于后来覆盖其上的泥炭沼泽形成，就必定已有数千年历史。⭐️【predate = 早于……存在或发生】⭐️【subsequently = 随后；后来】
He wrote to the National Museum in Dublin to ask them to investigate, but no one took him seriously. 他写信给位于都柏林的国家博物馆，请他们展开调查，但没有人认真对待他的发现。⭐️【take someone seriously = 认真对待某人及其所说的话】
It wasn’t until 40 years later, when Patrick Caulfield’s son Seamus, who had become an archaeologist by then, began to explore further. 直到四十年后，Patrick Caulfield的儿子Seamus才开始进一步探索，而那时他已经成为一名考古学家。
He inserted iron probes into the bog to map the formation of the stones, a traditional method which local people had always used for finding fuel buried in the bog for thousands of years. 他将铁制探杆插入泥炭沼泽，以绘制石头的分布形态。这是一种传统方法，当地人一直用它来寻找埋藏在沼泽中已有数千年的燃料。⭐️【iron probes = 铁制探杆】⭐️【map = 绘制……的分布图】
Carbon dating later proved that the site was over 5,000 years old and was the largest Neolithic site in Ireland. 后来的碳年代测定证明，这处遗址已有五千多年历史，是爱尔兰最大的新石器时代遗址。⭐️【carbon dating = 碳年代测定】
Thanks to the bog which covers the area, the remains of the settlement at Céide Fields, which is over 5,000 years old, are extremely well-preserved. 多亏了覆盖这一地区的泥炭沼泽，Céide Fields这处拥有五千多年历史的聚落遗迹保存得极为完好。⭐️【the remains of the settlement = 聚落遗迹】⭐️【well-preserved = 保存完好的】
A bog is 90 percent water; its soil is so saturated that when the grasses and heathers that grow on its surface die, they don’t fully decay but accumulate in layers. 泥炭沼泽的百分之九十是水，土壤含水量极高，以至于生长在表面的草和石南植物死亡后，不会完全腐烂，而是层层堆积起来。⭐️【saturated = 饱含水分的；浸透的】⭐️【decay = 腐烂；腐朽】⭐️【accumulate in layers = 层层堆积】
Objects remain so well preserved in these conditions because of the acidity of the peat and the deficiency of oxygen. 在这种环境下，物品之所以能保存得如此完好，是因为泥炭具有酸性，而且缺乏氧气。⭐️【peat = 泥炭】⭐️【acidity = 酸性】⭐️【deficiency of oxygen = 氧气不足】
At least 175 days of rain a year are required for this to happen; this part of Ireland gets an average of 225 days. 要形成这种环境，每年至少需要一百七十五个雨天，而爱尔兰这一地区平均每年有二百二十五个雨天。
The Neolithic farmers at Céide would have enjoyed several centuries of relative peace and stability.&nbsp; Céide的新石器时代农民想必曾经历过几个世纪相对和平、稳定的生活。 ⭐ ️【would have = 此处表示对过去情况的推测】
Neolithic farmers generally lived in larger communities than their predecessors, with a number of houses built around a community building. 新石器时代农民居住的社区通常比前人更大，多座房屋围绕着一幢公共建筑而建。⭐️【predecessors = 前人；前代居民】⭐️【a community building = 社区公共建筑】
As they lived in permanent settlements, Neolithic farmers were able to build bigger houses. 由于居住在固定聚落中，新石器时代农民能够建造更大的房屋。⭐️【permanent settlements = 固定聚落；永久性定居点】
These weren’t round as people often assume, but rectangular with a small hole in the roof that allowed smoke to escape. 这些房屋并不像人们通常以为的那样是圆形的，而是长方形的，屋顶上有一个供烟排出的小孔。
This is one of many innovations and indicates that the Neolithic farmers were the first people to cook indoors. 这是当时众多创新之一，也表明新石器时代农民是最早在室内做饭的人。⭐️【innovations = 创新；新方法】
Another new technology that Neolithic settlers brought to Ireland was pottery. 新石器时代的定居者带到爱尔兰的另一项新技术是制陶。
Fragments of Neolithic pots have been found in Céide and elsewhere in Ireland. 在Céide及爱尔兰其他地区，人们都发现过新石器时代的陶器碎片。⭐️【fragments = 碎片；残片】
The pots were used for many things; as well as for storing food, pots were filled with a small amount of fat and when this was set alight, they served as lamps. 陶器有许多用途：除了储存食物，人们还会在其中放入少量油脂，点燃后就可以当作灯使用。⭐️【set alight = 点燃】⭐️【serve as = 用作；起到……的作用】
It’s thought that the Céide Fields were mainly used as paddocks for animals to graze in. 人们认为，Céide Fields主要被用作供牲畜吃草的围场。⭐️【paddocks = 牲畜围场】⭐️【graze = 吃草；放牧】
Evidence from the Céide Fields suggests that each plot of land was of a suitable size to sustain an extended family. 来自Céide Fields的证据表明，每块土地的面积都足以供养一个大家庭。⭐️【a plot of land = 一小块土地】⭐️【sustain = 维持；供养】⭐️【an extended family = 大家庭，包括父母子女以外的其他亲属】
They may have used a system of rotational grazing in order to prevent over-grazing and to allow for plant recovery and regrowth. 他们可能采用了轮牧制度，以防止过度放牧，让植物得到恢复并重新生长。⭐️【rotational grazing = 轮牧】⭐️【over-grazing = 过度放牧】⭐️【allow for = 为……提供条件或机会】
This must have been a year-round activity as no structures have been found which would have been used to shelter animals in the winter. 放牧一定是全年进行的，因为人们没有发现任何可能用于在冬季为牲畜提供庇护的建筑。⭐️【a year-round activity = 全年进行的活动】
However, archaeologists believe that this way of life at Céide ceased abruptly. 然而，考古学家认为，Céide的这种生活方式突然终止了。⭐️【cease abruptly = 突然停止】
Why was this? 这是为什么呢？
Well, several factors may have contributed to the changing circumstances. 有几个因素可能促成了这种情况的变化。⭐️【contribute to = 促成；是……的原因之一】
The soil would have become less productive and led to the abandonment of farming. 土壤可能变得不再那么肥沃，导致人们放弃耕作。⭐️【productive = 土地肥沃的；高产的】⭐️【the abandonment of farming = 放弃耕作】
The crop rotationsystem was partly responsible for this as it would have been very intensive and was not sustainable. 作物轮作制度对此负有部分责任，因为这种耕作方式可能强度很高，无法长期持续。⭐️【crop rotation = 作物轮作】⭐️【be partly responsible for = 是……的部分原因】⭐️【intensive = 高强度的；集约的】
But there were also climatic pressures too. 不过，当时也存在气候方面的压力。⭐️【climatic pressures = 气候带来的压力】
The farmers at Céide would have enjoyed a relatively dry period, but this began to change and the conditions became wetter as there was a lot more rain.&nbsp; Céide的农民想必曾经历过一段相对干燥的时期，但后来情况开始变化，降雨明显增多，环境也变得更加潮湿。
It was these conditions that encouraged the bog to form over the area which survives today. 正是这些条件促使该地区形成了延续至今的泥炭沼泽。
