I never really planned to be a lifeboat volunteer when I came to live in Northsea. 刚搬到Northsea生活时，我其实从没打算成为一名救生艇志愿者。⭐️【lifeboat = 救生艇，尤指海上救援用艇】
I’d been  working in London as a website designer, but although that was interesting, I didn’t like city life. 我之前一直在伦敦做网站设计师，虽然工作很有意思，但我不喜欢城市生活。
I’d been really keen on boats as a teenager, and I thought if I went to live by the sea, I might be able to pursue that interest a bit more in my free time. 我十几岁时就对船很感兴趣，所以我想，如果搬到海边生活，也许就能在空闲时间里更多地发展这项爱好。⭐️【pursue an interest = 发展兴趣；投入某项爱好】
Then I found that the Lifeboat Institution was looking for volunteers, so I decided to apply. 后来，我发现救生艇机构正在招募志愿者，于是决定申请。
The Lifeboat Institution building here in Northsea’s hard to miss, it’s one of the largest in the country.&nbsp; Northsea的救生艇机构大楼很显眼，是全国规模最大的同类建筑之一。 ⭐ ️【hard to miss = 很显眼的；很容易找到的】
It was built 15 years ago with funds provided by a generous member of the public who’d lived here all her life. 这栋大楼建于十五年前，资金来自一位慷慨的女士，她一辈子都住在这里。
As the Lifeboat Institution is a charity that relies on that kind of donation, rather than funding provided by the government, that was a huge help to us. 救生艇机构是一家依靠这类捐款、而非政府拨款运作的慈善机构，因此这笔资助对我们帮助极大。
When I applied, I had to have a health assessment. The doctors were particularly interested in my vision. 申请时，我必须接受健康评估。医生尤其关注我的视力。
I used to be short-sighted, so I’d had to wear glasses, but I’d had laser eye surgery two years earlier so that was OK. 我以前近视，需要戴眼镜，但两年前已经做过激光视力矫正手术，所以这方面没有问题。⭐️【short-sighted = 近视的】
They gave me tests for colour blindness and they thought I might have a problem there, but it turned out I was OK. 他们给我做了色盲测试，一度认为我在这方面可能有问题，但最终确认没有问题。⭐️【colour blindness = 色盲】
When the coastguard gets an alert, all the volunteers are contacted and rush to the lifeboat station. 海岸警卫队接到警报后，会通知所有志愿者，大家随即赶往救生艇站。⭐️【coastguard = 海岸警卫队；海岸救援机构】
Our target’s to get there in five minutes, then we try to get the boat off the dock and out to sea in another six to eight minutes. 我们的目标是在五分钟内到达，然后争取再用六到八分钟让救生艇驶离码头、出海。
Our team’s proud that we usually achieve that – the average time across the country’s eight and a half minutes. 我们团队很自豪，因为通常都能达到这个目标——而全国平均出艇时间是八分半钟。
I’ve recently qualified as what’s called a ‘helmsman’, which means I have the ultimate responsibility for the lifeboat. 我最近取得了所谓的“舵手”资格，这意味着我要对救生艇承担最终责任。⭐️【helmsman = 舵手；掌舵者】⭐️【ultimate responsibility = 最终责任】
I have to check that the equipment we use is in working order – the crew have special life jackets that can support up to four people in the water. 我必须检查所用设备是否运转正常——艇员配备特殊救生衣，每件最多可以在水中支撑四个人。⭐️【in working order = 运转正常；处于可用状态】
And it’s ultimately my decision whether it’s safe to launch the boat. 是否能够安全出艇，最终也由我决定。⭐️【launch = 使船下水；出艇】
But it’s very rare not to launch it, even in the worst weather. 不过，即使天气极其恶劣，我们也很少不出艇。
As well as going out on the lifeboat, my work involves other things too. 除了随救生艇出海，我的工作还包括其他事务。
A lot of people underestimate how quickly conditions can change at sea, so I speak to youth groups and sailing clubs in the area about the sorts of problems that sailors and swimmers can have if the weather suddenly gets bad. 很多人低估了海上环境变化的速度，因此我会到当地的青年团体和帆船俱乐部，向他们介绍天气突然恶化时，航海者和游泳者可能遇到哪些问题。⭐️【underestimate = 低估】
We also have a lot of volunteers who organise activities to raise money for us, and we couldn’t manage without them. 我们还有很多志愿者负责组织筹款活动，没有他们，我们的工作就无法维持。
The training we get is a continuous process, focusing on technical competence and safe handling techniques, and it’s given me the confidence to deal with extreme situations without panicking. 我们接受的培训是持续进行的，重点在于专业技术能力和安全操作方法。这让我有信心在面对极端情况时保持镇定。⭐️【technical competence = 专业技术能力】⭐️【handling techniques = 操作方法；操控技巧】
I was glad I’d done a first aid course before I started, as that’s a big help with the casualty care activities we do. 我很庆幸自己在加入之前上过急救课，因为这对我们开展伤员救护工作很有帮助。⭐️【casualty care = 伤员救护】
We’ve done a lot on how to deal with ropes and tie knots –that’s an essential skill. 我们接受了大量有关绳索操作和打结的训练——这是一项必备技能。⭐️【tie knots = 打绳结】
After a year, I did a one-week residential course, led by specialists. 加入一年后，我参加了一门为期一周、由专家授课的住校培训课程。⭐️【residential course = 住宿式培训课程】
They had a wave-tank where they could create extreme weather conditions – so we could get experience at what to do if the boat turned over in a storm at night, for example. 那里有一个造浪水池，可以模拟极端天气条件，让我们积累应对经验，例如夜间暴风雨中船只倾覆时应该怎么办。⭐️【wave-tank = 造浪水池】⭐️【turn over = 翻覆；倾覆】
Since I started, I’ve had to deal with a range of emergency situations. But the work’s hugely motivating. 自从加入以来，我已经处理过各种紧急情况。不过，这份工作也给了我极大的动力。
It’s not just about saving lives – I’ve learned a lot about the technology involved. 这份工作不仅仅是拯救生命——我还学到了很多相关技术知识。
My background in IT’s been useful here, and I can use my expertise to help other volunteers. 我的信息技术背景在这里派上了用场，我可以利用自己的专业知识帮助其他志愿者。
They’re a great group – we’re like a family really, which helps when you’re dragging yourself out of bed on a cold stormy night. 他们是一群很棒的人——我们真的像一家人，而这种情谊能支撑你在寒冷的暴风雨之夜，强撑着从床上爬起来。⭐️【drag yourself out of bed = 勉强从床上爬起来】
But actually, it’s the colder months that can be the most rewarding time. That’s when the incidents tend to be more serious, and you realise that you can make a huge difference to the outcome. 但实际上，寒冷的几个月反而可能是最有成就感的时候。因为这时发生的事故往往更加严重，你会意识到，自己的行动能够极大地改变最终结果。⭐️【rewarding = 带来成就感的；令人感到值得的】⭐️【make a difference to = 对……产生影响；改变……】
So if any of you listeners are interested. . . 所以，如果听众朋友们有人感兴趣的话……
