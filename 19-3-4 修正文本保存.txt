In today’s lecture, I’m going to be talking about microplastics. 今天的讲座，我将讨论微塑料。⭐️【microplastics = 微塑料】
Microplastics are tiny pieces of plastic smaller than five millimetres in size. 微塑料是尺寸小于五毫米的微小塑料碎片。
Recently there’s been a greater awareness that there are large quantities of plastic waste – big and small – in the environment. 最近，人们越来越意识到，环境中存在大量大小不一的塑料垃圾。
The amount of plastic waste in the oceans has received widespread attention, but far less is known about the effects of microplastics in freshwater and particularly in soil. 海洋中的塑料垃圾数量已受到广泛关注，但人们对微塑料在淡水中，尤其是在土壤中的影响，了解要少得多。⭐️【receive widespread attention = 受到广泛关注】⭐️【freshwater = 淡水】
Microplastics can enter the environment via a number of different sources. 微塑料可以通过多种不同来源进入环境。
Threads and microfibres detach from synthetic clothing every time they’re put in a washing machine, and these find their way into the water system. 合成纤维衣物每次放进洗衣机清洗时，都会脱落线头和微纤维，而这些物质最终会进入水系统。⭐️【detach from = 从……脱落；与……分离】⭐️【synthetic clothing = 合成纤维衣物】⭐️【find their way into = 最终进入；辗转进入】
Other sources include big pieces of plastic waste that are already in the environment, and these break down into microscopic particles over a period of time. 其他来源包括环境中已有的大块塑料垃圾，它们会随着时间推移，分解成极其微小的颗粒。⭐️【break down into = 分解成】⭐️【microscopic particles = 微小颗粒】
On a larger scale, factory waste is another route, as are tyres which wear down as cars, lorries and so on travel along road surfaces. 从更大规模来看，工厂废弃物是另一个来源，轮胎也是如此：汽车、卡车等车辆在路面行驶时，轮胎会逐渐磨损。⭐️【on a larger scale = 从更大规模来看】⭐️【wear down = 逐渐磨损】
We already understand some of the impacts of microplastics from studies involving fish and other animals. 通过对鱼类及其他动物的研究，我们已经了解了微塑料造成的部分影响。
There is evidence that microplastics harm small creatures in a variety of ways, such as by damaging their mouths, or by impairing their ability to feed, for example when microplastics get lodged in their digestive system. 有证据表明，微塑料会以多种方式伤害小型生物，例如损伤它们的口部，或削弱它们的摄食能力，比如微塑料卡在其消化系统中时，就会产生这种影响。⭐️【impair = 损害；削弱】⭐️【get lodged in = 卡在；滞留在】⭐️【digestive system = 消化系统】
Surprisingly perhaps, it is likely that humans consume microplastics, as these have been detected in a wide range of food and drink products, including bottled water, as well as in water that comes direct from the tap. 也许令人惊讶的是，人类很可能也在摄入微塑料，因为人们已在包括瓶装水在内的多种食品和饮品，以及直接从水龙头流出的自来水中检测到了它们。⭐️【detect = 检测出；发现】
What’s more, salt and many kinds of seafood have also been found to contain microplastics. 此外，人们还发现，盐和许多种海鲜中也含有微塑料。
However, it’s important to underline that there is not yet conclusive proof that microplastics cause significant harm to people. 然而，需要强调的是，目前尚无确凿证据证明微塑料会对人体造成重大伤害。⭐️【underline = 强调】⭐️【conclusive proof = 确凿证据】
In many countries, including here in the UK, there is legislation which prevents manufacturers from adding plastic microbeads to shower gels, facial cleansers and toothpaste. 包括英国在内的许多国家都制定了法律，禁止制造商在沐浴露、洁面产品和牙膏中添加塑料微珠。⭐️【legislation = 法律；法规】⭐️【plastic microbeads = 塑料微珠】
It is very difficult to accurately estimate the total amount of microplastic particles in the soil as they can be hard to detect, but we do know they are carried in the air and deposited in the soil by rain. 由于微塑料颗粒可能很难检测，要准确估算土壤中的微塑料总量十分困难；但我们确实知道，它们会随空气传播，并通过降雨沉降到土壤中。⭐️【be deposited in = 沉积于；沉降到】
What’s more, many of the fertilisers used by both farmers and gardeners contain microplastics. 此外，农民和园艺种植者使用的许多肥料中都含有微塑料。
A team from the Anglia Ruskin University in Cambridge has carried out a study of the effects of microplastics on the digestive tracts of earthworms. 剑桥的安格利亚鲁斯金大学的一支团队，研究了微塑料对蚯蚓消化道的影响。⭐️【digestive tracts = 消化道】⭐️【earthworms = 蚯蚓】
These worms, which live in topsoil, are an essential component of our agricultural system. 这些生活在表层土壤中的蚯蚓，是农业系统不可或缺的组成部分。⭐️【topsoil = 表层土壤】⭐️【an essential component of = ……不可或缺的组成部分】
By feeding on soil, they mix nutrients into it, thereby making it more fertile. 它们通过摄食土壤，将养分混入其中，从而使土壤更加肥沃。⭐️【feed on = 以……为食】⭐️【thereby = 从而，连接行为及其结果】⭐️【fertile = 肥沃的】
The researchers set out to discover whether the introduction of microplastics into the soil– and the subsequent ingestion of these by earthworms – would impact soil quality and ultimately inhibit plant growth. 研究人员着手探究：微塑料进入土壤，随后被蚯蚓摄入，是否会影响土壤质量，并最终抑制植物生长。⭐️【set out to = 开始着手做；力求】⭐️【ingestion = 摄入；吞食】⭐️【inhibit plant growth = 抑制植物生长】
The short answer was, yes, it did. 简单来说，答案是肯定的，确实会。
After placing three different types of microplastic particles into the soil, they planted perennial rye grass. 研究人员将三种不同类型的微塑料颗粒放入土壤后，种植了多年生黑麦草。⭐️【perennial rye grass = 多年生黑麦草】
The particles of microplastic, which included biodegradable PLA and conventional high-density polyethylene, or HDPE, were then ingested by the earthworms in the soil. 随后，土壤中的蚯蚓摄入了这些微塑料颗粒，其中包括可生物降解的聚乳酸，也就是PLA，以及常规的高密度聚乙烯，也就是HDPE。⭐️【biodegradable = 可生物降解的】⭐️【high-density polyethylene = 高密度聚乙烯】
The result was that the worms lost weight rapidly. 结果，蚯蚓的体重迅速下降。
What’s more, a lower percentage than normal of the rye grass seeds germinated, and the researchers concluded that this was a direct result of the earthworms being unable to fulfil their normal role in making soil more fertile. 此外，黑麦草种子的发芽率低于正常水平。研究人员得出结论，这是因为蚯蚓无法正常发挥使土壤更肥沃的作用，直接导致了这一结果。⭐️【germinate = 发芽】⭐️【fulfil their normal role = 发挥其正常作用】
The team also discovered that there was an increase in the amount of acid found in the soil, and this was attributed mainly to the microplastic particles from conventional HDPE plastic. 团队还发现，土壤中的酸含量有所增加，而这主要被归因于常规HDPE塑料产生的微塑料颗粒。⭐️【be attributed to = 被归因于】
The conclusions of the study make for very interesting reading – I’ve included the reference in the notes to give you at the end of this session. 这项研究的结论读起来非常有意思——我已将文献出处列入本次课结束时发给大家的笔记中。⭐️【make for interesting reading = 读起来很有意思；值得一读】⭐️【reference = 文献出处；参考资料信息】
To summarise, the authors proposed the idea that we need to regard soil as we would regard any other process in nature. 总结来说，作者提出，我们需要像看待自然界中其他过程一样来看待土壤。⭐️【propose the idea that = 提出……这一观点】
This means we should accept the implications of soil being dependent on decaying and dead matter constantly being passed through the bodies of earthworms. 这意味着，我们应当认识到，土壤依赖腐烂和死亡的有机物不断经过蚯蚓身体处理，而这种依赖关系具有重要影响。⭐️【implications = 可能产生的影响；意味着的后果】⭐️【decaying and dead matter = 腐烂和死亡的有机物】
That is, when soil becomes impoverished by the presence of microplastics, not only ecosystems but also the whole of society are negatively impacted. 也就是说，当微塑料的存在使土壤变得贫瘠时，不仅生态系统，整个社会都会受到负面影响。⭐️【impoverished = 贫瘠的；养分匮乏的】
