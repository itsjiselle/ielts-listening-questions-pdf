Tree planting now dominates political and popular agendas and is often presented as an easy answer to the climate crisis, as well as a way for business corporations to offset their carbon emissions. 如今，植树已成为政治和公众议程中的重要议题，常被视为应对气候危机的简单办法，也是企业抵消碳排放的一种方式。⭐️【dominate agendas = 在议程中占据主导地位】⭐️【offset their carbon emissions = 抵消其碳排放】
But unfortunately, tree planting isn’t as straightforward as some people think. 但遗憾的是，植树并不像一些人想象的那么简单。⭐️【straightforward = 简单明了的；不复杂的】
When the wrong trees are planted in the wrong place, it can do considerably more damage than good, failing to help either people or the environment. 如果在不合适的地方种下不合适的树，造成的损害可能远大于益处，既无法帮助人类，也无法改善环境。⭐️【do more damage than good = 弊大于利；造成的损害大于益处】
Reforestation projects are currently being undertaken on a huge scale in many countries and it’s crucial that the right trees are selected. 目前，许多国家正在大规模开展森林恢复项目，选择合适的树种至关重要。⭐️【reforestation = 重新造林；森林恢复】⭐️【on a huge scale = 大规模地】
A mix of species should always be planted, typical of the local natural forest ecosystem and including rare and endangered species in order to create a rich ecosystem. 种植时应始终搭配多种树种，以当地天然森林生态系统中的典型物种为主，并包括珍稀和濒危物种，从而创造丰富的生态系统。⭐️【a mix of species = 多种物种的组合】⭐️【rare and endangered species = 珍稀和濒危物种】
It’s important to avoid non-native species that could become invasive. 避免种植可能成为入侵物种的外来物种十分重要。⭐️【non-native species = 外来物种】⭐️【invasive = 具有入侵性的】
Invasive species are a significant contributor to the current global biodiversity crisis and are often in competition with native species and may threaten their long-term survival. 入侵物种是造成当前全球生物多样性危机的重要因素，它们往往与本土物种竞争，并可能威胁本土物种的长期生存。⭐️【a significant contributor to = 造成……的重要因素】⭐️【be in competition with = 与……竞争】
Restoring biodiversity that will maximise carbon capture is key when reforesting an area, but ideally any reforestation project should have several goals. 在一个地区恢复森林时，关键是恢复能够最大限度促进碳捕获的生物多样性；不过，理想情况下，任何森林恢复项目都应设定多个目标。⭐️【maximise carbon capture = 最大限度地提高碳捕获量】
These could include selecting trees that can contribute to wildlife conservation, improve the availability of food for the local community and maintain the stability of soil systems. 这些目标可以包括选择有助于保护野生动植物、增加当地社区食物供应，以及维持土壤系统稳定性的树种。⭐️【wildlife conservation = 野生动植物保护】⭐️【the availability of food = 食物的可获得性；食物供应】
Meeting as many of these goals as possible, whilst doing no harm to local communities, native ecosystems and vulnerable species, is the sign of a highly successful tree-planting scheme. 在不损害当地社区、本土生态系统和脆弱物种的前提下，尽可能多地实现这些目标，才是一个高度成功的植树项目的标志。⭐️【meet goals = 实现目标】⭐️【vulnerable species = 易受威胁的物种；脆弱物种】
To ensure the survival and resilience of a planted forest, it’s vital to use tree seeds with appropriate levels of genetic diversity: the amount of genetic variation found within a species essential for their survival. 为了确保人工种植的森林能够存活并具有韧性，必须使用具有适当遗传多样性的树种种子。遗传多样性是指一个物种内部遗传变异的丰富程度，对物种生存至关重要。⭐️【resilience = 韧性；抵御干扰并恢复的能力】⭐️【genetic diversity = 遗传多样性】⭐️【genetic variation = 遗传变异】
Using seeds with low genetic diversity generally lowers the resilience of restored forests, which can make them vulnerable to disease and unable to adapt to climate change. 使用遗传多样性较低的种子，通常会降低恢复后森林的韧性，使其更容易受到疾病侵袭，也无法适应气候变化。⭐️【be vulnerable to disease = 容易受到疾病侵袭】
Choosing the right location for reforestation projects is as important as choosing the right trees. 为森林恢复项目选择合适的地点，与选择合适的树种同样重要。
Ultimately, the best area for planting trees would be in formerly forested areas that are in poor condition. 归根结底，最适合植树的地方，是那些过去曾有森林、如今状况不佳的地区。⭐️【formerly forested areas = 过去曾被森林覆盖的地区】
It’s better to avoid non-forested landscapes such as natural grasslands, savannas or wetlands as these ecosystems already contribute greatly to capturing carbon. 最好避开天然草地、稀树草原或湿地等非森林地貌，因为这些生态系统本身已经在碳捕获方面发挥着重要作用。⭐️【savannas = 稀树草原】⭐️【wetlands = 湿地】
It would also be advantageous to choose an area where trees could provide other benefits, such as recreational spaces. 选择树木还能带来其他益处的地区，例如能够提供休闲空间的地方，也会更有利。⭐️【recreational spaces = 休闲活动空间】
Reforesting areas which are currently exploited for agriculture should be avoided as this often leads to other areas being deforested. 应避免在目前用于农业生产的地区恢复森林，因为这往往会导致其他地区的森林被砍伐。⭐️【be exploited for agriculture = 被用于农业生产】⭐️【be deforested = 森林被砍伐清除】
Large-scale reforestation projects require careful planning. 大规模森林恢复项目需要周密规划。
Making the right decisions about where to plant trees depends on having the right information. 要正确决定在哪里植树，就必须掌握恰当的信息。
Having detailed and up-to-date maps identifying high-priority areas for intervention is essential. 必须具备详细且最新的地图，明确标出需要优先采取干预措施的区域。⭐️【up-to-date = 最新的】⭐️【high-priority areas for intervention = 需要优先干预的区域】
Drone technology is a useful tool in helping to prioritise and monitor areas of degraded forest for restoration. 无人机技术是一种实用工具，可以帮助确定退化森林的修复优先次序，并监测这些区域。⭐️【prioritise = 确定优先次序】⭐️【degraded forest = 退化的森林】
In Brazil, it’s being used to identify and quantify how parts of the Amazon are being devastated by human activities such as rearing cattle and illegal logging. 在巴西，无人机技术正被用来识别并量化亚马孙部分地区因养牛和非法伐木等人类活动而遭受严重破坏的程度。⭐️【quantify = 量化】⭐️【devastate = 严重破坏】⭐️【rear cattle = 饲养牛】⭐️【illegal logging = 非法伐木】
A good example of where the right trees were picked to achieve a restored forest is in NLampang Province in Northern Thailand. 泰国北部的南邦府提供了一个很好的案例，展示了如何通过选择合适的树种来恢复森林。
A previously forested site which had been degraded through mining was reforested by a cement company together with Chiang Mai University. 一家水泥公司与清迈大学合作，在一处过去曾有森林、后来因采矿而退化的土地上重新造林。⭐️【be degraded through mining = 因采矿而退化】
After spreading 60 cm of topsoil, they planted 14 different native tree species which included several species of fig. 他们先铺上六十厘米厚的表层土壤，然后种植了十四种不同的本土树种，其中包括几种榕属树木。⭐️【topsoil = 表层土壤】
Figs are a keystone species because of the critical role they play in maintaining wildlife populations. 榕属树木是关键物种，因为它们在维持野生动物种群方面发挥着至关重要的作用。⭐️【keystone species = 关键物种】⭐️【play a critical role in = 在……中发挥关键作用】
They are central to tropical reforestation projects as they accelerate the speed of the recovery process by attracting animals and birds which act as natural seed dispersers. 它们在热带森林恢复项目中十分重要，因为它们能吸引动物和鸟类充当天然的种子传播者，从而加快恢复过程。⭐️【be central to = 对……至关重要】⭐️【seed dispersers = 种子传播者】
This helps to promote diversity through the healthy regrowth of a wide range of plant species. 这有助于多种植物健康地重新生长，从而促进生物多样性。
Unlike the majority of fruit trees, figs bear fruit all year round, providing a reliable food source for many species. 与大多数果树不同，榕属树木全年结果，为许多物种提供可靠的食物来源。⭐️【bear fruit = 结果实】
At this site, for example, after only three rainy seasons, monkeys started visiting to eat the fig fruits, naturally dispersing seeds through defecation. 例如，在这处地点，仅仅经过三个雨季，猴子就开始前来食用榕果，并通过排便自然地传播种子。⭐️【defecation = 排便】
Reforestation projects should always aim to make sure that local communities are consulted and involved in the decision-making process. 森林恢复项目应始终确保征询当地社区的意见，并让他们参与决策过程。⭐️【consult = 征询……的意见】⭐️【the decision-making process = 决策过程】
The restoration of mangrove forests in Madagascar is an example of a project which has succeeded in creating real benefits for the community. 马达加斯加的红树林恢复项目，就是成功为社区带来切实益处的一个例子。⭐️【mangrove forests = 红树林】
Destruction of the mangrove forests had a terrible impact on plant and animal life, and also badly affected the fishing industry, which was a major source of employment for local people living in coastal areas. 红树林遭到破坏，给动植物带来了严重影响，也重创了渔业，而渔业原本是当地沿海居民就业的重要来源。⭐️【a major source of employment = 就业的重要来源】
The reforestation project involved hiring local people to plant and care for the new mangrove trees. 该森林恢复项目雇用了当地人种植和照料新栽的红树。
Millions of mangrove trees have now been planted which has resulted in the return of a healthy aquatic ecosystem. 如今，数百万棵红树已经种下，使健康的水生生态系统得以恢复。⭐️【aquatic ecosystem = 水生生态系统】
The mangroves also act as a defence against the increased threat of flooding caused by climate change. 红树林还发挥着防御作用，抵御气候变化所造成的日益严重的洪水威胁。⭐️【act as a defence against = 发挥抵御……的防护作用】
What’s more, the local economy is more stable and thousands more Madagascans are now able to send their children to school. 此外，当地经济更加稳定，又有数千名马达加斯加人如今能够送孩子上学。
