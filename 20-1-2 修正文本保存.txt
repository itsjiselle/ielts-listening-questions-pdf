Hello and welcome. My name’s Heather McCallum and I’m one of the potters who work here at Edelman Pottery. 大家好，欢迎各位。我叫Heather McCallum，是Edelman陶艺工坊的一名陶艺师。⭐️【potter = 陶艺师；制陶工匠】
Before we go into the workshop, I just want to say a bit about the craft of pottery. 进入工作室之前，我想先简单介绍一下制陶这门手艺。
Then we’ll have a look at the equipment, and you can try making a pot of your own. 然后我们会看看设备，大家也可以尝试亲手制作一件陶器。
Like many people, I’m sure you know that pottery as an art form is tens of thousands of years old, and we know this because it stands the test of time. 我相信，和许多人一样，大家都知道陶艺作为一种艺术形式，已经有数万年的历史。我们之所以知道这一点，是因为陶器经得起时间的考验。⭐️【stand the test of time = 经得起时间的考验】
Things like baskets and pictures don’t survive in the earth in the same way that pots do. 篮子、图画之类的物品，无法像陶器那样在地下保存下来。
And even if ancient pots are found in small pieces, they still provide a lot of information about the past. 即使发现的古代陶器已经碎成小片，它们仍能提供大量有关过去的信息。
There’s no doubt that pottery has given archaeologists a fascinating insight into how ancient humans lived. 毫无疑问，陶器让考古学家得以深入了解古代人类的生活，而这些发现十分引人入胜。⭐️【give someone an insight into = 使某人深入了解……】
The shape of an artefact may have been lost, but archaeologists can tell whether the pots were for, say, storage or cooking by examining the impressions on the clay, the scratches from tools; and the clay itself can reveal where the pots came from. 一件器物的原貌可能已经无法辨认，但考古学家可以通过检查陶土上的压痕和工具留下的划痕，判断陶器的用途，比如储存物品或烹饪；而陶土本身还能揭示陶器的来源。⭐️【artefact = 人工制品，尤指有历史价值的器物】⭐️【impression = 压痕；印痕】
When I ask people why they want to take a pottery class with me, they sometimes talk about these things. 当我问大家为什么想来跟我学陶艺时，他们有时会谈到这些。
Like our ancestors, they hope that something they create will also last longer than they do, that their work – whether it is good or not – might say something about humanity many years after their death. 和我们的祖先一样，他们希望自己创作的东西能比自己存在得更久，希望自己的作品——无论好坏——在他们去世多年后，仍能传达一些关于人类的信息。
Of course, you will all have your own reasons for coming here. 当然，各位来到这里都有自己的理由。
As far as I’m concerned, what I love most is the concentration you need to make a good pot. That focus takes you away from the stresses of everyday life. 对我而言，我最喜欢的是制作一件好陶器所需要的专注。这种专注能让你暂时摆脱日常生活的压力。⭐️【as far as I’m concerned = 对我而言（话语功能：引出个人观点）】
If you’re elderly, it’s also good exercise for hands and wrists and helps with arthritis and, of course, it’s a fun activity for children because it’s so messy! 对老年人来说，陶艺还能很好地锻炼双手和手腕，并有助于缓解关节炎。当然，对孩子来说，这也是一项有趣的活动，因为可以尽情把自己弄得脏兮兮的！⭐️【arthritis = 关节炎】
Here at Edelman Pottery, we show you some of the basic pottery techniques so that you can use these to create whatever you wish – a gift for a friend, perhaps. 在Edelman陶艺工坊，我们会教大家一些基本的制陶技巧，让你们能够运用这些技巧，创作任何想做的东西——比如送给朋友的礼物。
Like nearly everyone who comes here, I’m sure this is the first time you will have tried the art, so we’ll keep things simple today. 我想，和几乎所有来这里的人一样，各位也是第一次尝试陶艺，所以今天我们会从简单的内容开始。
Now, before we move on, can I just say a word about what you’re wearing. 继续之前，我想简单说一下大家的着装。
As we said in our email, please remove any watches, necklaces, etc. and put them somewhere safe. 正如我们在邮件中所说，请摘下手表、项链等物品，并妥善保管。
If you have long hair, do tie it back now. We’ll provide aprons later, but I trust your clothes are old but comfortable – not your favourite T-shirt or jeans! 如果你留着长发，请现在把头发扎到后面。稍后我们会提供围裙，不过我想大家应该都穿了旧而舒适的衣服，而不是自己最喜欢的T恤或牛仔裤！⭐️【tie your hair back = 把头发向后扎好】⭐️【apron = 围裙】
So now we’re in the workshop. Have a look around – there’s a lot going on. 现在我们已经进入工作室了。大家四处看看——这里有不少活动正在进行。
To make pottery that will last, you need a potter’s wheel, a kiln – which is basically a very hot oven where you fire the pottery – and some tools. 要制作经久耐用的陶器，你需要陶轮、陶窑以及一些工具。陶窑基本上就是一个温度极高、用来烧制陶器的炉子。⭐️【potter’s wheel = 陶轮；制陶拉坯机】⭐️【kiln = 窑；陶窑】⭐️【fire = 烧制，尤指陶器】
So first, the kiln. If you look over in the far corner, you’ll see one of ours. 首先说说陶窑。大家往远处的角落看，就能看到我们的一个陶窑。
Since their invention, kilns have changed very little, though in the past 20 years, a lot of progress has been made in temperature control. 自发明以来，陶窑本身变化不大，不过过去二十年间，温度控制技术取得了很大进步。
Basically, a kiln removes the water from clay at temperatures of around a thousand degrees Celsius. This allows anything you’ve made to set permanently in shape. 简单来说，陶窑在约一千摄氏度的高温下去除陶土中的水分，使制作出来的物品永久定型。⭐️【set = 凝固；变硬定型】
It’s a pretty ugly, heavy object that’s hard to keep in a house or flat so most people don’t have one. 陶窑外观不太好看，而且很笨重，难以放在住宅或公寓里，所以大多数人都没有。
You may think, ‘Can’t I use my oven?’ Well, that’s possible but domestic ovens don’t really get hot enough and eventually the clay will crack and fall apart. 你可能会想：“不能用家里的烤箱吗？”嗯，可以尝试，但家用烤箱的温度实际上不够高，陶土最终会开裂、碎掉。⭐️【domestic = 家用的】⭐️【fall apart = 散架；碎裂】
Some people fire pottery in a fire pit outside – but bear in mind that can be dangerous. 有些人会在户外的火坑里烧制陶器，但要记住，这可能很危险。⭐️【bear in mind = 记住；考虑到】
You also need to know about safety procedures for kilns as they release toxic compounds into the air. 你还需要了解陶窑的安全操作规程，因为陶窑会向空气中释放有毒化合物。⭐️【toxic compounds = 有毒化合物】
Every potter needs a potter’s wheel. This machine is used to shape the clay into an object with circular walls or sides – such as a bowl. 每位陶艺师都需要陶轮。这种机器用于把陶土塑造成器壁呈圆形的物品，比如碗。
Its invention revolutionised the pottery industry, allowing multiple items to be produced in a day. 陶轮的发明彻底改变了制陶业，使人们能够在一天内制作出多件陶器。⭐️【revolutionise = 彻底改变；使发生重大变革】
Lastly, there are a number of different tools that potters use, depending on what they want to make. 最后，陶艺师会根据想制作的物品，使用各种不同的工具。
When you start, your hands can make all kinds of shapes and curves without relying on a sculpting tool. 刚开始时，你可以用双手塑造各种形状和曲线，不必依赖雕塑工具。⭐️【sculpting tool = 雕塑工具；塑形工具】
However, there are some basic tools that you will need to handle the clay on the wheel. Some look very strange and have even odder names that you may find hard to remember. 不过，在陶轮上处理陶土时，你还是需要一些基本工具。有些工具看起来很奇怪，名字更奇怪，你可能会觉得很难记住。
Rather than go through them all now, I’ll just name a few tools as we go along. We can provide these and I wouldn’t recommend spending money on them yet. 我就不在这里逐一介绍了，而是在操作过程中随时告诉大家用到的工具名称。这些工具我们都可以提供，我不建议大家现在就花钱购买。⭐️【go through = 逐一介绍；逐项查看】⭐️【as we go along = 随着过程推进；边做边……】
So let’s try making a pot of your own … 那么，现在就来试着亲手制作一件陶器吧……
