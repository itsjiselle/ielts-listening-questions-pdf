It’s quite hard to think of a city that doesn’t have a big river running through it. 要想出一座没有大河穿城而过的城市，还真不容易。⭐️【run through = 穿过；贯穿】
If you think about the major cities in the world, Shanghai, New York, Mumbai, London, they’re nearly all built on rivers. 想想世界上的主要城市，比如上海、纽约、孟买和伦敦，它们几乎都沿河而建。⭐️【be built on rivers = 沿河而建】
When these cities were established hundreds or even thousands of years ago, the rivers were a big part of people’s lives. 几百年甚至几千年前，这些城市建立时，河流在人们的生活中占据着重要地位。
In 16th-century London, the quickest way to get from one part of the city to another was by river. 在十六世纪的伦敦，从城中一个地方到另一个地方，最快的方式是走水路。
But people also used the river for fishing, as the water then was relatively clean, and they would also go on boat trips up and down the river just for pleasure, as a relaxing escape from the noise and bustle of the city streets. 不过，人们也会在河里捕鱼，因为当时的河水相对干净。他们还会单纯为了游玩而乘船沿河往返，暂时逃离城市街道的喧嚣与忙碌，放松身心。⭐️【the noise and bustle = 喧嚣与忙碌】⭐️【an escape from = 对……的暂时逃离】
But as industries developed and populations increased, city rivers suffered. 但随着工业发展、人口增加，城市河流遭到了破坏。
The rising number of people meant there was a huge increase in the amount of sewage discharged into the rivers. 人口增加意味着排入河流的污水量大幅上升。⭐️【sewage = 污水；生活污水】⭐️【discharge into = 排放到……中】
Rivers had always been used for this purpose, but when the number of inhabitants was so small that wasn’t such a problem. 河流一直被用于排污，但过去居民人数很少时，这还不算什么大问题。
However, as cities grew to over a million inhabitants, the impact on the rivers became more serious. 然而，当城市人口增长到一百多万时，对河流造成的影响就变得更加严重。
In addition, other types of pollution increased as factories were built beside the river and discharged their waste materials into the water. 此外，随着工厂沿河兴建，并将废弃物排入水中，其他类型的污染也随之增加。
This got worse over time. 这种情况随着时间推移不断恶化。
As recently as 1957, scientists at London’s Natural History Museum declared that the River Thames was ‘dead’ in biological terms, as the water was too filthy to support any kind of life. 直到1957年，伦敦自然历史博物馆的科学家还宣布，泰晤士河在生物学意义上已经“死亡”，因为河水污秽不堪，无法维持任何生命。⭐️【as recently as = 就在……时还……，强调时间距今并不久】⭐️【in biological terms = 从生物学角度而言】⭐️【filthy = 极其肮脏的】
But in recent years, as rivers lost their industrial function, cities have begun to recognise their true value and to take steps to clean them up. 不过，近年来，随着河流逐渐失去工业用途，城市开始认识到它们的真正价值，并采取措施治理污染。⭐️【take steps to = 采取措施做……】⭐️【clean up = 清理；治理污染】
For example, the River Thames is now cleaner than it’s been for a hundred and fifty years. 例如，如今泰晤士河的水质比过去一百五十年中的任何时候都更干净。
These days you can see seals swimming in the water and recently people had to try to rescue a whale which had got lost and swum up the river from the sea by mistake. 如今，你能看到海豹在河中游泳；最近，人们还曾设法营救一头迷路的鲸鱼，它误从海里游进了河中，沿河而上。
Unfortunately they didn’t succeed, but the problem was disorientation rather than the quality of the water. 遗憾的是，救援没有成功，但问题在于鲸鱼迷失了方向，而不是水质。⭐️【disorientation = 方向感丧失；迷失方向】
Then, all around the world, riverside areas are now seen as prime sites for development. 此外，如今世界各地的滨河区域都被视为开发的黄金地段。⭐️【prime sites = 黄金地段；最适宜的地点】
Warehouses that were once used for storing goods are now being converted into expensive restaurants and also into apartments with river views, which are in great demand and sell for astronomical prices. 过去用于储存货物的仓库，如今正被改造成高档餐厅和河景公寓；这些公寓需求旺盛，售价高得惊人。⭐️【be converted into = 被改造成】⭐️【in great demand = 需求旺盛】⭐️【astronomical prices = 天价；高得惊人的价格】
In Los Angeles, on the west coast of the USA, an architect has plans to revitalise the banks of the river and to make a park there which can provide facilities for sports as well as a natural environment for relaxing in. 在美国西海岸的洛杉矶，一位建筑师计划让河岸重新焕发生机，在那里建造一座公园，既提供运动设施，也提供可供放松的自然环境。⭐️【revitalise = 使重新焕发生机；振兴】
It’s also hoped that the riverside can be used for other purposes – it’s been proposed that facilities could be provided for displaying projects related to various kinds of art that have been produced by local people, for example. 人们还希望滨河区域能够有其他用途——例如，有人提议设置设施，展示当地居民创作的各类艺术项目。
In the city of Paris, during the summer months of July and August, all the traffic is banned from the roads by the sides of the river and the banks are transformed into beaches, where people can relax in deckchairs under potted palm trees, sunbathe, or buy a drink or a snack while enjoying the view. 在巴黎，每年夏季的七月和八月，河两岸的道路禁止所有车辆通行，河岸则被改造成沙滩。人们可以在盆栽棕榈树下的躺椅上休息、晒日光浴，或者买些饮料和小吃，同时欣赏风景。⭐️【be transformed into = 被转变为；被改造成】⭐️【deckchairs = 折叠躺椅】
But to make the most of our rivers in our increasingly crowded cities, we need to allow them to regain their original purpose and be used as a means of transport, reclaiming our streets from cars and lorries. 不过，要在日益拥挤的城市中充分利用河流，我们需要让它们恢复最初的用途，重新承担交通运输功能，把街道从汽车和卡车的占用中解放出来。⭐️【make the most of = 充分利用】⭐️【regain = 重新获得；恢复】⭐️【reclaim … from … = 从……手中收回……】
To do this we’ll have to shift more traffic back to the river, but this time cleanly and silently, making the most of modern technology. 为此，我们必须将更多运输活动转回河流，但这一次，要充分利用现代技术，以清洁、安静的方式进行。⭐️【shift … back to … = 将……重新转移到……】
Already more than two billion passengers use the ferry to travel in cities around the world like Istanbul, San Francisco and New York. 在伊斯坦布尔、旧金山和纽约等世界各地的城市中，已有超过二十亿人次乘坐渡轮出行。⭐️【ferry = 渡轮】
And these numbers are set to rise further. 而这一数字预计还将继续上升。⭐️【be set to = 预计将；很可能会】
Admittedly, it’s not a fast way of travelling, but neither is a car when it’s stuck in traffic. 诚然，这种出行方式并不快，但汽车堵在路上时也同样快不起来。⭐️【admittedly = 诚然，用于承认某个不利事实】⭐️【neither is … = ……也不，使用倒装结构】
Of course, passenger traffic on roads might decrease as more people start working from home. 当然，随着更多人开始居家办公，道路客运量可能会减少。
But another recent development, the huge rise in online shopping, has meant that another form of urban traffic just keeps on growing, and that’s deliveries. 但最近的另一项变化，也就是网购的大幅增长，意味着另一类城市运输活动持续增加，那就是配送。⭐️【deliveries = 配送；送货活动】
Trucks and vans in the city pollute and double park while dropping off parcels. 城市中的卡车和厢式货车在配送包裹时会造成污染，还会并排停在已停靠的车辆旁边。⭐️【double park = 并排停车，停在已停靠车辆旁并占用车道】⭐️【drop off = 送达；顺路送下】
Imagine using the immense capacity of shipping to take these trucks off the road. 想象一下，如果利用水运巨大的运输能力，让这些卡车不再上路，会怎样呢？⭐️【immense capacity = 巨大的运载能力】⭐️【take … off the road = 使……不再上路行驶】
One freight barge can replace 44 large trucks, uses far less energy and causes less pollution. 一艘货运驳船可以替代四十四辆大型卡车，消耗的能源少得多，造成的污染也更少。⭐️【freight barge = 货运驳船】
When the barge docks at the riverside, the parcels could be taken the last few kilometres to their final destination on cargo bikes – electric ones, of course. 当驳船在河岸停靠后，包裹最后几公里的配送就可以由货运自行车完成，送到最终目的地——当然，是电动货运自行车。⭐️【dock = 停靠码头】⭐️【cargo bikes = 货运自行车】
This is already happening in the Dutch city of Amsterdam. 在荷兰城市阿姆斯特丹，这种做法已经开始实施。
And in future, the final stage could even be carried out by drone, although at present this isn’t allowed. 将来，最后一个配送环节甚至可以由无人机完成，尽管目前还不被允许。⭐️【be carried out by = 由……执行或完成】
Wouldn’t it be great to unblock our city centres in this way! 如果能用这种方式疏通市中心的交通，那该多好！⭐️【unblock = 疏通；使不再堵塞】
Looking further ahead … 再展望更长远的未来……⭐️【look further ahead = 展望更长远的未来】
