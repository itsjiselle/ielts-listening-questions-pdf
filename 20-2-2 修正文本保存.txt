Good morning everyone.I’m Steve Wainwright, from Elmleigh Town Council, and I organise the town’s volunteer scheme. 大家早上好。我叫Steve Wainwright，来自Elmleigh镇议会，负责组织镇上的志愿服务计划。
I’m delighted you’re all interested in joining the scheme. 很高兴大家都有兴趣加入这项计划。
Our volunteers help to create a sense of community among the many people who live in our historic town of Elmleigh, and make residents and visitors feel welcome at local events. 我们的志愿者帮助生活在Elmleigh这座历史小镇的居民建立社区归属感，也让参加当地活动的居民和游客感受到热情欢迎。⭐️【a sense of community = 社区归属感；邻里认同感】
First I’ll mention just a few of the activities that volunteers carry out. 首先，我来简单介绍几项志愿者承担的工作。
One is to walk around the town centre streets wearing our volunteer T-shirt. 其中一项是穿着我们的志愿者T恤，在镇中心的街道上巡走。
Tourists often ask how to get to a particular shop, and they might also be grateful for recommendations about what to visit. 游客经常会询问某家商店怎么走，也可能很希望得到一些景点推荐。
The town holds a large number of concerts each year, and part of the volunteers’ role is to get everyone in the audience to the right place as smoothly as possible. 镇上每年都会举办大量音乐会，志愿者的一项职责就是尽可能顺畅地引导所有观众到达正确的位置。
You’d be surprised how many people buy tickets then don’t check them, and head for the wrong section of the hall! 买完票却不核对票面信息，结果走错会场分区的人多得会让你吃惊！
Volunteers may get involved with community groups, such as sports clubs or gardeners’ associations. 志愿者也可能参与社区团体的工作，比如体育俱乐部或园艺爱好者协会。
Here the volunteers talk about how groups can help each other. 在这方面，志愿者会与各团体讨论如何互相帮助。
For instance, a writing group might want to travel to another town to hear a talk by a well-known author, but may not know that another club has a coach they could travel in. 例如，一个写作小组可能想去另一个镇听知名作家的讲座，却不知道另一个俱乐部有一辆可以供他们乘坐的大客车。⭐️【coach = 大客车；长途客车】
The town produces a monthly magazine, and anyone who lives in the town can send in articles. 镇上每月出版一本杂志，所有居民都可以投稿。⭐️【send in = 提交；寄送，尤指稿件或申请】
It’s free to residents, and is paid for by local businesses – that’s the responsibility of the council’s advertising department. 杂志免费提供给居民，费用由当地商家承担，相关事宜由镇议会的广告部门负责。
We depend on volunteers, though, to find out what people think of events they’ve attended, and any suggestions they have for the future. 不过，我们需要依靠志愿者了解大家对所参加活动的看法，以及他们对未来活动的建议。
The volunteers then send a summary to the editors. 然后，志愿者会将汇总内容发送给编辑。
There are a number of clubs for retired people, and every year, the council arranges lunch for all the members. 镇上有多个退休人士俱乐部，镇议会每年都会为所有会员安排一次午餐聚会。
The volunteers welcome the guests, and when everyone’s sitting down and relaxing after the meal, some volunteers put on a show – usually around half an hour of songs and short plays. 志愿者负责迎接宾客，饭后大家坐下来放松时，一些志愿者会带来表演，通常是大约半小时的歌曲和短剧。⭐️【put on a show = 举办演出；表演节目】
The club members really welcome the chance to chat to the volunteers at these events. 俱乐部会员非常珍惜在这些活动中与志愿者聊天的机会。
The town council has a website, of course, and volunteers are asked to help by making sure residents know about it. 当然，镇议会也有网站，我们希望志愿者协助宣传，让居民知道这个网站。
It’s updated every day with information about future activities, and we want as many people as possible to use it. 网站每天都会更新即将举办的活动信息，我们希望有尽可能多的人使用它。
OK, now you know some of the things our volunteers do, I’ll go on to some practical matters. 好的，现在大家已经了解了志愿者的部分工作，接下来我会介绍一些具体安排。
As you probably know, the town arranges three major festivals every year, and they all depend on a large number of volunteers. 大家可能知道，镇上每年都会举办三大节庆活动，每一项都需要大量志愿者的支持。
The book festival lasts three days, and uses several venues, which all need volunteers. 图书节持续三天，在多个场地举办，每个场地都需要志愿者。
More are needed for the music festival, because that lasts a whole week, and even more help is required for the science festival, even though it’s only two days long. 音乐节需要更多志愿者，因为它持续整整一周；而科学节需要的人手还要更多，尽管它只有两天。
It involves quite a lot of venues, though. 不过，科学节涉及的场地相当多。
It’s a good idea to help at the festival you’re most interested in, because you can attend most of the events for free. 建议大家选择自己最感兴趣的节庆活动提供帮助，因为这样可以免费参加其中大部分活动。
We try to use volunteers who are flexible, though, because some festival events are held outdoors, and the weather may affect the size of the audience, and even whether the event can take place; so there can be changes at short notice. 不过，我们会尽量安排能够灵活应变的志愿者，因为部分节庆活动在户外举行，天气可能影响观众人数，甚至决定活动能否举办，因此有时会临时调整安排。⭐️【flexible = 能灵活应变的】⭐️【at short notice = 提前很短时间通知；临时】
What’s essential, though, is being able to get on well with other people, and also to deal with someone who’s behaving badly – as occasionally happens. 不过，最重要的是能够与他人融洽相处，也能应对行为不当的人——这种情况偶尔确实会发生。⭐️【get on well with = 与……相处融洽】
Our plan is to get you all working in September, after a week’s training starting on the 2nd. 我们计划让大家在九月份开始工作，先从2日开始接受为期一周的培训。
So we’ll be timetabling you for duties the following week, from the 9th onward. 因此，我们会安排大家从接下来一周，也就是9日起开始值勤。⭐️【timetable someone for duties = 为某人安排值勤时间】
Later, in the week beginning September 23rd, we have a chat with each of you, to find out how you feel about being a volunteer, and what extra support you need. 之后，在9月23日开始的那一周，我们会与各位分别聊一聊，了解大家做志愿者的感受，以及还需要哪些额外支持。
As a thank you to the volunteers, we arrange an annual event. 为了感谢志愿者，我们每年都会组织一次活动。
In recent years we’ve had a party in the town hall, and last year a barbecue in Chamber Park. 近几年，我们在市政厅举办过聚会，去年则在Chamber Park举办了烧烤活动。
Our forthcoming event is a trip along the canal from here to Dewhurst and back. 接下来的活动是沿运河从这里前往Dewhurst，再原路返回的一次游览。⭐️【forthcoming = 即将到来的】
It’s on Saturday September the 28th, and if you’d like to attend, you can sign up once you start work. 活动定于9月28日星期六举行，如果想参加，大家开始工作后就可以报名。
Now this is the volunteers’ T-shirt … 现在，这就是我们的志愿者T恤……
