There are trends in food, much as there are trends in clothing. 食物也有潮流，就像服装有潮流一样。⭐️【much as = 正如……一样，此处表示相似】
Interest in food fashions has risen rapidly since the birth of the smartphone when people first began taking photos of their food and instantly sharing them with their friends. 自智能手机诞生、人们开始拍摄食物并即时与朋友分享以来，大家对饮食潮流的兴趣迅速增长。
The food industry in the UK, in particular, is obsessed with finding and exploiting the next big food trend. 尤其是英国食品行业，热衷于寻找并利用下一股热门饮食潮流。⭐️【be obsessed with = 痴迷于；一心想着】⭐️【exploit = 利用……获取利益】
Marketeers aim to create a huge demand for a food item, which was previously unknown, or not needed. 营销人员力图让一种此前不为人知、或原本并非人们所需的食品产生巨大的市场需求。⭐️【marketeers = 营销人员】⭐️【create demand for = 创造对……的需求】
One of the most effective ways of promoting a new food product is by using social media influencers as brand ambassadors. 推广新食品最有效的方式之一，就是让社交媒体网红担任品牌大使。⭐️【social media influencers = 社交媒体网红；有影响力的内容创作者】⭐️【brand ambassadors = 品牌大使】
In return for free samples, many influencers will post content about a product, although there are influencers with hundreds of thousands of followers who can command large fees for their services. 许多网红会以发布产品相关内容来换取免费样品，不过，一些拥有数十万粉丝的网红能够为自己的推广服务收取高额费用。⭐️【in return for = 作为……的交换或回报】⭐️【command large fees = 能够收取高额费用】
Companies which sell vegan produce were pioneers in being able to increase sales really quickly in this way. 销售纯素食品的公司是率先通过这种方式迅速提高销量的先行者。⭐️【vegan produce = 纯素食品】⭐️【pioneers = 先行者；开拓者】
For a food item to become really popular it has to be readily available, so supermarkets have a role to play in creating a new food trend. 一种食品要真正流行起来，就必须让人们很容易买到，因此超市在创造新的饮食潮流方面也发挥着作用。⭐️【readily available = 容易获得的；随时可以买到的】⭐️【have a role to play in = 在……中发挥作用】
They have dedicated teams closely following which new products or ingredients are trending on social media and are particularly interested in what well-known chefs are putting on their menus. 超市有专门的团队，密切关注社交媒体上哪些新产品或食材正流行，尤其留意知名厨师正在将什么加入菜单。⭐️【dedicated teams = 专门负责某项工作的团队】⭐️【trending = 正在流行的；受到热议的】
I’d like to look at a few examples of marketing campaigns which were really successful in launching a new fashion trend. 我想介绍几个营销活动的例子，它们都非常成功地带动了一股新潮流。⭐️【marketing campaigns = 营销活动】
Starting in the 1990s with the avocado. 先从二十世纪九十年代的牛油果说起。
A British PR company was hired to raise its profile and stimulate demand. 一家英国公关公司受聘提高牛油果的知名度，并刺激市场需求。⭐️【raise its profile = 提高其知名度】⭐️【stimulate demand = 刺激需求】
They paid for a group of journalists to travel out to South Africa to meet avocado farmers. 他们出资安排一组记者前往南非，与牛油果种植者见面。
Articles written following this visit helped to educate the British public about the avocado, which at this time was certainly not the daily staple it’s since become. 这次访问后发表的文章帮助英国公众了解了牛油果。当时，它显然还不是后来那种日常饮食中常见的食品。⭐️【educate … about … = 向……普及有关……的知识】⭐️【a daily staple = 日常经常食用的基本食品】
Advertisements were designed to promote the avocado as a ‘superfood’: rich in nutrients and therefore beneficial for health. 广告将牛油果宣传为一种“超级食物”：富含营养物质，因此有益健康。⭐️【promote … as … = 将……宣传为……】⭐️【rich in nutrients = 富含营养物质】
Avocados became hugely fashionable and within a few years UK avocado sales had grown from 13 million pounds annually to around 150 million, making it one of the most successful fresh produce campaigns in UK history. 牛油果迅速风靡，短短几年内，英国牛油果的年销售额就从一千三百万英镑增长到约一亿五千万英镑，使这场推广成为英国历史上最成功的生鲜农产品营销活动之一。⭐️【fresh produce = 生鲜农产品】
Oat milk is a recent example of a new product which became fashionable very quickly. 燕麦奶是近年新产品迅速流行起来的一个例子。
Now there are many brands available, but one company which had early success was the Swedish brand, Oatly. 如今市场上已有许多品牌，但较早取得成功的公司之一是瑞典品牌Oatly。
They attracted a lot of attention with a media campaign which used provocation as a way of getting their message across effectively. 他们通过一场媒体宣传活动吸引了大量关注，利用挑衅性的宣传方式有效传达品牌信息。⭐️【provocation = 挑衅；故意引发反应的言行】⭐️【get their message across = 清楚有效地传达信息】
The fact that this campaign aggravated competitors producing milk from dairy cows was seen as a plus, as it helped to make oat milk seem cool. 这场宣传激怒了生产牛奶的竞争对手，但这反而被视为一项优势，因为它有助于让燕麦奶显得很酷。⭐️【aggravate = 激怒；使恼火】⭐️【a plus = 优势；有利因素】
In the USA, the brand decided against a big retail launch in favour of getting the product into coffee chains, which removed the need for a big advertising budget. 在美国，该品牌决定不进行大规模零售上市推广，而是选择让产品进入连锁咖啡店，从而省去了投入巨额广告预算的必要。⭐️【decide against = 决定不做……】⭐️【in favour of = 转而选择；支持】
This proved far more effective than offering samples in supermarkets. 事实证明，这比在超市提供试用样品有效得多。
Oat milk had an advantage over other alternative milk products such as almond milk. 与杏仁奶等其他替代乳品相比，燕麦奶具有一项优势。⭐️【have an advantage over = 比……具有优势】
Many consumers prefer it because it has less of an impact on the environment. 许多消费者更喜欢它，因为它对环境的影响较小。
It requires significantly less water to produce than other alternative milk products and it also has a relatively low carbon footprint. 与其他替代乳品相比，生产燕麦奶所需的水明显更少，而且它的碳足迹也相对较低。⭐️【carbon footprint = 碳足迹，某项活动或产品造成的温室气体排放量】
Norwegian skrei, a rarely available seasonal fish delicacy, otherwise known as Arctic cod, is now found on the menus of Michelin starred restaurants throughout Europe. 挪威skrei是一种供应稀少的时令鱼类珍馐，也被称为北极鳕鱼，如今已出现在欧洲各地米其林星级餐厅的菜单上。⭐️【delicacy = 珍馐；美味佳肴】⭐️【otherwise known as = 又称】⭐️【Michelin starred restaurants = 米其林星级餐厅】
The demand for skrei has been used by a food marketing agency to build the reputation of Norway’s fisheries in general. 一家食品营销机构利用人们对skrei的需求，提升了挪威渔业的整体声誉。⭐️【build the reputation of = 树立、提升……的声誉】⭐️【in general = 总体而言；整体上】
Marketing surveys have shown that a significant number of shoppers now associate Norway with excellent seafood. 市场调查显示，如今有相当多消费者会将挪威与优质海鲜联系起来。⭐️【associate … with … = 将……与……联系起来】
Food trends can be considered a good thing in some ways as they can benefit farmers and food producers enormously. 从某些方面来看，饮食潮流可以被视为一件好事，因为它能给农民和食品生产商带来巨大收益。
The public can also be encouraged to buy things which are more sustainably produced. 它也能鼓励公众购买以更可持续的方式生产的产品。
But ethical concerns have been raised about the effects a surge in demand can cause.Quinoa is a classic example. 但需求激增可能造成的影响，也引发了伦理方面的担忧。藜麦就是一个典型例子。⭐️【ethical concerns = 伦理方面的担忧】⭐️【a surge in demand = 需求的激增】⭐️【quinoa = 藜麦】
This plant is native to Peru, and when demand peaked some years ago, the price soared, making it unaffordable for local people. 这种植物原产于秘鲁，几年前，当需求达到顶峰时，价格也随之飙升，导致当地人买不起。⭐️【be native to = 原产于】⭐️【peak = 达到顶峰】⭐️【soar = 急剧上涨】
While the popularity of quinoa has benefitted farmers financially, there have been other negative consequences. 虽然藜麦的流行为农民带来了经济收益，但也产生了其他负面后果。
As demand grew, farmers began working the land all year round in order to produce more quinoa. 随着需求增长，农民开始全年耕种土地，以生产更多藜麦。⭐️【work the land = 耕种土地】
One issue has been that the fertility of the soil decreased dramatically, which could potentially lead to desertification in some areas. 其中一个问题是，土壤肥力大幅下降，这可能导致一些地区出现荒漠化。⭐️【the fertility of the soil = 土壤肥力】⭐️【desertification = 荒漠化】
