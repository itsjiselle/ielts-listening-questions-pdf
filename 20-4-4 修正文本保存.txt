We’ve been looking at different types of conflicts that may arise between wildlife and humans at the boundaries of protected areas such as national parks and animal sanctuaries. 我们一直在探讨国家公园、动物保护区等保护区域边界处，野生动物与人类之间可能发生的不同类型的冲突。⭐️【arise = 出现；发生】⭐️【animal sanctuaries = 动物保护区】
I’d like to illustrate this by telling you about some research that I’ve been involved in recently in the central African country of Zambia in the area around the Chembe Bird Sanctuary, which contains over three hundred of the listed birds of Zambia. 我想通过介绍最近参与的一项研究来说明这一问题。研究地点位于非洲中部国家赞比亚的Chembe鸟类保护区周边，该保护区拥有赞比亚已记录鸟类中的三百多种。
These include a number of birds of prey, such as eagles, hawks and owls, that live by hunting and killing other birds and animals. 其中包括鹰、隼和猫头鹰等多种猛禽，它们依靠捕杀其他鸟类和动物为生。⭐️【birds of prey = 猛禽】
Now, most of the people living in the local communities near to the bird sanctuary are small-scale farmers, and these birds of prey provide important social and ecological benefits to them. 鸟类保护区附近社区的大多数居民都是小规模经营的农民，这些猛禽为他们带来了重要的社会和生态效益。⭐️【small-scale farmers = 小规模经营的农民】
For example, a lot of damage can be caused to farmers’ crops by rodents such as rats, which would consume the crops as they grow in the fields as well as after harvesting if they weren’t hunted and killed by the birds. 例如，老鼠等啮齿动物会严重破坏农作物。如果没有这些鸟类捕杀它们，它们不仅会啃食田间生长的庄稼，也会吃掉收获后的粮食。⭐️【rodents = 啮齿动物】
And the predatory habits of these birds also protect farmers in other ways. 这些鸟类的捕食习性还会以其他方式保护农民。⭐️【predatory habits = 捕食习性】
For example, a major danger to rural workers is snakes, whose bite may be dangerous or even fatal, and birds of prey have a major role in keeping their populations under control. 例如，蛇是农业劳动者面临的一大危险，蛇咬可能造成严重伤害，甚至致命，而猛禽在控制蛇的数量方面发挥着重要作用。⭐️【fatal = 致命的】⭐️【keep … under control = 将……控制在一定范围内】
Local people have always been aware of these benefits and for years, even before the sanctuary was opened in 1973, the birds played a key role in the culture of the region. 当地人一直了解这些益处。多年来，甚至在保护区于1973年开放之前，这些鸟类就在当地文化中占据着重要地位。
However, more recently, the sanctuary and its birds have also become increasingly important to the community in economic terms, because at present, after a relatively slow start, tourism has become an important source of revenue for them. 不过，近年来，保护区及其中的鸟类在经济上对当地社区也变得越来越重要，因为旅游业虽然起步较慢，如今却已成为他们重要的收入来源。⭐️【in economic terms = 从经济角度而言】⭐️【a source of revenue = 收入来源】
However, although these birds of prey are protected by the government, their numbers are falling. 然而，尽管这些猛禽受到政府保护，它们的数量仍在下降。
Some of these deaths are accidental. 其中一些死亡是意外造成的。
Fatalities occur when birds alight on roads to catch and eat their prey, and are hit by fast-moving traffic. 当鸟类降落在道路上捕捉和进食猎物时，可能被高速行驶的车辆撞死。 ⭐ ️【fatalities = 死亡事件；死亡个体】⭐️【alight on = 降落在；停落在】【traffic （A1） 路上行驶的车辆】
Drivers in Zambia have to take special care at night, as birds may regard the quieter roads as safe places to sleep. 赞比亚的驾驶员夜间必须格外小心，因为鸟类可能将较为安静的道路视为安全的睡觉地点。
Accidental deaths may also occur if these birds fly close to high-voltage power lines, as they may be electrocuted. 如果这些鸟飞近高压电线，也可能因触电而意外死亡。⭐️【high-voltage power lines = 高压电线】⭐️【be electrocuted = 触电死亡】
This is a particular danger in the heavy rain which can occur in the region in the months from December to April. 在当地十二月至四月间可能出现的大雨天气里，这种危险尤其突出。
And local farmers also pose a threat to these birds. 当地农民也会对这些鸟类构成威胁。⭐️【pose a threat to = 对……构成威胁】
As well as growing crops, small-scale farmers in the area also rear chickens. 除了种植农作物，当地的小规模农户也养鸡。⭐️【rear chickens = 养鸡】
These provide food for the farmers’ families, as well as being an important source of income. 这些鸡既为农民家庭提供食物，也是重要的收入来源。
But they’re also an easy target for birds of prey, and so farmers may shoot these birds, which is illegal but understandable, or they may poison the birds, which again is illegal and can have negative effects on the ecosystem. 但鸡也是猛禽容易捕获的目标，因此农民可能会射杀这些鸟类，虽然违法，但可以理解；他们也可能毒杀鸟类，这同样违法，而且可能对生态系统产生负面影响。⭐️【an easy target = 容易攻击或捕获的目标】
So how else can farmers protect their chickens from birds of prey? 那么，农民还能用什么其他方法保护鸡群，避免它们遭到猛禽袭击呢？
Some people believe that to prevent the predators from settling near the area where the chickens are kept it’s best to keep this area free from vegetation. 有些人认为，为防止捕食者在养鸡区域附近栖居，最好清除这片区域的植被。⭐️【keep … free from … = 使……没有……】
But in fact this is counter-productive as it means the chickens have no cover to hide in and they’ll be easier for the birds to see. 但事实上，这样做适得其反，因为鸡群会失去可以藏身的遮蔽物，更容易被猛禽发现。⭐️【counter-productive = 适得其反的】⭐️【cover = 可供躲藏的遮蔽物】
Another possibility would be to prevent the chickens from going outside at all, and to keep them safe from predators inside a building, but this would cost far too much to be a practical solution. 另一种可能的办法是完全不让鸡外出，将它们养在建筑物内，避免捕食者的袭击；但这样成本过高，无法成为切实可行的解决方案。⭐️【a practical solution = 切实可行的解决方案】
Nearly all the farmers reported that they spent a lot of time and effort trying to frighten off the birds of prey without actually harming them. 几乎所有农民都表示，他们花费了大量时间和精力，试图在不伤害猛禽的情况下将其吓跑。⭐️【frighten off = 吓跑】
Most of the farmers had at least one dog, and said this was a big help at scaring away the predators. 大多数农民至少养了一条狗，并表示狗对吓跑捕食者很有帮助。⭐️【scare away = 吓走】
Some of the farmers also reported that during the breeding season, when the chickens were particularly vulnerable, they encouraged their children to watch over the chickens and to hit pans with a metal spoon so that the resulting noise would succeed in driving away birds that were trying to seize the young chicks. 一些农民还表示，在鸡群特别容易受到袭击的繁殖季，他们会让孩子们看护鸡群，并用金属勺敲打锅具，利用发出的声响赶走试图抓走小鸡的鸟类。⭐️【vulnerable = 易受伤害的】⭐️【watch over = 照看；看护】⭐️【drive away = 驱赶；赶走】⭐️【seize = 猛然抓住；攫取】
None of these methods was a hundred per cent effective, so as a result, the village people told us that rather than just using one method, they were forced to use a combination for them to have any effect. 这些方法没有一种是百分之百有效的，因此村民告诉我们，要想起到效果，他们不得不结合使用多种方法，而不是只依靠一种。
And even so, these birds of prey remain a major threat to the chickens’ survival and cause considerable economic loss to farmers. 即便如此，这些猛禽仍然严重威胁着鸡群的生存，并给农民造成相当大的经济损失。⭐️【even so = 即便如此】⭐️【considerable economic loss = 相当大的经济损失】
