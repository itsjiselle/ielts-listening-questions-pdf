Good morning everyone. Today's lecture forms part of the Hospitality and Tourism module. 大家早上好。今天的讲座是酒店接待与旅游模块的一部分。
Last week I looked at the economy end of the hotel business; this week I'm going to discuss the luxury end of the market. 上周，我介绍了酒店业的经济型市场；本周，我将讨论高端奢华市场。⭐️【the economy end = 经济型市场】⭐️【the luxury end = 高端奢华市场】
Let's consider the following scenario... 我们来设想以下情境……
You wake up in the middle of the night in a strange hotel miles away from home, disoriented most probably from jet lag, when even the most expensive surroundings can seem empty and dispiriting. 半夜，你在远离家乡的一家陌生酒店里醒来，很可能因为时差而感到恍惚。此时，即使再昂贵奢华的环境，也可能显得空荡荡的，让人情绪低落。⭐️【disoriented = 迷失方向的；头脑恍惚的】⭐️【dispiriting = 令人沮丧的；使人失去精神的】
You have paid a great deal of money to stay in this first-class hotel with its contemporary technology, but according to recent research carried out by an international travel and public relations company, all is not well. 你花了大笔钱，入住这家配备现代科技的一流酒店，但根据一家国际旅游与公关公司近期开展的研究，情况并非尽如人意。⭐️【all is not well = 情况并非一切顺利；存在问题】
The research suggests that even the most opulent, luxurious hotels seem to have underestimated the most basic needs of their customers-be they travelling for work or pleasure: the need to feel at home in surroundings which are both familiar and inviting. 研究表明，即使是最富丽堂皇的豪华酒店，似乎也低估了顾客最基本的需求——无论他们是出差还是休闲旅行，都希望在熟悉而温馨宜人的环境中感受到家的自在。⭐️【opulent = 富丽堂皇的；奢华的】⭐️【be they … or … = 无论他们是……还是……】⭐️【inviting = 宜人的；令人想要置身其中的】
Do these findings, however, apply only to hotels situated in particular areas? 不过，这些研究结果是否只适用于位于特定地区的酒店呢？
Is it possible that the external environment can affect a guest's well-being? 外部环境有没有可能影响住客的身心感受呢？
The company's research covered a whole range of different hotel types, both independent hotels and those which are part of large chains. 该公司的研究涵盖了各种不同类型的酒店，既包括独立经营的酒店，也包括大型连锁集团旗下的酒店。
They investigated chic so-called boutique hotels in the heart of downtown business districts, stately mansions located in the depths of beautiful countryside, and plush hotels built at the edge of tropical beaches surrounded by palm trees and idyllic blue ocean. 他们调查了位于市中心商业区核心地段、时髦的所谓精品酒店，坐落在优美乡间深处的宏伟宅邸，以及建在热带海滩边、被棕榈树和如诗如画的蔚蓝海洋环绕的豪华酒店。⭐️【chic = 时髦雅致的】⭐️【boutique hotel = 精品酒店】⭐️【stately mansion = 宏伟气派的宅邸】⭐️【plush = 豪华舒适的】⭐️【idyllic = 田园诗般美好的；宁静如画的】
And the research concluded that what was outside the hotel building simply didn't matter. 研究得出的结论是，酒店建筑外部的环境根本无关紧要。
This is a fascinating revelation and those of you hoping to move into careers in the travel and leisure industry would be well advised to look at the findings in more detail. 这一发现十分耐人寻味。如果各位希望将来进入旅游休闲行业，最好进一步仔细研究这些结果。⭐️【revelation = 出人意料的新发现】⭐️【would be well advised to = 最好……；明智的做法是……】
But back to the main point of this lecture... the need to feel at home. 不过，让我们回到本次讲座的核心……人们对家的自在感的需求。
What can the hotel industry do about it? 酒店业能为此做些什么呢？
And is the very idea so subjective that it's impossible to do anything about it on a global basis? 而且，这一概念是否太过主观，以至于无法在全球范围内采取统一措施呢？⭐️【subjective = 主观的】⭐️【on a global basis = 在全球范围内】
However, nothing stands still in this world. 然而，这个世界上没有什么会停滞不变。⭐️【stand still = 停滞；保持不变】
One company has come up with the slogan 'Take Your Home With You', and aims to provide clients with luxury serviced apartments.Those in the business travel industry maintain that these serviced apartments dispense with all the unwanted and expensive hotel services that business travellers don't want, while maximising the facilities they do want. 一家公司提出了“带着家去旅行”的口号，旨在为客户提供豪华服务式公寓。商务旅游业人士认为，这些服务式公寓省去了商务旅客不需要的昂贵酒店服务，同时尽可能完善他们真正需要的设施。 ⭐ ️【serviced apartment = 服务式公寓，提供清洁等服务的配套公寓】 ️【maintain = 坚持认为；主张】⭐️【dispense with = 省去；摒弃】
For example, not only sleeping and living accommodation, but also a sleek modern kitchen that allows guests to cook and entertain if they wish, at no additional cost. 例如，除了睡眠和起居空间，还配有简洁时尚的现代厨房，让住客可以根据意愿自行烹饪、招待客人，而无须支付额外费用。⭐️【sleek = 线条简洁流畅的；时尚的】⭐️【entertain = 招待客人】
The attractions of such facilities are obvious and it'll be interesting to see whether the company manages to establish a trend all over the world and make a lasting impact on the luxury accommodation market. 这些设施的吸引力显而易见。值得关注的是，这家公司能否在全球掀起一股潮流，并对豪华住宿市场产生持久影响。⭐️【make a lasting impact on = 对……产生持久影响】
Now, finally I want to consider the psychology underpinning the traditional holiday hotel industry. 最后，我想探讨支撑传统度假酒店业的心理因素。⭐️【underpin = 构成……的基础；支撑】
As a hotelier, how do you go about attracting people to give up the security of their own home and entrust themselves to staying in a completely strange place and sleeping in an unfamiliar bed? 作为酒店经营者，你该如何吸引人们放下自己家中的安全感，放心地住进一个完全陌生的地方，睡在一张不熟悉的床上？⭐️【hotelier = 酒店经营者】⭐️【go about doing = 着手做；采取办法做】⭐️【entrust = 托付；放心交给】
Firstly, hotels exploit people's need to escape the predictability of their everyday lives. 首先，酒店利用了人们想要逃离一成不变日常生活的需求。⭐️【exploit = 利用】⭐️【predictability = 可预见性；此处指生活的一成不变】
For a few days people can pretend they are free of responsibilities and can indulge themselves. 在短短几天里，人们可以假装自己不必承担任何责任，尽情享受。⭐️【indulge yourself = 纵情享受；尽情满足自己】
Secondly, there is something very powerful in our need to be pampered and looked after, it's almost as if we return to being a baby, when everything was done for us and we felt safe and secure. 其次，我们内心有一种强烈的需求，希望受到悉心呵护和照顾。这几乎让我们回到了婴儿时期：一切都有人替我们做好，让我们感到安全、安心。⭐️【pamper = 悉心呵护；娇宠】
And not far removed from this is the pleasure in being spoilt and given little treats-like the miniscule bottles of shampoo and tiny bars of soap, the chocolate on your pillow at night-and we actually forget that we are paying for it all! 与此相近的，是受到宠爱、获得小小惊喜时的愉悦，比如迷你瓶装洗发水、小块香皂，以及晚上放在枕头上的巧克力——我们竟然忘了，这一切其实都是自己付费购买的！⭐️【not far removed from = 与……相差不远；与……相近】⭐️【little treats = 带来愉悦的小礼物／小享受】
