In today's lecture, I'm going to talk about Monosodium Glutamate, or MSG, as it's more commonly known. 今天的讲座中，我将讨论谷氨酸钠（Monosodium Glutamate），也就是大家更熟悉的 MSG。⭐ as it's more commonly known = 正如大家更熟悉的称呼（C1）｜monosodium glutamate (MSG) = 谷氨酸钠
Now, MSG as you probably know, is a flavour enhancer which is used particularly in Chinese and Japanese cooking. 正如大家可能知道的，MSG 是一种增味剂，尤其被用于中国和日本料理中。⭐ flavour enhancer = 增味剂（C1）｜be used particularly in... = 尤其用于……（B2）
Today I am going to explore why it is so popular in these cuisines and, more importantly, how does it enhance the flavour of food? 今天我将探讨为什么它在这些饮食文化中如此流行，更重要的是，它究竟是如何提升食物味道的？⭐ explore = 探究；深入研究（C1）｜cuisine = 菜系；饮食文化（C1）｜enhance = 提升；增强（C1）
The main reason why MSG is more commonly used in Japanese meals is tradition.&nbsp; MSG在日本料理中更常见的主要原因是传统。 ⭐ the main reason why... = ……的主要原因
For many thousands of years the Japanese have incorporated a type of seaweed known as kombu in their cooking, as they discovered it had the ability to make food taste better. 数千年来，日本人在烹饪中一直加入一种叫作昆布（kombu）的海藻，因为他们发现它能够让食物味道更好。⭐ incorporate sth in/into sth = 将某物加入；融入某物（C1）｜a type of...known as... = 一种被称为……的……｜have the ability to do sth = 具有做某事的能力（B2）
But it wasn't until 1908 that the ingredient in kombu which was responsible for the improvement in flavour was actually discovered to be glutamate by scientists working there. 但直到 1908 年，科学家才发现，昆布中负责提升味道的成分实际上是谷氨酸。 ⭐ It wasn't until...that... = 直到……才……（C1，非常重要句型）｜be responsible for = 对……负责；导致……（B2）｜improvement in flavour = 味道提升
From 1908 until 1956, glutamate was produced commercially in Japan by a very slow and expensive means of extraction. 从 1908 年到 1956 年，日本通过一种非常缓慢且昂贵的提取方式商业化生产谷氨酸。⭐ commercially = 商业化地（C1）｜means of extraction = 提取方式（C1）
It was in 1956 that the speed of the process was improved, and industrial production increased dramatically and still continues to increase to this day. 直到 1956 年，这一生产过程的速度才得到提升，工业生产量也大幅增加，并且一直增长到今天。⭐ It was in...that... = 正是在……（强调句型）｜increase dramatically = 大幅增长（C1）｜to this day = 直到今天（C1）
In fact, hundreds of thousands of tonnes of MSG are produced all over the world today. 事实上，如今全世界每年生产数十万吨 MSG。⭐ hundreds of thousands of = 数十万的（B2）｜all over the world = 世界各地
So what exactly is MSG? 那么，MSG 到底是什么呢？⭐ what exactly is...? = ……究竟是什么？
Well, Monosodium Glutamate contains seventy-eight point two per cent glutamate, twelve point two per cent sodium and nine point six per cent water. 谷氨酸钠包含 78.2% 的谷氨酸、12.2% 的钠以及 9.6% 的水。⭐ contain = 包含（B2）｜per cent = 百分比
Glutamate is an amino acid that can be found naturally in all protein-containing foods, erm, so this includes food such as meat and cheese. 谷氨酸是一种氨基酸，它天然存在于所有含蛋白质的食物中，比如肉类和奶酪。⭐ be found naturally in = 天然存在于（B2）｜protein-containing foods = 含蛋白质的食物（C1）
It is widely known that Chinese and Japanese food contains MSG but many people don't seem to be aware that it is also used in foods in other parts of the world. 众所周知，中国和日本食物中含有 MSG，但很多人似乎并不知道，世界其他地区的食品中也会使用 MSG。⭐ It is widely known that... = 众所周知……（C1）｜be aware that... = 意识到……（B2）｜other parts of the world = 世界其他地区
For example it is found in commercially made Italian pizzas, in American fast food and in Britain MSG is used in things like potato crisps. 例如，商业制作的意大利披萨、美国快餐中都能发现 MSG，而在英国，MSG 被用于类似薯片这样的食品中。⭐ commercially made = 商业制作的（C1）｜things like... = 类似……的东西（B2）
So, how exactly does MSG work? 那么，MSG 究竟是如何发挥作用的呢？⭐ how exactly does...work? = ……究竟如何运作？
Well, in the Western world, we commonly talk of four 'tastes', and I'm sure you're all familiar with the concepts of sweet, sour, bitter and salt. 在西方世界，我们通常讨论四种味觉，我相信大家都熟悉甜、酸、苦、咸这些概念。⭐ be familiar with = 熟悉（B2）｜concept = 概念（B2）
Well, in 1908, Kikunae Ikeda identified a fifth 'taste'.&nbsp; 1908年，池田菊苗发现了第五种味觉。 ⭐ identify = 发现；确认（C1）
And it is thought that MSG intensifies this naturally occurring 'taste' in some food. 人们认为，MSG 能够增强某些食物中天然存在的这种味道。⭐ It is thought that... = 人们认为……（C1）｜intensify = 加强；增强（C1）｜naturally occurring = 自然存在的（C1）
It does make perfect evolutionary sense that we should have the ability to detect or taste glutamate because it is the amino acid which is most common in natural foods. 从进化角度来看，我们具备检测或感知谷氨酸的能力是完全合理的，因为谷氨酸是天然食物中最常见的氨基酸。⭐ make perfect sense = 完全合理（C1）｜evolutionary sense = 进化意义（C1）｜have the ability to detect = 具有检测……的能力（C1）｜most common = 最常见的
John Prescott, an associate professor at the University of Chicago, suggests that this fifth taste serves a purpose just as the other tastes do. 芝加哥大学副教授 John Prescott 认为，这第五种味觉和其他味觉一样，也具有某种功能。⭐ associate professor = 副教授｜serve a purpose = 发挥作用；有用途（C1）
He suggests that it signals to us the presence of protein in food, in the same way that sweetness indicates that a food contains energy-giving carbohydrates. 他认为，这种味觉向我们提示食物中蛋白质的存在，就像甜味提示食物中含有能够提供能量的碳水化合物一样。⭐ signal the presence of... = 表明……的存在（C1）｜in the same way that... = 以同样方式……（B2）｜energy-giving = 提供能量的（C1）
Bitterness, he says, alerts us of toxins in the food,&nbsp; 他说，苦味提醒我们食物中可能含有毒素， ⭐ alert sb of/about sth = 提醒某人注意某事（C1）｜toxin = 毒素（C1）
while sourness warns us of spoilage and saltiness signals the presence of minerals. 而酸味警告我们食物可能已经变质，咸味则表明其中含有矿物质。 ⭐ warn sb of sth = 警告某人某事（B2）｜spoilage = 腐坏；变质（C1）｜signal the presence of... = 表明……存在（C1）
