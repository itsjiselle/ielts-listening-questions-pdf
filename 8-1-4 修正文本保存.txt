We've come a very long way from the early exploration of the world by sailing ships when geographers only had pens and paper at their disposal. 相比早期地理学家只能依靠笔和纸、乘坐帆船探索世界的时代，如今我们已经取得了巨大的进步。⭐ come a long way = 取得巨大进步（C1）｜exploration of the world = 世界探索｜at one's disposal = 可供某人使用（C1）
