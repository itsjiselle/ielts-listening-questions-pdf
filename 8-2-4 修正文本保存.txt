I've been doing some research into what people in Britain think of doctors, the ones who work in general practice - 我一直在研究英国民众如何看待医生，尤其是从事全科诊疗的医生——⭐️【do research into = 对……进行研究】⭐️【general practice = 全科医疗；基层诊疗】
the first call for medical care - and comparing this with the situation in a couple of other countries. 也就是人们寻求医疗服务时最先接触的医生——并将英国的情况与另外几个国家作比较。⭐️【the first call for medical care = 寻求医疗服务时的第一站／首要联系对象】⭐️【a couple of = 两三个；几个】
I want to talk about the rationale behind what I decided to do. 我想谈谈自己作出这些研究设计决定背后的依据。⭐️【the rationale behind = ……背后的理论依据／理由】

contacting them by email, to organize things for me at their end. 我通过电子邮件联系他们，请他们在各自所在地帮我安排相关事宜。⭐️【at their end = 在他们那一边；在他们所在地】
I thought I would have trouble recruiting help but in fact everyone was very willing and sometimes their tutors got involved too. 我原以为招募协助者会遇到困难，但事实上每个人都很乐意帮忙，有时他们的导师也参与了进来。⭐️【have trouble doing = 做……有困难】⭐️【recruit help = 招募协助者】⭐️【in fact = 事实上（话语功能：纠正原先预期）】⭐️【get involved = 参与其中】

I decided that people under 18 should be excluded because most of them are students or looking for their first job, 我决定排除18岁以下的人，因为他们大多数仍是学生，或者正在寻找第一份工作。⭐️【be excluded = 被排除在外】

I specifically wanted to do a questionnaire, and interviews with a focus group. 我特别希望采用问卷调查以及焦点小组访谈。⭐️【questionnaire = 问卷调查】⭐️【focus group = 焦点小组】
With the questionnaire, rather than limiting it to one specific point, I wanted to include as much variety as possible. 在问卷方面，我不想把内容局限于某一个具体问题，而是希望尽可能涵盖多样化的内容。⭐️【rather than = 而不是（话语功能：对比选择）】⭐️【limit something to = 把……限制在……】⭐️【include as much variety as possible = 尽可能涵盖多样化内容】
I know questionnaires are a very controlled way to do things but I thought I could do taped interviews later on to counteract the effects of this. 我知道问卷是一种控制程度很高的研究方式，但我认为之后可以进行录音访谈，以抵消这种方式带来的影响。⭐️【a controlled way to do something = 一种高度受控的做事／研究方式】⭐️【taped interview = 录音访谈】⭐️【counteract the effects of = 抵消……的影响】
