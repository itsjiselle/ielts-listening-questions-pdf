Once a business has successfully introduced a new process - managing a branch bank, say, or selling a new product - 一旦一家企业成功引入了一个新的流程——比如管理一家分行，或者销售一种新产品——⭐ introduce a process = 引入流程（C1）｜branch bank = 银行分行

However, surprisingly, getting things right the Second time is not necessarily any simpler than it was the first time. 然而，令人惊讶的是，第二次把事情做好并不一定比第一次更简单。⭐ get things right = 把事情做好；做对（B2）｜not necessarily = 不一定（B2）
Now, there's been a lot of research into how companies can repeat their previous successes, and it certainly hasn't been confined to the United States. 目前，关于企业如何复制过去成功经验的研究很多，而且这些研究当然并不仅限于美国。⭐ research into = 关于……的研究（C1）｜previous success = 过去的成功｜be confined to = 局限于（C1）
It seems that most large industries are trying to repeat their own successes, and manage the knowledge they've acquired - but even so it has been shown that the overwhelming majority of attempts fail. 似乎大多数大型行业都在尝试复制自身成功经验，并管理他们获得的知识——但即便如此，研究显示绝大多数尝试都会失败。⭐ manage knowledge = 管理知识（C1）｜acquire knowledge = 获取知识（C1）｜even so = 即便如此（C1）｜the overwhelming majority of = 压倒性多数的；绝大多数的（C1）｜attempt fail = 尝试失败
A host of studies confirm this, covering a wide range of business settings: branch banks, retail stores, real estate agencies, factories, call centres, to name but a few. 大量研究证实了这一点，涉及各种商业环境：银行分行、零售店、房地产机构、工厂、呼叫中心等等，不胜枚举。⭐ a host of = 大量的（C1）｜cover a wide range of = 涉及广泛的（C1）｜business setting = 商业环境｜to name but a few = 仅举几个例子（C1）

Let's consider one reason for failure - placing too much trust in the people who are running the successful operation, the 'experts' shall we say. 我们来看一个失败原因——过度信任那些负责运营成功项目的人，也就是我们所谓的"专家"。⭐ a reason for failure = 失败原因｜place trust in sb = 信任某人（C1）｜run an operation = 运营某项业务（C1）｜shall we say = 我们姑且称之为……（C1）
Managers who want to apply existing knowledge typically start off by going to an expert - such as the person who designed and is running a successful department store - and picking their brains. 想应用已有知识的管理者通常会先去找专家——比如设计并运营一家成功百货商店的人——向他们请教。⭐ apply existing knowledge = 应用已有知识｜start off by doing sth = 开始做某事（B2）｜pick someone's brain(s) = 向某人请教；获取某人的想法（C1）
Now, this approach can be used if you want to gain a rough understanding of a particular system, or understand smaller, isolated problems. 这种方法可以用于你想大致了解某个系统，或者理解一些较小、独立的问题时。⭐ approach = 方法（C1）｜gain an understanding of = 理解；获得认识（C1）｜rough understanding = 粗略理解｜isolated problem = 独立的问题
The trouble is, even the expert doesn't fully grasp the whole thing because when it comes to complex systems, the individual components of the process are interwoven with one another. 问题在于，即使专家也无法完全理解整个系统，因为对于复杂系统而言，流程中的各个组成部分都是相互交织的。⭐ The trouble is = 问题在于（C1）｜fully grasp = 完全理解（C1）｜when it comes to = 当涉及……时（C1）｜component = 组成部分（C1）｜interwoven with = 与……交织在一起（C1）
The expert never has complete access to the necessary information. 专家永远无法完全获得所有必要的信息。⭐ have access to = 获取；接触（B2）｜necessary information = 必要信息
And the situation's complicated even further by the fact that experts are usually not aware of their own ignorance. 而且情况由于专家通常并不知道自己的无知这一事实而变得更加复杂。⭐ complicate further = 进一步使复杂（C1）｜be aware of = 意识到（B2）｜ignorance = 无知；不了解（C1）
The ignorance can take various forms. For instance, a lot of details of the system are invisible to managers. 这种无知可能以各种形式存在。例如，系统中的很多细节对于管理者来说是不可见的。⭐ take various forms = 以各种形式存在（C1）｜be invisible to sb = 对某人不可见（C1）
Some may be difficult to describe - learned on the job and well known by workers perhaps, but impossible to describe in a way that's helpful. 有些内容可能很难描述——也许是员工在工作中学到的、他们非常熟悉的东西，但却无法用一种有帮助的方式表达出来。⭐ learn on the job = 在工作中学习（B2）｜in a way that... = 以一种……的方式（B2）
And there are some things that people know or do that they're not even aware of. 还有一些事情，是人们知道或会做，但他们甚至没有意识到自己知道或会做。⭐ be aware of = 意识到
Now, let's consider two types of mistake that can occur when a manager actually starts to set up a duplicate system to replicate a successful process. 现在，让我们考虑一下，当管理者真正开始建立一个复制成功流程的系统时，可能出现的两类错误。⭐ occur = 发生（B2）｜set up = 建立（B2）｜duplicate system = 复制系统｜replicate = 复制；再现（C1）
Firstly, perhaps he forgets that he was just trying to copy another process, and starts trying to improve on it. 首先，也许他忘记了自己原本只是想复制另一个流程，反而开始尝试改进它。⭐ improve on/upon = 改进；优于（C1）
Another mistake is trying to use the best parts of various different systems, in the hope of creating the perfect combination. 另一个错误是试图利用不同系统中最好的部分，希望创造出完美组合。⭐ in the hope of doing sth = 希望做某事（C1）｜perfect combination = 完美组合
Unfortunately, attempts like these usually turn out to be misguided and lead to problems. 不幸的是，这类尝试通常最终会被证明是错误的，并导致问题。⭐ turn out to be = 结果证明是（B2）｜misguided = 被误导的；错误的（C1）｜lead to = 导致（B2）
Why? Well, for various reasons. Perhaps there weren't really any advantages after all, because the information wasn't accurate. 为什么？原因有很多。也许最终根本没有真正的优势，因为信息并不准确。⭐ after all = 毕竟；最终（B2）｜accurate = 准确的（B2）

But the underlying problem has more to do with attitudes than the actual difficulty of the task, and there are ways of getting it right. 但根本问题更多在于态度，而不是任务本身的难度，而且确实有方法可以做好。⭐ underlying problem = 根本问题（C1）｜have more to do with = 更多与……有关（C1）｜actual difficulty = 实际难度
