Good morning, everyone. 大家早上好。
I've been invited to talk about my research project into Australian Aboriginal rock paintings. 我受邀来介绍自己关于澳大利亚原住民岩画的研究项目。⭐️【Australian Aboriginal rock paintings = 澳大利亚原住民岩画】
The Australian Aborigines have recorded both real and symbolic images of their time on rock walls for many thousands of years. 数千年来，澳大利亚原住民一直在岩壁上记录自己所处时代的真实与象征性图像。⭐️【symbolic images = 象征性图像】
Throughout the long history of this tradition, new images have appeared and new painting styles have developed. 在这一传统的漫长历史中，新的图像不断出现，新的绘画风格也逐渐形成。
And these characteristics can be used to categorise the different artistic styles. 我们可以利用这些特征，对不同的艺术风格进行分类。⭐️【categorise = 分类；归类】
Among these are what we call the Dynamic, Yam and Modern styles of painting. 其中包括我们所说的“动态风格”“薯蓣风格”和“现代风格”。
One of the most significant characteristics of the different styles is the way that humans are depicted in the paintings. 不同风格最显著的特征之一，是画中描绘人物的方式。⭐️【depict = 描绘；刻画】
The more recent paintings show people in static poses. 较晚期的绘画呈现的是姿态静止的人物。⭐️【static poses = 静止的姿态】
But the first human images to dominate rock art paintings, over 8,000 years ago, were full of movement. 但在八千多年前，最早在岩画中占据主导地位的人物形象却充满了动感。⭐️【dominate = 占据主导地位】
These paintings showed people hunting and cooking food and so they were given the name 'Dynamic' to reflect this energy. 这些绘画展现了人们狩猎和烹饪食物的场景，因此被命名为“动态风格”，以体现其中的活力。
It's quite amazing considering they were painted in such a simple stick-like form. 考虑到这些人物只是用如此简单的线条人形画出来的，这种表现力相当惊人。⭐️【stick-like form = 简笔线条人形；火柴人式形态】
In the Yam period, there was a movement away from stick figures to a more naturalistic shape. 到了薯蓣时期，人物形象从简笔线条人形转向了更加写实的形态。⭐️【a movement away from … to … = 从……向……的转变】⭐️【naturalistic = 写实的；贴近自然形态的】
However, they didn't go as far as the Modern style, which is known as 'x-ray' because it actually makes a feature of the internal skeleton as well as the organs of animals and humans. 不过，这种写实程度还没有达到现代风格的水平。现代风格也被称为“X光风格”，因为它会突出表现动物和人类体内的骨骼及器官。⭐️【make a feature of = 将……作为突出特色】⭐️【internal skeleton = 内部骨骼】
The Yam style of painting got its name from the fact that it featured much curvier figures that actually resemble the vegetable called a yam, which is similar to a sweet potato. 薯蓣绘画风格之所以得名，是因为它描绘的人物轮廓更加圆润，实际上很像一种叫作薯蓣、类似甘薯的蔬菜。⭐️【curvier figures = 轮廓更加圆润的人物形象】⭐️【resemble = 与……相似】
The Modern paintings are interesting because they include paintings at the time of the first contact with European settlers. 现代时期的岩画很有意思，因为其中包括原住民最初接触欧洲移民时创作的画作。
Aborigines managed to convey the idea of the settlers' clothing by simply painting the Europeans without any hands, indicating the habit of standing with their hands in their pockets! 原住民仅仅通过把欧洲人的双手省略不画，就成功表现出了移民服装的特点，暗示了他们站立时把双手插进口袋的习惯！⭐️【convey the idea of = 传达……的概念；表现出……的特点】
Size is another characteristic. 大小是另一个特征。
The more recent images tend to be life size or even larger, but the Dynamic figures are painted in miniature. 较晚期的图像往往与真人一样大，甚至更大，而动态风格的人物则画得很小。⭐️【life size = 与实物或真人一样大的】⭐️【in miniature = 以缩小的形式；微型的】
Aboriginal rock art also records the environmental changes that occurred over thousands of years. 原住民岩画还记录了数千年来发生的环境变化。
For example, we know from the Dynamic paintings that over 8,000 years ago, Aborigines would have rarely eaten fish and sea levels were much lower at this time. 例如，从动态风格的绘画中我们可以推知，八千多年前，原住民很少吃鱼，当时的海平面也低得多。⭐️【would have = 此处表示对过去情况的推测】
In fact, fish didn't start to appear in paintings until the Yam period along with shells and other marine images. 事实上，直到薯蓣时期，鱼才开始与贝壳及其他海洋图像一起出现在岩画中。⭐️【marine images = 海洋生物或事物的图像】
The paintings of the Yam tradition also suggest that, during this time, the Aborigines moved away from animals as their main food source and began including vegetables in their diet, as these feature prominently. 薯蓣传统的绘画还表明，这一时期原住民开始不再以动物为主要食物来源，而是将蔬菜纳入饮食，因为蔬菜在画中十分突出。⭐️【move away from = 逐渐不再采用；改变原有做法】⭐️【feature prominently = 占据显著位置；被突出呈现】
Freshwater creatures didn't appear in the paintings until the Modern period from 4,000 years ago. 直到始于四千年前的现代时期，淡水生物才出现在岩画中。⭐️【freshwater creatures = 淡水生物】
So, these paintings have already taught us a lot. 因此，这些绘画已经让我们了解到很多信息。
But one image that has always intrigued us is known as the 'Rainbow Serpent'. 不过，有一种被称为“彩虹蛇”的图像，一直令我们十分好奇。⭐️【intrigue = 激起……的好奇心或研究兴趣】
The Rainbow Serpent, which is the focus of my most recent project, gets its name from its snake or serpent-like body and it first appeared in the Yam period 4 to 6,000 years ago. 彩虹蛇是我最近研究项目的重点，它因蛇形的身体而得名，最早出现在距今四千至六千年前的薯蓣时期。
Many believe it is a curious mixture of kangaroo, snake and crocodile. 许多人认为，它是袋鼠、蛇和鳄鱼的奇特结合。⭐️【a curious mixture of = ……的奇特混合体】
But we decided to study the Rainbow Serpent paintings to see if we could locate the animal that the very first painters based their image on. 不过，我们决定研究彩虹蛇岩画，看看能否找到最早的画家创作这一形象时所依据的动物原型。⭐️【base … on … = 以……为依据或原型】
The Yam period coincided with the end of the last ice age. 薯蓣时期恰逢上一次冰河时代结束。⭐️【coincide with = 与……同时发生；恰逢】
This brought about tremendous change in the environment, with the sea levels rising and creeping steadily inland. 这给环境带来了巨大变化，海平面不断上升，海水逐渐向内陆推进。⭐️【bring about = 引起；导致】⭐️【creep inland = 缓慢向内陆推进】
This flooded many familiar land features and also caused a great deal of disruption to traditional patterns of life, hunting in particular. 这淹没了许多熟悉的地貌，也严重扰乱了传统生活方式，尤其是狩猎活动。⭐️【land features = 地貌特征】⭐️【disruption to = 对……的扰乱或破坏】
New shores were formed and totally different creatures would have washed up onto the shores. 新的海岸形成了，一些完全不同的生物想必也被海水冲上了岸。⭐️【wash up onto the shores = 被冲上海岸】
We studied 107 paintings of the Rainbow Serpent and found that the one creature that matches it most closely was the Ribboned Pipefish, which is a type of sea horse. 我们研究了一百零七幅彩虹蛇岩画，发现与其形象最为接近的生物是带纹海龙，它是一种海马。⭐️【match … closely = 与……高度吻合】⭐️【Ribboned Pipefish = 带纹海龙】
This sea creature would have been a totally unfamiliar sight in the inland regions where the image is found and may have been the inspiration behind the early paintings. 在发现这些图像的内陆地区，这种海洋生物当时想必是人们完全陌生的景象，也可能正是早期绘画的灵感来源。⭐️【the inspiration behind = ……背后的灵感来源】
So, at the end of the ice age there would have been enormous changes in animal and plant life. 因此，在冰河时代结束时，动植物的种类与分布想必发生了巨大变化。
It's not surprising then, that the Aborigines linked this abundance to the new creatures they witnessed. 那么，原住民将这种繁盛景象与他们所见到的新生物联系起来，也就不足为奇了。⭐️【abundance = 丰富；繁盛】⭐️【link … to … = 将……与……联系起来】
Even today, Aborigines see the Rainbow Serpent as a symbol of creation, which is understandable given the increase in vegetation and the new life forms that featured when the image first appeared. 即使到了今天，原住民仍将彩虹蛇视为创造的象征。考虑到这一图像首次出现时，植被增多、新的生命形态纷纷出现，这种观念是可以理解的。⭐️【a symbol of creation = 创造的象征】⭐️【given = 考虑到；鉴于】
