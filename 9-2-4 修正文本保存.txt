Good morning everyone. 大家早上好。
Now whether you're going to university to study business or some other subject, many of you will eventually end up working for a company of some kind. 无论你们上大学是学习商科还是其他专业，最终你们当中的许多人都会进入某种类型的公司工作。⭐ eventually = 最终；最后（B2）｜end up doing sth = 最终做某事（B2）｜of some kind = 某种类型的（B2）
Now, when you first start working somewhere you will realise that the organisation you've joined has certain characteristics. 当你第一次开始在某个地方工作时，你会意识到，自己加入的组织具有某些特征。⭐ realise that... = 意识到……（B2）｜characteristic = 特征；特点（B2）
And we often refer to these social characteristics as the culture of the organisation-this includes its unwritten ideas, beliefs, values and things like that. 我们通常把这些社会特征称为组织文化，其中包括组织中没有明文写出的观念、信念、价值观以及诸如此类的东西。⭐ refer to A as B = 把 A 称为 B（B2）｜unwritten = 不成文的；没有明文规定的（C1）｜and things like that = 以及诸如此类的东西（口语）
One well known writer has classified company cultures by identifying four major types. 一位知名作家通过划分四种主要类型，对公司文化进行了分类。⭐ classify A by... = 通过……对 A 进行分类（C1）｜identify = 识别；确定（C1）｜major type = 主要类型
The first type is called the Power Culture, and it's usually found in small organisations. 第一种类型被称为权力型文化，通常存在于小型组织中。⭐ be known/called as... = 被称为……｜be found in... = 存在于……（常见词特殊义）
It's the type of culture that needs a central source of power to be effective, and because control is in the hands of just one or two people there aren't many rules or procedures. 这种文化需要一个集中的权力来源才能有效运作，而且由于控制权仅掌握在一两个人手中，因此不会有太多规章制度或程序。⭐ a central source of power = 集中的权力来源（C1）｜be effective = 有效运作｜control is in the hands of sb = 控制权掌握在某人手中（C1）｜procedure = 程序；规程（B2）
Another characteristic is that communication usually takes the form of conversations rather than, say, formal meetings or written memos. 另一个特征是，沟通通常以谈话的形式进行，而不是通过正式会议或书面备忘录等方式。⭐ take the form of... = 采取……的形式（C1）｜rather than = 而不是（B2）｜say = 比如说；例如（口语插入语）｜written memo = 书面备忘录（C1）
Now one of the benefits of this culture is that the organisation has the ability to act quickly, so it responds well to threat, or danger on the one hand, and opportunity on the other. 这种文化的一个好处是，组织能够迅速采取行动，因此一方面能够很好地应对威胁或危险，另一方面也能够把握机会。⭐ have the ability to do sth = 有能力做某事（B2）｜respond well to... = 对……作出良好反应（B2）｜on the one hand...on the other = 一方面……另一方面……（B2）
But on the negative side, this type of organisation doesn't always act effectively,&nbsp; 但从负面来看，这类组织并不总能有效运作， ⭐ on the negative side = 从负面来看（C1）
because it depends too much on one or two people at the top, and when these people make poor decisions there's no-one else who can influence them. 因为它过度依赖最高层的一两个人；而当这些人作出糟糕的决策时，没有其他人能够影响他们。 ⭐ depend too much on = 过度依赖（B2）｜people at the top = 高层管理人员（C1）｜make poor decisions = 作出糟糕的决策｜influence sb = 影响某人（B2）
And the kind of person who does well in this type of business culture is one who is happy to take risks, and for whom job security is a low priority. 在这种企业文化中表现良好的人，通常愿意承担风险，而且并不把工作稳定性放在很重要的位置。⭐ do well in... = 在……中表现良好（B2）｜take risks = 承担风险（B2）｜job security = 工作稳定性；职业保障（C1）｜a low priority = 优先级较低的事情（C1）
The next type is known as Role Culture-that's R-O-L-E, not R-O-double L, by the way, and this type is uaually found in large companies, which have lots of different levels in them. 下一种类型被称为角色型文化——顺便说明一下，是 R-O-L-E，而不是 R-O-double L。这种文化通常存在于拥有许多不同层级的大型公司中。⭐ be known as... = 被称为……（B2）｜by the way = 顺便说一下（B2）｜level = 组织层级（常见词特殊义）
These organisations usually have separate departments that specialise in things like finance, or sales, or maintenance, or whatever. 这些组织通常设有彼此独立的部门，分别负责财务、销售、维护或其他领域。⭐ separate department = 独立部门｜specialise in = 专门从事；专攻（B2）｜or whatever = 或其他类似的事情（口语）
Each one is co-ordinated at the top by a small group of senior managers,&nbsp; 每个部门都由一小群高级管理人员在顶层进行协调， ⭐ be co-ordinated by... = 由……协调（C1）｜senior manager = 高级管理人员
and typically everyone's job is controlled by sets of rules and procedures - for example, there are specific job descriptions, rules for discipline, and so on. 而且通常每个人的工作都受到一整套规则和程序的控制，例如明确的职位说明、纪律规定等等。 ⭐ a set of rules and procedures = 一套规章制度和程序（C1）｜job description = 职位说明（C1）｜rules for discipline = 纪律规定｜and so on = 等等（B2）
What are the benefits of this kind of culture? 这种文化有哪些好处呢？
Well firstly, because it's found in large organisations, its fixed costs or over head as they are known are low in the relation to its outputs or what it produces. 首先，由于这种文化存在于大型组织中，因此其固定成本，也就是所谓的间接成本，相对于产出而言较低。⭐ fixed costs = 固定成本（C1）｜overhead = 间接成本；运营开销（C1）｜in relation to = 相对于；与……有关（C1）｜output = 产出；产量（C1）
In other words it can achieve economies of scale. 换句话说，它可以实现规模经济。⭐ in other words = 换句话说（B2）｜achieve economies of scale = 实现规模经济（C1）
And secondly, it is particularly successful in business markets where technical expertise is important. 其次，在技术专业能力非常重要的商业市场中，这种文化尤其成功。⭐ technical expertise = 技术专业能力；专业知识（C1）｜be particularly successful in... = 在……方面尤其成功
On the other hand, this culture is often very slow to recognise the need forchange, and even slower to react. 另一方面，这种文化通常很难迅速意识到变革的必要性，而作出反应的速度甚至更慢。⭐ on the other hand = 另一方面（B2）｜recognise the need for change = 意识到变革的必要性（C1）｜be slow to do sth = 迟迟不做某事；对做某事反应迟缓（B2）｜react = 作出反应（B2）
What kind of person does this type of culture suit? 这种文化适合哪类人呢？⭐ suit sb = 适合某人（B2）
Well it suits employees who value security, and who don't particularly want to have responsibility. 它适合那些重视稳定性、并且不太希望承担责任的员工。⭐ value security = 重视稳定性（C1）｜take/have responsibility = 承担责任（B2）
Moving on now to Task Cultures-this type is found in organistations that are project-oriented. 接下来是任务型文化，这类文化存在于以项目为导向的组织中。⭐ move on to... = 转向……（B2）｜project-oriented = 以项目为导向的（C1）
You usually find it where the market for the company's product is extremely competitive or where the products themselves have a short life-span. 这种文化通常存在于公司产品市场竞争极其激烈，或者产品本身生命周期较短的环境中。⭐ extremely competitive = 竞争极其激烈的（C1）｜life-span = 生命周期；寿命（C1）
Usually top management delegates the projects, the people and other resources. 通常，高层管理者会分配项目、人员以及其他资源。⭐ top management = 高层管理层（C1）｜delegate = 分派；授权（C1）｜resource = 资源（B2）
And once these have been allocated, little day-to-day control is exercised from the top, because this would seem like 'breaking the rules'. 一旦这些资源被分配完成，高层就很少实施日常控制，因为这样做似乎会被视为"破坏规则"。⭐ be allocated = 被分配（C1）｜day-to-day control = 日常控制（C1）｜exercise control = 实施控制（C1）｜from the top = 来自高层｜break the rules = 违反规则（B2）
Now one of the major benefits of this culture is that it's flexible. 这种文化的一个主要优点是具有灵活性。⭐ flexible = 灵活的（B2）
But it does have some major disadvantages too. 但它确实也存在一些重大缺点。⭐ does have = 的确有（助动词强调）
For instance, it can't produce economies of scale or great depth of expertise. 例如，它无法实现规模经济，也无法形成非常深厚的专业能力。⭐ produce economies of scale = 形成规模经济（C1）｜depth of expertise = 专业知识的深度（C1）
People who like working in groups or teams prefer this type of culture. 喜欢以小组或团队形式工作的人更偏爱这种文化。⭐ work in groups or teams = 以小组或团队形式工作｜prefer = 更喜欢（B2）
And finally, the fourth category is called the Person Culture… 最后，第四种类型被称为个人型文化。⭐ category = 类别（B2）｜Person Culture = 个人型文化
